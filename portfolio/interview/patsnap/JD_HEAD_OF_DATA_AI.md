Engineering Lead – Data, Search & AI 

Position: Engineering Lead – Data, Search & AI
About the RoleWe are looking for an experienced Engineering Lead to own the technical roadmap, strengthen data and search capabilities, and lead a team of engineers developing AI-enabled products and data-driven solutions.This role will work closely with Product, Sales and domain experts to translate complex data and business requirements into practical engineering solutions, from initial concepts and prototypes to production-ready applications.We are looking for a strong technical leader who can build and develop a team while remaining hands-on in coding, debugging and technical problem-solving when needed.
What You'll Own
Technical Strategy and Roadmap: Assess existing systems, identify architectural gaps and define a technical roadmap across data, search and AI. Prioritise investments and determine what to improve, simplify, replace or rebuild.
Data and Search Engineering: Lead work across data ingestion, processing, normalisation, knowledge modelling, indexing and retrieval. Establish standards for data quality, coverage, freshness and search relevance.
Production Excellence: Set engineering standards for system design, testing, deployment and observability. Improve system reliability, performance and cost, including the development and productionisation of AI applications.
Hands-on Technical Leadership: Stay technically involved and step in when needed to write code, debug issues, review architecture and resolve complex engineering problems.
Product and Business Collaboration: Partner with Product, Sales and domain experts to translate customer needs, specialised knowledge and raw data into technical plans, prototypes and practical solutions. Support POCs and customer-facing demonstrations.

Job Requirements
Proven Engineering Management: Experience leading engineers through team building, hiring, development and performance management.
Technical Roadmap Ownership: A track record of defining and executing technical roadmaps for complex, data-intensive products or platforms, from identifying problems through to delivery.
Data and Search Expertise: Substantial experience building or leading production data platforms, pipelines, search or retrieval systems, with a strong understanding of data quality, indexing and relevance.
Strong Engineering Fundamentals: Sound architectural judgement across backend services, databases, distributed systems and APIs, with practical experience operating reliable production systems.
Hands-on Technical Credibility: Strong production engineering experience in backend, data or search systems, with proficiency in relevant languages such as Java, Go, Scala, Python, Rust or C++. Able to review code, write code and debug complex issues when needed.
Product-oriented Leadership: Able to translate ambiguous requirements into actionable engineering plans, make pragmatic trade-offs and communicate clearly across Engineering, Product, Sales and other stakeholders.
1. Technical & Engineering Skills
Data Engineering & Architecture: Designing and scaling production-level data platforms, ingestion pipelines, ETL/ELT processes, data normalization, and quality standards (freshness, coverage, and integrity).

Search & Information Retrieval: Expertise in search engines, vector databases, knowledge modeling, indexing, query processing, ranking, and search relevance.

AI & Machine Learning Productionization: Building, deploying, and operating AI-enabled applications and LLM/RAG pipelines in reliable production environments.

Software & Systems Engineering: Strong backend development, distributed systems, API design, database management, microservices architecture, testing, and system observability.

Hands-on Coding & Debugging: Proficiency in core systems/backend languages (such as Python, Java, Go, Scala, Rust, or C++) with the ability to write code, review architecture, and resolve complex technical bottlenecks.

2. Leadership & Management Skills
Engineering Management: Hiring, mentoring, team building, setting performance standards, and managing team deliverables.

Technical Roadmap & Strategy: Evaluating legacy infrastructure, identifying architectural gaps, prioritizing tech debt, and defining execution roadmaps (deciding what to build, simplify, replace, or scale).

Production Excellence: Establishing engineering best practices around reliability, system performance, scalability, security, and cost optimization.

3. Product & Business Collaboration
Cross-Functional Communication: Translating complex technical concepts for business stakeholders (Product, Sales, domain experts) and vice versa.

Pragmatic Problem Solving: Balancing ideal architecture with business timelines, making practical technical trade-offs under ambiguity.

Rapid Prototyping & Sales Support: Leading Proof of Concepts (POCs) and technical demonstrations for prospective clients or external partners.


Technical Core: Data Engineering (pipelines, ETL/ELT), Search & Information Retrieval (indexing, relevance, vector search), AI/ML Productionization (LLMs, RAG), Backend Engineering (Java, Go, Python, C++, Rust), and Distributed Systems.

Leadership & Strategy: Engineering Management (hiring, team building), Technical Roadmapping (legacy migration, system scaling), and Production Excellence (reliability, monitoring, cost efficiency).

Product & Stakeholder Engagement: Cross-functional collaboration with Product and Sales, technical prototyping/POCs, and pragmatic trade-off decision-making.
