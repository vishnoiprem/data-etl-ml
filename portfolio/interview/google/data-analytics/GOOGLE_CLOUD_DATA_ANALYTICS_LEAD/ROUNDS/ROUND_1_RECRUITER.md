### ❓ Likely Questions & Model Answers

#### Q1: "Walk me through your background and current role."
**Framework:** 90-second narrative — Scope → Impact → Why This Role

**Model Answer:**
> "I'm currently a **Senior Data Engineering Manager** at [Company], leading **12 engineers across 3 squads** (platform, migration, ML ops) delivering **enterprise data platform modernizations on GCP**. My team owns the **migration factory** — reusable patterns, automation, and validation frameworks that have migrated **500+ TB** from on-prem Hadoop/Teradata to BigQuery/Dataproc with **99.9% data fidelity** and **40% cost reduction**.
>
> Before that, I spent **4 years at [SI Partner/Consulting]** as a **Principal Consultant** managing **$3M+ engagements** with clients like [Fortune 500 names], working directly with **Deloitte/Accenture delivery teams** — defining architecture, governing quality, and driving client outcomes.
>
> I'm drawn to **Google Cloud Consulting** because it sits at the intersection I love: **deep technical architecture + client-facing delivery + practice building + people leadership**. I want to scale my impact by building the **team and assets** that help Google's largest customers transform their data estates."

**Key Elements:** Quantified scope, GCP-specific, partner experience, consulting DNA, clear motivation.

---

#### Q2: "What's the largest team you've managed? How did you structure it?"
**Model Answer:**
> "Currently **12 direct reports** across **3 functional squads**: Platform (4), Migration (5), ML Ops (3). Each squad has a **Tech Lead** I mentor. I also manage a **dotted-line relationship** with 8 contractors from [SI Partner] for migration execution.
>
> Structure: **Weekly 1:1s**, **bi-weekly squad retros**, **monthly all-hands**, **quarterly planning + career conversations**. We use **OKRs** tied to practice-level goals (utilization, CSAT, asset reuse)."

---

#### Q3: "Describe a complex cloud data migration you led."
**Framework:** Situation → Architecture → Challenges → Outcome (2 min max)

**Model Answer:**
> "Led a **$2.5M migration** for a **financial services client** moving **120 TB** from **on-prem Hadoop/Vertica** to **BigQuery + Dataproc**.
>
> **Architecture:** Assessed 200+ pipelines → categorized by 6 R's → **Replatformed 60% via Dataproc** (Spark), **Refactored 30% to Dataflow** (streaming), **Retired 10%**.
>
> **Challenges:** Strict **PCI-DSS compliance**, **zero-downtime cutover**, **SI partner coordination** (20-person delivery team).
>
> **Outcome:** Completed in **14 weeks** (vs 24 plan), **99.99% data validation pass**, **$1.2M/yr savings**. Built **reusable migration accelerator** now used across 3 accounts."

---

#### Q4: "What GCP data services have you used in production?"
**Model Answer:** (Rapid-fire, show breadth + depth)
> "**BigQuery** — partitioned/clustered tables, BI Engine, materialized views, slot reservations.
> **Dataflow** — streaming & batch, flex templates, custom transforms, windowing.
> **Dataproc** — Spark/Hive/Flink, autoscaling, init actions, Hive metastore migration.
> **Dataplex** — lakehouse governance, data lineage, quality scans.
> **Looker** — embedded analytics, semantic layer, LookML.
> **Pub/Sub** — event ingestion, dead-letter handling, ordering keys.
> **Vertex AI** — feature store, model registry, pipeline integration with Dataflow.
> **Cloud Composer (Airflow)** — DAG design, sensor patterns, dynamic task mapping."

---

#### Q5: "How do you work with System Integrator (SI) partners?"
**Model Answer:**
> "I treat SIs as **extensions of my team** with clear governance:
> 1. **Joint planning** — shared sprint cycles, unified backlog
> 2. **Architecture ownership** — we own design; they own execution
> 3. **Quality gates** — code review, automated validation, UAT checklists
> 4. **Escalation paths** — weekly steering, monthly exec sync
> 5. **Knowledge transfer** — runbooks, training, asset contribution
>
> Example: On the financial services migration, I **embedded our architects** in their pods, **defined 'Definition of Done'** for each migration wave, and **co-authored the migration playbook**."

---

#### Q6: "Why Google Cloud Consulting? Why now?"
**Model Answer:**
> "Three reasons:
> 1. **Market moment** — Enterprises are accelerating cloud data migrations; Google's **data + AI differentiation** (BigQuery, Vertex AI, Gemini) is unique.
> 2. **Consulting model** — I prefer **outcome-based delivery** over staff augmentation. Google's **'land, expand, transform'** aligns with how I work.
> 3. **Practice building** — The opportunity to **codify patterns into reusable assets** that scale across the region is exactly the leverage I want.
>
> Timing: I've built the migration factory, grown the team, delivered the outcomes — now I want to do it at **Google's scale with Google's technology**."

**Model Answer:**
