# Round 4: Leadership — People & Team Building
## Google Cloud Data & Analytics Lead — Cloud Consulting

---

### 🎯 Round Overview
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Duration** | 45 minutes |
| **Format** | Video, behavioral (STAR) |
| **Interviewer** | Senior Manager / Director from a peer practice |
| **Goal** | Assess how you hire, grow, retain and manage engineers and consultants |
| **Weight** | High — the role is a people-leadership role first |

> Order varies by loop. Leadership and Googleyness are sometimes merged or swapped; prepare both.

---

### 📋 What They're Assessing

| Criteria | Minimum Bar | Strong Signal |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Hiring** | Has hired | Defined rubrics, ran the loop, measured quality of hire |
| **Development** | Mentors | Career ladders, promotions, sponsorship |
| **Performance management** | Has had hard conversations | PIP handled with dignity, clear outcome |
| **Culture** | Positive team | Specific practices (blameless postmortems, eNPS) with metrics |
| **Retention** | Low attrition | Knows why people stay and leave, acts on it |

---

### 🗂️ STAR Story Bank (8 stories, <2 min each)

Replace every `[ ]` with your real facts. Do not use numbers you can't defend.

| # | Category | Prompt to answer | Your story (fill in) |
|---|----------|------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Hire** | Build a team fast without lowering the bar | [Situation / Task / Action / Result / Reflection] |
| 2 | **Develop** | Grow someone into a bigger role | [ ] |
| 3 | **Retain** | Keep a flight-risk top performer | [ ] |
| 4 | **PIP** | Manage out or turn around an underperformer | [ ] |
| 5 | **Conflict** | Resolve disagreement between two senior people | [ ] |
| 6 | **Org change** | Lead a reorg or strategy pivot | [ ] |
| 7 | **Crisis** | Delivery or production incident, team under pressure | [ ] |
| 8 | **Innovation** | Create a reusable asset or new practice | [ ] |

**Format:** Situation (15s) → Task (10s) → Action (60s, "I" not "we") → Result (20s, metric) → Reflection (15s, what you'd do differently).

---

### ❓ Likely Questions

1. Tell me about the hardest hire you made and how you decided.
2. How do you build a career ladder for consultants vs engineers?
3. Describe a time you put someone on a PIP. What happened?
4. How do you keep a distributed team motivated through a long migration?
5. A top engineer wants to be promoted but isn't ready. What do you do?
6. How do you handle an SI partner engineer who underperforms on your engagement?

---

### 🔴 Failure Modes
- Generic "I empower my team" with no example
- No hard conversation or PIP story
- "We" throughout — interviewer can't tell what *you* did
- No metrics (headcount, retention, promotions, MTTR)

### ✅ Pass Signal
Specific, measurable, shows both care and accountability.
