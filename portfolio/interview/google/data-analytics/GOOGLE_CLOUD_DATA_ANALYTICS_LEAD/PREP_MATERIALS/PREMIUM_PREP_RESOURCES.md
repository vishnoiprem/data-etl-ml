### High-Value Prepfully Questions for This Role (From Recent Candidates)

#### Technical Architecture (Cloud Consulting)
```
Q: "Design a migration strategy for a 50TB on-prem Teradata warehouse to BigQuery. 
    The client has 200+ ETL jobs in Informatica, 500+ reports in Tableau, and requires 
    near-zero downtime. Walk through your approach, tool selection, and validation."
    
Q: "A financial services client in Singapore is subject to MAS-TRM. They want to use 
    Vertex AI for fraud detection but are concerned about data residency and model 
    explainability. How do you architect this?"
    
Q: "Your team is delivering a Data Mesh implementation for a conglomerate. One LOB 
    refuses to adopt the self-serve platform, citing skill gaps. How do you handle 
    this technically and politically?"
    
Q: "Design a real-time analytics platform for a SEA e-commerce client: 1M events/sec, 
    sub-second dashboards, cross-border data (SG, ID, TH, VN), GDPR + PDPA compliance."
```

#### Partner & Delivery Management
```
Q: "An SI partner (Accenture) misses two milestones on a $2M migration. 
    Google Sales wants to protect the relationship. The client is escalating. 
    What do you do? Walk through your escalation framework."
    
Q: "How do you structure a partner enablement program for 3 SIs across SEA 
    (Accenture, TCS, local)? What certifications, what cadence, how measure ROI?"
    
Q: "You're co-delivering with a partner. Their architect proposes a design you 
    know will fail at scale. The client trusts the partner. How do you intervene?"
```

#### Leadership & People
```
Q: "How do you ensure strong leadership in technical projects? How would those 
    skills be valuable at Google Cloud Consulting?"
    
Q: "You inherit a team with 40% attrition and low morale. 6 months later, 
    retention is 92% and eNPS +45. What did you do?"
    
Q: "Your practice needs to hire 10 consultants in 6 months. Google's hiring bar 
    is high. How do you balance speed vs quality? What's your hiring framework?"
```

#### Commercial & Strategy
```
Q: "A client wants a fixed-price migration but scope is unclear. Sales says 
    'just commit, we'll figure it out.' Your delivery team says 6 months. 
    How do you structure the deal?"
    
Q: "Build a business case for investing $500K in reusable migration assets 
    (Terraform modules, runbooks, automation). What's the ROI model?"
    
Q: "How do you price a GenAI pilot for a bank: T&M, fixed-fee, or outcome-based? 
    What metrics tie to the outcome bonus?"
```
