# Google Cloud Data & Analytics Lead — Complete Interview Guide
## Cloud Consulting Professional Services

---

### 🎯 Role Summary
**Level:** Senior Manager / Lead (L6-L7 equivalent)  
**Team:** Google Cloud Consulting Professional Services  
**Focus:** Lead technical team delivering enterprise data platform migrations & modernizations on Google Cloud  
**Key Requirements from JD:**
- 7+ years troubleshooting technical issues for partners/customers
- Architecture/design/deploy distributed data processing (Spark, Hadoop, Hive, etc.)
- Partner management (channel partners, SIs, third-party developers)
- Big data technologies + database management systems
- People management (engineers/architects), delivering technical data projects
- **Preferred:** MBA/Masters CS, partner ecosystem experience, AI/cloud tech, large-scale implementations, programming (Java/Python/Go/C++)

---

### 📋 Interview Loop Structure (6 Rounds)

| Round | Duration | Format | Focus | Weight |
|-------|----------|--------|-------|--------|
| **1. Recruiter Screen** | 30 min | Phone | Background, leadership scope, cloud consulting fit, logistics | Gate |
| **2. Technical Architecture Screen** | 45-60 min | Video + Whiteboard | Data architecture, cloud platforms, migration patterns, distributed systems | High |
| **3. Case Study / Client Engagement** | 60 min | Video + Document | Client scenario: migration strategy, delivery planning, stakeholder management | High |
| **4. Leadership & People Management** | 45-60 min | Video | Team building, coaching, conflict, hiring, performance, culture | High |
| **5. Googliness / Cross-Functional** | 45 min | Video | Influence without authority, ambiguity, stakeholder mgmt, "Googleyness" | High |
| **6. Executive / Hiring Committee** | 30-45 min | Video | Strategic vision, business impact, long-term thinking, organizational fit | Deciding |

---

### 🎯 Evaluation Criteria (What They Score)

| Competency | Weight | Evidence Sources |
|------------|--------|------------------|
| **Technical Leadership** | 25% | Rounds 2, 3 — Architecture depth, migration patterns, technology choices |
| **Consulting Delivery** | 20% | Round 3 — Client engagement, scoping, risk mitigation, quality assurance |
| **People Leadership** | 20% | Round 4 — Hiring, developing, retaining, performance management |
| **Strategic Thinking** | 15% | Rounds 3, 6 — Business alignment, portfolio thinking, innovation |
| **Influence & Communication** | 10% | Rounds 3, 5 — Stakeholder management, executive presence |
| **Googleyness** | 10% | Rounds 1, 5, 6 — Bias for action, user focus, comfort with ambiguity |
