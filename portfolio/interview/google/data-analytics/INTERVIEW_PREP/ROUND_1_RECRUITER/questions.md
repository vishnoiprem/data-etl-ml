# Round 1: Recruiter Screen - All Questions
## Google Cloud Data & Analytics Lead, Cloud Consulting (Singapore)
### Duration: 30-45 minutes | Format: Phone/Video

---

## 🎯 Core Questions (Asked in Every Interview)

### 1. Why Google Cloud Consulting? Why Data & Analytics Lead?
**What they're testing:** Motivation, role understanding, alignment with Google's mission

### 2. Walk me through your background - 7 years troubleshooting for customers?
**What they're testing:** Customer-facing experience, technical depth, progression

### 3. Experience managing partners - SIs, channel partners, third-party developers?
**What they're testing:** Partner ecosystem experience (JD requirement)

### 4. Experience with Hadoop, Spark, Hive, SQL - at what scale?
**What they're testing:** Technical credibility at scale (JD requirement)

### 5. People management - how many engineers/architects managed?
**What they're testing:** Leadership scope (JD requirement)

### 6. Do you have current right to work in Singapore? (JD: no visa sponsorship)
**What they're testing:** Eligibility - hard requirement

### 7. Why are you looking for new position? What are you looking for?
**What they're testing:** Retention risk, motivation alignment

## 🚫 Red Flags (Auto-Reject)
- No right to work in Singapore
- <5 years customer-facing experience
- No partner/SI management experience
- No people management experience
- Vague on technical scale (can't quote TB/nodes/concurrent queries)
- "I don't know" without "here's how I'd find out"
