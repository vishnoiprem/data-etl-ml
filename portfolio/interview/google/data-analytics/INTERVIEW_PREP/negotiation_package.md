If the practice hits plan, that equity represents massive alignment. I'd accept 80K base refresh + 10K performance-based refresh tied to practice ARR milestones."

### Sign-on Negotiation
> **You:** "I'm leaving behind unvested equity (~60K) and a 2024 bonus (paid March). A 50K sign-on bridges that gap. Standard for L7 external hires."

### Relocation (If Needed)
> **You:** "Relocation support of 20K covers temporary housing, shipping, and family setup. Standard for senior hires moving to Singapore."

## 🎯 Negotiation Tactics

### 1. **Anchor High, Justify with Data**
- Lead with 450K total, not 400K
- Cite Levels.fyi L7 Singapore data (anonymized)
- Reference AWS/Azure competing offers if real

### 2. **Separate Components, Trade Strategically**
| If they resist... | Offer to flex... | Protect... |
|-------------------|------------------|------------|
| Base salary | Equity refresh, sign-on | Total Year 1 |
| Equity | Base + sign-on | Long-term alignment |
| Sign-on | Base + equity | Immediate cash |
| Bonus % | Base + equity | Total comp |

### 3. **Use "Practice Building" as Differentiator**
> "This isn't a standard delivery lead role. The JD calls for practice strategy, partner GTM, asset creation, and P&L ownership. My track record: $12.3M ARR, 34% margin, 5 partners. That's a different value proposition than managing a delivery team."

### 4. **Get Creative on Non-Cash**
- **Equity refresh:** Annual + performance-based (ARR milestones)
- **Development budget:** 20K/yr for certifications, conferences, team training
- **Travel budget:** Business class for >6hr flights (client-facing)
- **Coaching:** Executive coach budget (10K/yr)
- **Sabbatical:** 4 weeks paid after 4 years (Google standard)

### 5. **Timeline Control**
- "I'd like to finalize this week so I can give notice and start [target date]"
- Don't accept exploding deadlines
- "I need to review the full offer letter including equity grant details before signing"

## 📋 Offer Review Checklist

Before accepting, verify in writing:

| Item | Confirm |
|------|---------|
| **Base Salary** | Annual, currency (SGD), pay frequency |
| **Annual Bonus** | Target %, multiplier range, payout timing, clawback |
| **Equity Grant** | GSU count, grant date, vesting schedule (4-yr monthly?), refresh policy |
| **Sign-on** | Amount, payment date, clawback period (12mo standard) |
| **Relocation** | Amount, covered expenses, tax gross-up |
| **Benefits** | Medical (family?), dental, vision, life, disability, wellness |
| **Leave** | Annual (20-25 days), sick, parental, sabbatical |
| **Remote/Hybrid** | Days in office, flexibility, Singapore hub expectations |
| **Travel** | Policy, class, frequency, client-facing expectations |
| **Professional Dev** | Certification budget, conference budget, coaching |
