## ⚡ Efficiency Principles

1. **Deliberate Practice > Passive Reading** - Every hour: write, speak, draw, code
2. **Spaced Repetition** - Review Day 1 STARs on Day 4, 7, 10, 13
3. **Feedback Loops** - Every practice session: record → review → refine
4. **Simulate Conditions** - Timed, recorded, with peers, dressed for interview
5. **Sleep + Nutrition** - Non-negotiable. Cognitive performance depends on it.
6. **One Thing at a Time** - Multitasking kills retention. Single-thread your prep.

---

## 🚨 Red Flags - Adjust Plan If:
- [ ] Mock scores not improving by Day 10
- [ ] STAR stories feel memorized, not conversational
- [ ] Whiteboard >5 min consistently
- [ ] Technical rapid-fire <70% accuracy
- [ ] Values veto risk in any behavioral answer
- [ ] Burnout signs (irritability, poor sleep, dread)

**Adjustment:** Add rest day, reduce scope, focus only on veto risks.
