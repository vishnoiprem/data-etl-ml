## 📋 Mock Session Structure (Standardized)

### Pre-Mock (10 min)
1. **Brief partner** on persona, questions, rubric
2. **Set up recording** (Zoom/Meet local recording)
3. **Share timer** (visible to both)
4. **You:** Water, notes ready, camera on, dressed appropriately

### During Mock (Per Round)
| Round | Duration | Partner Role | Your Focus |
|-------|----------|--------------|------------|
| 1: Recruiter | 30 min | Recruiter | Concise, quantified, motivated |
| 2: Technical | 60 min | Architect | Depth, tradeoffs, live coding |
| 3: Consulting | 45 min | Practice Lead | Client-facing, negotiation, structure |
| 4: Presentation | 45 min | Panel (2-3) | Storytelling, whiteboard, Q&A |
| 5: Cognitive | 60 min | Cross-func (3) | Values, leadership, Singapore, objections |

### Post-Mock (15 min per round)
1. **Partner scores** using rubric (2 min)
2. **Partner gives feedback:** "Start/Stop/Continue" (5 min)
3. **You self-reflect:** "What went well / What would I change" (3 min)
4. **Record scores** in tracking sheet (2 min)
5. **Save recording** with naming: `Mock_Set1_Round2_Technical_20241115.mp4`

## 📊 Scoring Rubrics (Per Round)

### Round 1: Recruiter (30 min) - Pass/Fail + Score
| Dimension | Weight | 1 - Below | 2 - Meets | 3 - Exceeds | 4 - Exceptional |
|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Communication** | 30% | Rambling, vague | Clear, structured | Concise, compelling | Memorable, inspiring |
| **Technical Baseline** | 25% | Gaps in fundamentals | Solid basics | Deep where relevant | Teaches interviewer |
| **Consulting DNA** | 25% | Technical only | Client-aware | Pilot mindset, partner aware | Strategic consulting view |
| **Motivation/Fit** | 20% | Generic | Specific to Google | Specific to role + Singapore | Compelling narrative |

**Pass:** No 1s, Average ≥2.5, Communication ≥3

---

### Round 2: Technical (60 min) - Score 1-4
| Dimension | Weight | 1 - Below | 2 - Meets | 3 - Exceeds | 4 - Exceptional |
|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **SQL/Beam Fluency** | 25% | Syntax errors, no opt | Works, basic opt | Clean, partitioned, explains plan | Innovative patterns |
| **Architecture** | 25% | Missing components | Complete, standard | Scalable, cost-aware, secure | Novel, anticipates issues |
| **GCP Service Depth** | 20% | Names services | Knows configs | Knows limits, quotas, integrations | Product feedback level |
| **Tradeoff Articulation** | 15% | No tradeoffs | Basic pros/cons | Contextual, customer-specific | Framework-based decisions |
| **Production Reality** | 15% | Theoretical only | Some ops awareness | Monitoring, alerts, runbooks | Battle scars, war stories |

**Pass:** Average ≥3.0, No dimension <2

- [ ] "I don't know" without plan
- [ ] Can't explain technical to non-technical

- [ ] Inflated ownership (verified in ref check)

**Pass:** No Values Veto, Average ≥3.0

## 🎯 Mock-Specific Instructions

### Mock Set 1 (Day 9) - Full Simulation
- **Partners:** 4-5 (1 per round + 1 extra for panel)
- **Conditions:** Full dress rehearsal - same clothes, same setup, same breakfast
- **Goal:** Baseline score, identify top 3 gaps
- **Debrief:** 60 min deep dive, create Day 10 gap closure plan

### Mock Set 2 (Day 11) - Technical Variations
- **Partners:** 2-3 technical architects (rotate)
- **Format:** 5 specialist rounds back-to-back (2A-2E)
- **Goal:** Technical fluency across all domains
- **Focus:** Tradeoff articulation, production reality, "I don't know but..."

### Mock Sets 3 & 4 (Day 12) - Leadership + Presentation
- **Partners:** 1 Practice Lead, 1 Partner Manager, 1 Hiring Manager, 2 Panel
- **Format:** 3 leadership rounds + 2 presentation variations
- **Goal:** Consulting DNA, leadership presence, presentation polish
- **Focus:** Strategic frameworks, partner governance, storytelling

### Mock Sets 5 & 6 (Day 13) - Cognitive + Curveballs
- **Partners:** 1 HR/Values, 1 Director, 1 Singapore Expert, 1 Curveball specialist
- **Format:** 5 cognitive rounds + curveball stress test
- **Goal:** Values evidence, Googleyness, composure under pressure
- **Focus:** STAR authenticity, Singapore cold knowledge, curveball protocol

### Mock Set 10 (Day 14) - FINAL DRESS REHEARSAL
- **Partners:** 5-6 (full panel for each round)
- **Conditions:** **EXACT real interview conditions**
  - Same time of day as real interview
  - Same clothes
  - Same room/setup
  - Same breakfast
  - No notes during mock (only during prep)
  - Full 5-round sequence with 15-min breaks
- **Goal:** Final validation, day-of readiness
- **Outcome:** FINAL SCORECARD → GO/NO-GO for real interview

## 🚨 Mock Quality Gates

### Before Each Mock:
- [ ] Partners briefed (10 min minimum)
- [ ] Recording tested
- [ ] Timer visible
- [ ] Rubrics printed/shared
- [ ] You: hydrated, fed, notes organized

### After Each Mock:
- [ ] Scores recorded in tracking sheet
- [ ] Recording saved with standard naming
- [ ] Feedback documented (Start/Stop/Continue)
- [ ] Self-reflection written (3 min)
- [ ] Action items for next session created

### Weekly Review (Day 7 & Day 14):
- [ ] Trend analysis updated
- [ ] Top 3 improvements identified
- [ ] Top 3 persistent gaps identified
- [ ] Plan adjusted for next week
