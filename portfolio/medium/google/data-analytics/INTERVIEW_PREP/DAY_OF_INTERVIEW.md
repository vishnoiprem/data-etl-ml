## 🎯 PRE-INTERVIEW MINDSET (T-1:00 to T-0:00)

### 3 Affirmations (Say Aloud)
1. *"I have prepared thoroughly. I know my stories. I know my tech. I'm ready."*
2. *"They want me to succeed. This is a conversation, not an interrogation."*
3. *"Business judgment > technical cleverness. Customer outcomes > architectural purity."*

### 3 Questions for Them (Memorized)
1. *"How does the Singapore practice measure success beyond revenue - customer outcomes, partner enablement, consultant development?"*
2. *"What's the biggest challenge the Data & Analytics practice faces in APAC this year, and how would this role help solve it?"*
3. *"How does Google Cloud Consulting balance reusable IP development with custom delivery in the Singapore market?"*

### Emergency Protocols (Memorized)
| Scenario | Response |
|----------|----------|
| **Mind goes blank** | Pause. Breathe 4-7-8. "Let me think for a moment." |
| **Don't know answer** | "I don't know but here's how I'd find out: [1] Check docs [2] Consult team [3] Pilot approach]" |
| **Technical failure** | "I'm having connection issues. Let me [switch to hotspot / call in / reschedule if needed]." |
| **Hostile interviewer** | Stay calm. "I hear your concern. Let me address that directly." |
| **Values veto trap** | Own it. "That was a failure. Here's what I learned and how I applied it systemically." |

## 🎬 ROUND-BY-ROUND EXECUTION GUIDE

### ROUND 1: RECRUITER (30 min) - "The Gateway"
| Minute | Action | Key Message |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| 0-2 | Warm greeting, thank them | "Thanks for the time, excited to be here" |
| 2-5 | **3-min narrative** | Quantified, migration-focused, Singapore-aware |
| 5-10 | **BQ Architecture (2 min)** | Colossus/Dremel/Borg, partitioning, slots, BI Engine |
| 10-15 | **Cost objection (A.C.E.)** | Acknowledge → TCO context → Pilot evidence → Pilot close |
| 15-20 | **Partner experience** | Governance model, scorecards, escalation, co-creation |
| 20-25 | **Why Google Cloud Consulting SG** | Specific: MAS-TRM wave, partner ecosystem, FinServ hub |
| 25-30 | **Your 1 question** | "How does the team measure consultant success beyond utilization?" |

**Success Signals**: Concise (<3 min answers), quantified metrics, consulting vocabulary, Singapore specifics

### ROUND 2: TECHNICAL (60 min) - "The Deep Dive"
| Minute | Action | Key Message |
|--------|--------|-------------|

**Success Signals**: Clean code, explains WHY not just WHAT, admits constraints, "in production we..."

- [ ] **Google Attribute Mapping**: Explicitly name which attribute

## 🛑 VALUES VETO - REAL-TIME SELF-MONITORING

### During Every Answer, Ask Yourself:
| Veto Risk | Self-Check | Correction |
|-----------|------------|------------|
| **Blame** | "Did I say 'they failed'?" | "WE had a gap. I OWNED the fix." |
| **"I don't know" dead-end** | "Did I give a plan?" | "I don't know but here's my approach..." |
| **Jargon to non-tech** | "Would my mom understand?" | Use analogy: "Like a library catalog..." |
| **Arrogance** | "Did I say 'I built it all'?" | "The TEAM delivered. My role was..." |
| **Inflated ownership** | "Can ref check verify this?" | Precise scope: "I LED the migration WORKSTREAM" |

## 🍽️ BREAK PROTOCOLS

### 15-min Break (Between Rounds 1-2, 4-5)
1. Stand, stretch, walk around (2 min)
2. Bathroom, hydrate (3 min)
3. **No phone, no email, no Slack** (critical)
4. Review next round's 3 key messages (5 min)
5. 4-7-8 breathing x3 (2 min)
6. Power pose: Hands on hips, chest open (1 min)

### 30-min Break (After Technical)
1. Light snack: banana, nuts, dark chocolate (5 min)
2. Walk outside if possible (10 min)
3. Review consulting scenarios (10 min)
4. No technical review - trust your prep

### 60-min Lunch Break
1. Real meal: protein + complex carbs (20 min)
2. Walk outside, no screens (20 min)
5. Light review: Presentation flow + Q&A (15 min)

## 🚨 CONTINGENCY PLANS

| Issue | Plan A | Plan B | Plan C |
|-------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Internet dies** | Switch to hotspot (pre-tested) | Call in via phone (recruiter has #) | Request reschedule (last resort) |

- [ ] **No cramming** - trust your prep
- [ ] **Visualize success** - each round going well (5 min)
- [ ] **Prepare clothes, bag, materials** - zero morning decisions
