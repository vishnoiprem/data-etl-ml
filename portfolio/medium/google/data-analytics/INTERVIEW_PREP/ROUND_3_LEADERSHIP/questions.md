## 🎯 STAR Answer Framework (Use for EVERY behavioral question)

```
SITUATION (30 sec): 
- Client/context: [Company, industry, scale]
- Your role: [Lead/Manager/Director]
- Team size: [X engineers/architects]
- Business challenge: [Specific problem]

TASK (15 sec):
- What you needed to achieve
- Constraints: timeline, budget, resources
- Success criteria defined upfront

ACTION (90 sec - MEAT):
- Specific steps YOU took (not "we")
- Technical decisions made
- People/process changes implemented
- Tools/frameworks introduced
- Stakeholder management

RESULT (30 sec - MUST HAVE METRICS):
- Quantitative: % improvement, $ saved, time reduced, revenue impact
- Qualitative: NPS, retention, adoption, team feedback
- Business outcome: contract renewed, expansion, reference
- Your learning: what you'd do differently
```

## 🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **No metrics** in Result → Auto-fail
- **"We" instead of "I"** → Can't assess your contribution
- **Generic answers** → Must be YOUR specific experience
- **Blaming others** → Own the problem, show leadership
- **No learning/reflection** → Shows lack of growth mindset
- **Rambling** → Practice 3-minute limit per story
