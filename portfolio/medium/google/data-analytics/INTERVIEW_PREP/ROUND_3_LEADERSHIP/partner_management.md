### 2. Escalation Model (4 Levels)

| Level | Participants | Cadence | SLA | Triggers |
|-------|-------------|---------|-----|----------|
| **L1: Tactical** | Google PM ↔ SI PM | Daily (during delivery) | 24 hrs | Task blockers, resource gaps, timeline slips <1 week |
| **L2: Operational** | Google Delivery Lead ↔ SI Delivery Lead | Weekly | 48 hrs | Milestone risk, quality issues, scope changes |
| **L3: Strategic** | Google Practice Head ↔ SI Practice Head | Monthly | 1 week | Repeated L2 issues, contract changes, major scope |
| **L4: Executive** | Google VP ↔ SI VP | Quarterly | 2 weeks | Commercial disputes, strategic pivots, termination risk |

**Auto-escalation rules:**
- Scorecard <3.0 for 2 consecutive months → L3
- Customer escalation to Google VP → L4 immediate
- SLA breach >3 times in quarter → L3

## 🎓 Enablement Program

### Certification Pathway
```
TIER 1: ASSOCIATE (Entry)
├── Google Cloud Fundamentals (Coursera)
├── BigQuery Basics (Qwiklabs)
├── Dataflow Essentials (Qwiklabs)
└── Exam: Associate Cloud Engineer

TIER 2: PROFESSIONAL (Delivery-Ready)
├── Data Engineering on Google Cloud (4-day ILT)
├── Migration Workshop (2-day hands-on)
├── Security/Governance Deep-dive (1-day)
└── Exam: Professional Data Engineer

TIER 3: EXPERT (Architecture/Leadership)
├── Advanced Architecture (3-day)
├── Customer-facing Skills (2-day)
├── Presales Methodology (1-day)
└── Exam: Professional Cloud Architect + Case Study Defense
```

### Enablement Calendar (Annual)
| Quarter | Focus | Format | Audience |
|---------|-------|--------|----------|
| **Q1** | New Service Launches (BQ ML, Vertex AI updates) | 2-day virtual summit | All tiers |

- **Certification velocity**: Avg time Associate→Professional < 6 months
- **Lab completion rate**: >80% for assigned tracks
- **Knowledge retention**: Post-training assessment >80%
- **Application rate**: % using learned skills in engagements >60%

## 💰 Commercial Model

### Engagement Types
| Model | When to Use | Google Role | SI Role | Economics |
|-------|-------------|-------------|---------|-----------|
| **Google-Led** | Strategic, complex, new technology | Architecture, delivery, PM | Staff augmentation | Google bills customer, pays SI T&M |
| **Partner-Led** | Standard migrations, scale | Architecture review, quality gates | Full delivery | SI bills customer, Google pays enablement fee |
| **Co-Delivery** | Large programs, skill transfer | Lead architect, key workstreams | Bulk delivery | Joint pricing, shared margin |
| **Advisory** | Assessment, strategy, roadmap | Lead | Subject matter experts | Fixed fee, Google bills |

### Margin Structure
```
STRATEGIC PARTNER (Co-Delivery Example)
Customer Price: $1,000,000
├── Google Architecture/Oversight (20%): $200,000
├── SI Delivery (60%): $600,000
├── Tools/Licenses (10%): $100,000
├── Contingency (10%): $100,000
└── Total Cost: $800,000
    Margin: 20% ($200,000)
    Split: Google 60% / SI 40% of margin
```

### Deal Registration
- **Required for all opportunities >$250K**
- **Protection period**: 180 days (extendable to 365 with activity)
- **Google commitment**: No direct competing bid during protection
- **SI commitment**: Quarterly pipeline update, joint account plan

## 🛡️ Risk Management

### Common Failure Patterns & Prevention
| Pattern | Early Signal | Prevention |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| **Resource bait-and-switch** | Key architects not on project | Certified resource names in SoW, substitution clause |
| **Scope creep without change order** | "Small additions" weekly | Rigorous change control, weekly scope review |
| **Knowledge transfer skipped** | No docs, no shadowing | KT plan in SoW, sign-off required for milestone payment |
| **Quality issues late** | No code reviews, no automation | Mandatory quality gates, automated checks in CI/CD |
| **Communication breakdown** | Missed standups, no escalation | Cadence in contract, auto-escalation triggers |

### Probation & Exit Criteria
```
PROBATION TRIGGERS (Any = 90-day probation):
- Scorecard <2.5 for 2 months
- Customer escalation to Google VP
- Certification regression (lapsed certs)
- Commercial non-compliance (deal reg, pricing)

EXIT CRITERIA (After probation):
- Scorecard <3.0 for 3 consecutive months
- 2+ customer escalations to VP
- Material breach (IP, security, ethics)
- Strategic misalignment (no pipeline investment)

PROCESS: 30-day notice → Transition plan → Knowledge transfer → Formal exit
```

## 🎯 Interview Talking Points

### "How do you manage SI partners?"
1. **Governance**: Monthly scorecards, 4-level escalation, joint account planning
2. **Enablement**: 3-tier certification, hands-on labs, shadowing, co-delivery
3. **Commercial**: Clear engagement models, deal registration, margin transparency
4. **Risk**: Early signals, auto-escalation, probation/exit criteria
5. **Results**: "Reduced delivery defects 40%, grew partner pipeline $2M→$8M, 2 partners achieved Specialization"

→ Use STAR story from star_answers.md (Joint war room, root cause, remediation, contract leverage, prevention framework)
