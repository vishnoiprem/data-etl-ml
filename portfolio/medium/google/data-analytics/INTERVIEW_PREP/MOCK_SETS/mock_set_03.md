### Questions

#### Q1: "You're inheriting a team of 12 consultants. 3 are underperforming, 2 are rockstars leaving for competitors. 90-day plan?"
**Expected Answer Structure:**
```
WEEK 1-2: ASSESS
├── 1:1s with all 12 - understand motivations, blockers, aspirations
├── Review last 4 quarters: utilization, NPS, project outcomes, skill gaps
├── Meet key stakeholders: Sales, Partners, Customer Success
└── Identify: Quick wins, structural issues, retention risks

WEEK 3-4: STABILIZE
├── Rockstars: Retention packages (growth path, visibility, comp review)
├── Underperformers: Clear PIPs with specific, measurable goals (30/60/90)
├── Team: Quick win - fix top 3 process pain points (staffing, tooling, KT)
└── Hiring: Launch reqs for 2 senior + 2 mid-level (diverse slate requirement)

WEEK 5-8: BUILD
├── Skill matrix: Map current vs needed (technical + consulting)
├── Mentorship: Pair rockstars with high-potential mids
├── Practice assets: Launch "Reusable IP" program (1 asset/quarter per consultant)
├── Partner enablement: Certify 2 more partners to Specialization

WEEK 9-12: SCALE
├── Quarterly business review: Revenue, margin, pipeline, NPS, KT completion
├── Promotion cycle: Transparent criteria, calibration
├── Culture: Monthly "Lessons Learned" + quarterly "Innovation Day"
└── Metrics dashboard: Live, shared, accountability

KEY METRICS I TRACK:
├── Revenue / Consultant / Quarter
├── Margin (>35% target)
├── Utilization (75% billable)
├── Pipeline Coverage (3x)
├── Customer NPS (>4.5)
├── KT Completion (100%)
├── Asset Creation (1 reusable asset/consultant/quarter)
└── Retention (>95% at 90 days, >90% at 1 year)
```

#### Q2: "Partner wants to use their proprietary migration tool instead of our Dataflow templates. They claim 2x faster. You know it lacks exactly-once and schema evolution. Handle."
**Expected Answer:**
```
APPROACH: "Evidence over opinion. Let's test."

1. **Acknowledge:** "Speed matters. If their tool delivers 2x with same quality, I'm all for it."
2. **Define Criteria:** Exactly-once, schema evolution, dead letter, monitoring, MAS-TRM compliance
3. **PoC:** Side-by-side on same workload (1TB, schema changes, late data)
   - Our template vs Their tool
   - Measure: Data correctness, latency, ops burden, audit readiness
4. **Decision Framework:**
   - If their tool passes ALL criteria: Adopt, contribute to our library
   - If gaps: "Here's what's missing. Let's co-build or use ours for now."
5. **Contractual:** SoW specifies quality gates, not tools. Tool choice = partner decision, quality = shared accountability.

RELATIONSHIP: "This isn't Google vs Partner. It's 'what delivers the customer outcome with least risk.'"
```

#### Q4: "Partner bills customer directly (Partner-Led model). Customer complains partner invoices don't match SoW milestones. Mediate."
**Expected Answer:**
```
IMMEDIATE:
├── Get both sides' documentation: SoW, invoices, delivery evidence
├── Joint call: Google PM + Partner PM + Customer PM
├── Reconcile: Milestone definition vs delivery evidence vs invoice

ROOT CAUSES (Typical):
├── Milestone definitions ambiguous ("Migration complete" vs "100% data validated")
├── Partner invoicing on effort, not outcomes
├── Customer UAT delayed, partner invoiced anyway

FIX:
├── SoW Amendment: Milestone = automated validation pass + customer sign-off
├── Invoicing: Monthly reconciliation meeting (Google + Partner + Customer)
├── Escrow: 10% holdback until hypercare complete
├── Governance: Monthly partner scorecard includes "Billing Accuracy" metric

RELATIONSHIP: "This is a process failure, not a partner failure. We fix the process together."
```

#### Q2: "Interviewer calibration - two interviewers give different scores on same candidate. One says 'Strong Hire', one says 'No Hire'. Process?"
**Expected Answer:**
```
CALIBRATION MEETING (30 min, all interviewers):
1. Each shares recommendation + SPECIFIC EVIDENCE (2 min each)
   - "Strong Hire: SQL was exceptional - used QUALIFY, partition hints, explained EXPLAIN plan"
   - "No Hire: Couldn't explain Dataflow watermark concept, guessed on windowing"
2. Discuss DISCREPANCIES - focus on EVIDENCE not feelings
   - "What specific question? What specific answer?"
3. Hiring Manager DECIDES - but VALUES VETO = No Hire (any round)
4. Document rationale for future reference

PREVENTION:
├── Quarterly calibration with real candidate packets
├── Structured rubrics (not "gut feel")
├── Bias awareness training annually
├── Question bank rotation (no reuse >6 months)
├── Feedback quality audit: specific, behavioral, actionable
```

#### Q4: "Retention: 2 senior consultants left for competitors in 6 months. Exit interviews: 'no growth path', 'burnout'. Fix?"
**Expected Answer:**
```
ROOT CAUSES:
├── Growth: No clear Staff/Principal path for ICs. Management only path.
├── Burnout: 85% utilization, no bench time, no asset-building time.
├── Recognition: No practice-level recognition for reusable IP.
├── Voice: No mechanism to influence practice strategy.

FIXES IMPLEMENTED:
1. **Dual Career Ladder:** IC track (Consultant → Senior → Staff → Principal) with same comp bands as management
2. **Protected Time:** 20% non-billable for: Asset building, certifications, mentoring, innovation
3. **Asset Royalties:** Consultants get credit + bonus for reusable IP adopted across practice
4. **Practice Council:** Elected representatives (rotating) advise Director on strategy, hiring, tools
5. **Wellbeing:** Quarterly "pulse" survey, mandatory 2-week annual disconnect, burnout risk flag in 1:1s

METRICS: Track quarterly - target: 0 regretted attrition, eNPS > 50
```
