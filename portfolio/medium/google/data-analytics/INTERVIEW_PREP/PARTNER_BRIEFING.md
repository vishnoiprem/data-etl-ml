## 🎯 CURVEBALLS (Mock Set 6 & 10 Only)

Use these to test composure under pressure:
1. **Hostile**: "Your last project failed. Why should we trust you?" (Stay calm, own it, show learning)
2. **Ethical**: "Customer asks you to bypass security for deadline." (Hard no, escalate, protect customer)
3. **"We decided no"**: Interviewer says "We're not hiring." (Ask for feedback, thank them, leave door open)
4. **Impossible technical**: "Design a system for 1M eps with 1ms latency on $100 budget." (Clarify constraints, tradeoffs, "I don't know but...")
5. **Cultural trap**: "Singapore partners are just cheaper labor." (Correct firmly, highlight value, Strategic partners)
