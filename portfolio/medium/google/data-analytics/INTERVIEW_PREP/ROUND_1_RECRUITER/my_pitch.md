> **"I'm a Data & Analytics Lead with 7 years taking enterprise customers from legacy Hadoop and Teradata to modern GCP data platforms. I've personally architected 100TB+ migrations saving 28%+ in costs, managed SI partners like Accenture and Deloitte across JAPAC, built and retained high-performing consultant teams with 92% retention, and created reusable assets - migration runbooks, Dataflow templates, Dataplex policy packs - that scale expertise across the practice.**
>
> **I want to bring this combination of technical depth, partner fluency, and team leadership to Google Cloud Consulting Singapore to help enterprise customers unlock AI-ready data platforms on BigQuery and Vertex AI. My right to work in Singapore is confirmed - no sponsorship needed."**

## 🎯 Pitch Practice Checklist
- [ ] Timed at 60 seconds exactly
- [ ] No "um", "ah", "like" - clean delivery
- [ ] Metrics are YOUR actual numbers
- [ ] Flows naturally, not memorized robot
- [ ] Ends with Singapore eligibility confirmation
- [ ] Can extend to 90s if they want more detail

---

## 🎯 Body Language & Tone
- **Energy:** Confident, not arrogant
- **Pace:** Measured, not rushed
- **Eye contact:** Camera (if video) or natural
- **Hands:** Visible, open gestures
- **Smile:** Genuine at "Google Cloud Consulting Singapore"
