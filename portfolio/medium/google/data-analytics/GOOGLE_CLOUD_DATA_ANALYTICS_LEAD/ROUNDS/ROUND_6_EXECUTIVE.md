# Round 6: Executive — Strategic Partner
## Google Cloud Data & Analytics Lead — Cloud Consulting

---

### 🎯 Round Overview
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Duration** | 30–45 minutes |
| **Format** | Video, conversational |
| **Interviewer** | Director / VP, Professional Services |
| **Goal** | Assess business thinking, portfolio management and long-term vision |
| **Weight** | Final gate — decides level and fit |

---

### 📋 What They're Assessing

| Criteria | Minimum Bar | Strong Signal |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Business alignment** | Links tech to outcomes | Ties platform work to revenue, cost or risk in $ |
| **Portfolio thinking** | Runs multiple engagements | Balances utilization, margin, quality, on-time delivery |
| **Innovation** | Uses new tech | Built a reusable asset others adopted (e.g. GenAI + data) |
| **Long-term vision** | Has a plan | Roadmap: migration factory → managed services → IP |
| **Executive presence** | Clear | Concise, confident, connects tech, business and people |

---

### 📄 One-Pager to Prepare: First 90 Days + 3-Year Vision

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Days 1–30: Listen** | Meet team, partners, top accounts; assess pipeline, delivery health, skills gaps |
| **Days 31–60: Align** | Agree priorities with leadership; fix the top 2 delivery risks; define KPIs |
| **Days 61–90: Act** | Launch 1–2 accelerators; set the hiring plan; first QBR with metrics |
| **Year 1** | Repeatable migration playbook, partner scorecards, team at target size |
| **Year 2** | Managed services offer, reusable IP, GenAI-on-data offerings |
| **Year 3** | Practice at scale, regional leadership, measurable revenue and margin growth |

Put your own targets on this page. Keep it to one page.

---

### ❓ Likely Questions
1. What would you do in your first 90 days?
2. How do you decide which engagements to take or decline?
3. How would you grow this practice 40% in a year?
4. Where does GenAI change the data practice?
5. How do you measure the health of a consulting practice?
6. Why Google Cloud, and why this role?

---

### 🔴 Failure Modes
- Only tactical answers; can't zoom out
- No business language (margin, utilization, pipeline)
- Vision with no sequencing or measures

### ✅ Pass Signal
"You think like a Director/VP."
