# Module 02 — Theory

> **7 lessons · 4 videos · ~3 hours of reading + practice**

The mental models. The frameworks. The taxonomy of question types.
The signal senior+ interviewers are listening for. The calibration
rubric. The "so what" test. The common pitfalls.

Read this module after Module 01. The Fast Track gives you the
intuition; this module gives you the *vocabulary* — the words and
concepts that will let you talk to other candidates, debrief with
your interviewer, and read the rubric on the debrief.

## How to read this module

1. **Read Lessons 01-03 in order.** They build on each other. STAR →
   taxonomy → principles-based mapping.
2. **Lesson 04 is the most important.** If you only read one lesson
   in this module, read this one. It tells you what the interviewer
   is actually listening for.
3. **Lessons 05-07 are reference material.** Skim them, then come
   back when you have a specific question. Lesson 07 in particular
   is useful to reread before any interview.
