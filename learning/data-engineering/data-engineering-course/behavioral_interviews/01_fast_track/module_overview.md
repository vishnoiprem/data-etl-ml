# Module 01 — Fast Track (Start Here)

> **8 lessons · 4 videos · ~3 hours of reading + 1-2 hours of practice**

This module is the on-ramp. In 8 lessons, you get the entire mental
model: what behavioral interviews actually test, the three question
types, why senior engineers get downleveled, and what "senior" sounds
like. End with a worked mock interview and a frame-by-frame analysis.

If you only have one weekend, do this module and Module 03's story
bank. You'll already be ahead of 80% of candidates.

## How to read this module

1. **Lessons 01-06 in one sitting (~90 min).** Don't take notes — let the
   mental model build up. The cross-references will make more sense
   after the second pass.
2. **Lesson 07 — read it as if you were the interviewer.** Cover the
   answers on the right side. Take 30 seconds per question to plan
   *your* answer out loud. Then reveal and compare.
3. **Lesson 08 — read it twice.** Once to absorb the analysis. Once with
   a pen, marking the patterns you see in your *own* recent answers.
4. **End-of-module exercise:** record yourself answering 3 of the
   questions in Lesson 07 from scratch. Compare to the model. Note
   three specific things you'll change next time.
