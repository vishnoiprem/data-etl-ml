# Module 04 — Managing Team Execution

> **6 lessons · 2 videos · ~3 hours**

The operating system of the EM job: planning, 1:1s, cadence,
dependencies, on-call, and hiring. This is where most senior
ICs have the most ground to cover — the "running a team"
muscle is muscle they've never built. The lessons in this
module are designed to be operational, not theoretical. Read
them with a current project in mind.

## How to use this module

1. **Read Lesson 11 first.** Planning is the foundation — the
   1:1s in Lesson 12 are the execution substrate for the plan,
   the cadence in Lesson 13 is the rhythm that keeps the plan
   on track.
2. **Lessons 12 + 13 are paired.** The 1:1 is the private
   cadence; the staff meeting is the public cadence. Read
   both before changing your team's rhythm.
3. **Lessons 14 + 15 are operational muscle. Read with a
   current project in mind.** The dependency mapping in
   Lesson 14 is something you should do for your current
   project, not a hypothetical one.
4. **Lesson 16 is hiring.** This is the lesson most senior
   ICs have the least experience with. Block 2 passes. The
   first pass is the framework; the second is the rubric-
   based interview pattern.
5. **For each lesson, identify the stories in your EM story
   bank that fit.** The "Try it" sections ask you to write a
   90-second answer for a canonical question; do it with your
   own stories.
