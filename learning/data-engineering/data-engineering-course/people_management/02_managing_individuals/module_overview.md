# Module 02 — Managing Individuals

> **4 lessons · 2 videos · ~2.5 hours**

The bread and butter of the EM job: coaching engineers, running
career conversations, setting goals, and delegating without
abdication. This is the "managing down" muscle, and it's where the
bulk of the People Management round's questions live.

The lessons in this module are organized around the **1:1**, which
is the substrate for everything else. Coaching, career, goals, and
delegation all happen in 1:1s. If your 1:1s are good, the rest of
the work is easier than it looks. If your 1:1s are bad, nothing
else compensates.

## How to use this module

1. **Read Lesson 03 first.** The coaching framework is the
   foundation for everything else — career conversations are a
   special case of coaching, goals are a special case of
   expectations, and delegation is a special case of trust.
2. **Lessons 04-06 build on each other.** The career-conversation
   lesson assumes you can run a coaching 1:1. The goals lesson
   assumes you understand level expectations. The delegation
   lesson assumes you can hold someone accountable to a goal.
3. **For each lesson, identify 1-2 stories from your EM story bank
   that fit the questions in that lesson.** The "Try it" sections
   ask you to write a 90-second answer; do it with your own
   stories, not the worked examples.
4. **Pair each lesson with a real action.** Coaching: run a real
   GROW 1:1 this week. Career: have a real career conversation
   with one of your directs. Goals: write real OKRs with your
   team. Delegation: pick one thing to delegate at a higher level
   than you would naturally. The lessons are more useful when
   you're trying them in parallel.
