## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Apollo scenario
**Q:** *"Auto manufacturer wants Apollo on assembly line."*
**A (winner):** *"Safety-rated end-effector, fence + light curtain + estop. Task library: part insertion, picking from bin. On-site for 4 weeks. Eval: cycle-time, failure-recovery time. Pilot 1 line, 1 robot. Compliance: OSHA robot safety."*
**Why it worked:** Right safety-first humanoid thinking.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Apollo scenario
**Q:** *"Customer worried about worker safety."*
**A (loser):** "We have safety features."
**What they should have said:** *"ISO 10218 + ISO/TS 15066 (cobots). Risk assessment per task. Speed-and-separation monitoring. Always present force-torque limits. Documented safety case. Monthly safety audit."*
