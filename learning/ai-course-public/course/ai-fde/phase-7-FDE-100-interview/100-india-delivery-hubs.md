# TCS / Infosys / Wipro (India Delivery Hubs) — International FDE

## Company Snapshot
- **HQ**: Bangalore / Hyderabad / Mumbai
- **Team Size**: ~3,000+ combined (TCS, Infosys, Wipro)
- **Comp**: $40K–$110K base; TC $60K–$180K
- **What they deploy**: Global GenAI delivery back-office + customer FDE
- **Travel**: 10–30%

## Interview Process
1. Recruiter
2. Coding (medium)
3. Cloud / data architecture
4. Loop — coding, customer scenario (often virtual)

---

## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Delivery scenario
**Q:** *"US bank wants offshore FDE team for AI platform build."*
**A (winner):** *"Hub-and-spoke: 1 US-based lead + 3 offshore engineers. Comparison + iterations daily. Use Scrum with US overlap hours. Knowledge transfer via pair-programming. Pilot: 1 service in 8 weeks."*
**Why it worked:** Right offshore delivery structure.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Offshore scenario
**Q:** *"Communication broke down."*
**A (loser):** "Use Slack."
**What they should have said:** *"Overlap hours, daily stand-ups, written design docs, recorded demos, weekly reviews with US stakeholders. Communication infrastructure matters as much as tech."*
