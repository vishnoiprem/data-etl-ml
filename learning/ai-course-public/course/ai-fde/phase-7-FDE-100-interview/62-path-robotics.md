## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Welding scenario
**Q:** *"Customer wants robotic welding for their steel fab shop."*
**A (winner):** *"Path scan-and-weld: structured-light 3D scan + path planner + welder head. Pilot: 1 fixture, 4 weeks. Validate welds to AWS D1.1. Iterative calibration. Eval: cycle time, weld quality (X-ray / UT). Compliance: AWS QC. Train welders on supervision mode."*
**Why it worked:** Right welding-specific (AWS D1.1 quality standards).

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Welding scenario
**Q:** *"Welds are cracking."*
**A (loser):** "Weld parameters."
**What they should have said:** *"Investigate: heat input, joint prep, fit-up, parent material. Process audit (voltage / wire speed / travel speed). Add in-process monitoring (arc sensing). Sample failure X-ray vs spec."*
