- **HQ**: Boston, MA
- **Team Size**: ~600+ AI/Engineering Consultants
- **Comp**: $160K–$220K; TC $250K–$450K
- **What they deploy**: GenAI strategy + build for Fortune 1000
- **Travel**: 80–100%

## Interview Process
1. Recruiter
2. Case-style + coding
3. Loop — case ×2, technical scenario

---

## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### BCG case
**Q:** *"Industrial client wants to deploy GenAI for ops."*
**A (winner):** *"Diagnose: where are biggest costs? Map ops process. Identify top 3 candidate use cases. Quantify value. Recommend: phased pilot (1 use case, 3 months). Build vs buy, change mgmt, governance."*
**Why it worked:** Right BCG structuring.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### BCG case
**Q:** *"Same kind of case, candidate was unstructured."*
**A (loser):** "Use AI everywhere."
**What they should have said:** Use MECE framework, prioritize by value, recommend pilot, address change mgmt.
