## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Health ML scenario
**Q:** *"Health plan wants Verily for population-health analytics."*
**A (winner):** *"Verily's population analytics platform + custom risk model. PHI in BAA-covered Verily Cloud; aggregate analytics in Looker. Compliance: HIPAA + HITRUST. Eval: lift in risk stratification, cost savings. Pilot: 1 health plan, 1 line of business."*
**Why it worked:** Right HIPAA + HITRUST framing.

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
