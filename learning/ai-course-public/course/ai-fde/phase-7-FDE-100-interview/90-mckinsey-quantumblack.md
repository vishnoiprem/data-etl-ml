## Interview Process
1. Recruiter
2. Problem-solving test (PST)
3. Loop — case interview ×2, technical scenario, behavioral

---

## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### McKinsey case
**Q:** *"Bank wants to reduce fraud losses by 30%."*
**A (winner):** *"Framework: diagnostic (where do losses come from?), approach (combine rules + ML), capability (data, MLOps, governance), impact ($ saved). Issue tree: by channel × product × segment. Recommend: 3 parallel initiatives — (1) real-time fraud model, (2) device + behavior signals, (3) dispute-ops automation. Pilot 9 months."*
**Why it worked:** Right McKinsey structuring.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### McKinsey case
**Q:** *"Same as above, candidate gave a vague answer."*
**A (loser):** "Use AI."
**Why it failed:** No structuring, no impact framing.
**What they should have said:** Use MECE issue tree, quantified impact, action plan.

---

### Technical scenario
**Q:** *"How would you ship a GenAI project?"*
**A (loser):** "Use OpenAI."
**What they should have said:** Discovery → use-case selection → feasibility (data, ROI) → architecture (data, model, app) → pilot → scale → change mgmt.
