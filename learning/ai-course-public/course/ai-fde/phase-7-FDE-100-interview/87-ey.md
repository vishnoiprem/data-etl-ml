## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### EY.ai case
**Q:** *"Manufacturer wants AI for predictive maintenance."*
**A (winner):** *"EY.ai discovery framework, choose 1 line, integrate IoT telemetry + service records, build anomaly model, deploy with MLOps. Pilot 3 months. Compliance: SOC2. Eval: % unplanned-downtime reduction, ROI."*
**Why it worked:** Right structured big-4 arc.

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### EY.ai
**Q:** *"Customer wants $1M project for $50K budget."*
**A (loser):** "We'll scope down."
**What they should have said:** *"Reset expectations. Define MVP, defer nice-to-haves. Document trade-offs. Propose scope that meets 80% of value at 30% of cost. Communicate transparently."*
