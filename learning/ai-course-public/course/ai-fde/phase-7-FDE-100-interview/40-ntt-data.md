## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Delivery scenario
**Q:** *"Customer wants GenAI for customer service across 12 markets."*
**A (winner):** *"Hub-and-spoke: 1 global team builds the platform on Azure OpenAI; 12 local teams deploy per market with localization (language, compliance). Multi-region Bedrock + per-market RAG. Eval per market. Rollout: 2 markets in pilot (week 8), expand 2 markets/quarter."*
**Why it worked:** Right delivery pattern (hub-and-spoke), named multi-region.

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Delivery scenario
**Q:** *"Project is behind schedule."*
**A (loser):** "Add more people."
**What they should have said:** *"Diagnose: scope creep? integration issues? skill gaps? Then — descope to MVP + add people to the highest-friction integration. Communicate to customer with new timeline."*
