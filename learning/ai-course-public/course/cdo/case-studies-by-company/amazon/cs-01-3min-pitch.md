# CS-01 (Amazon) — The 3-minute pitch to Andy Jassy (or a Bar Raiser) for a senior data/AI role

> **Mapping:** Original CS-01. Retargeted to Amazon's senior data/AI interview with Andy Jassy or a Bar Raiser.
> **Goal:** 3 minutes. Convince Andy (or the Bar Raiser) in 30 seconds, defend in the next 2:30.

## The brief

You are in the final round for a senior data/AI role at Amazon. The Bar Raiser walks in. "You have 3 minutes. Tell me why you're the right person. Then I have 10 minutes of follow-ups."

**What you have to do:**
- Open with the answer in 30 seconds
- Defend in the next 2:30 with the *Amazon-native* vocabulary (LPs + flywheels)
- Take 2–3 sharp follow-ups

**What you know about the Bar Raiser's taste:**
- They score against the **16 Leadership Principles** explicitly
- They value **Dive Deep** — the data, the details, the math
- They value **Ownership** — never "that's not my job"
- They value **Bias for Action** — speed, but with rigor
- They are allergic to **vendor-speak** and **narrative without numbers**
- They will push back; **Have Backbone, Disagree and Commit** is the test

## Solution sketch (do not copy — internalize and re-speak)

**The 30-second opener (the only thing that matters).**
> "I'm a builder-operator who's led data + AI at $8B businesses. I'd lead Amazon data + AI around the four flywheels — retail, AWS, Prime, and the new AI flywheel — by hiring a small platform team, setting the data contract standard, and shipping one customer-visible AI product in my first 90 days."

Why this works:
- "Builder-operator" — owner, not consultant (LP: Ownership)
- "$8B business" — sized to Amazon's scale
- "Four flywheels" — Amazon vocabulary
- "Hiring a small platform team" — names the org shape in 5 words
- "One customer-visible AI product in 90 days" — measurable, fast, visible (LP: Bias for Action)

**The 2:30 defense (the supporting math).**

| Flywheel | What it is | What to ship in Y1 | What to ship in Y2 | What to ship in Y3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Retail flywheel** | Lower prices → more customers → more sellers → more selection → lower prices | Rufus v2 (purchase intent); Buy with Prime; Retail recommendation ML v2; supply chain ML v2 | Rufus v3 (conversational shopping); Retail recommendation ML v3 (per-segment); supply chain ML v3 | First autonomous retail AI (auto-pricing + auto-inventory) |
| **AWS flywheel** | More AWS customers → more infra utilization → lower unit cost → lower prices | Bedrock FM marketplace (Anthropic, Meta, Mistral, Cohere, Amazon Titan); Q in production; Trainium v2 | Bedrock v2 (multi-agent); Q v2 (enterprise); Trainium v3; Inferentia v3 | AWS as the AI cloud (Bedrock + Q + Trainium + Inferentia) |
| **Prime flywheel** | More Prime members → more 1P + 3P demand → more Prime benefits → more Prime members | Prime member analytics v2; Prime Video ML (recommendation + ads); Buy with Prime for SMB | Prime member ML v3; first $XM Prime AI agent | Prime as the AI platform (the consumer-side AI) |
| **AI flywheel (new)** | More data from retail + AWS + devices → better models → better customer experience → more customers | Bedrock + Q eval harness; shared feature store; Anthropic partnership; first cross-BU model | First cross-segment AI product; first $XM AI revenue from Bedrock | AI flywheel as the 5th engine |

**The 30-second closer (LP-anchored).**
> "I'd hire a 10-person platform team, ship the cross-segment metric model in 6 months, and the first customer-visible AI product in 12. I'd do that while keeping Bedrock within `[CONFIRM: latency / cost per query]` SLOs, and I'd *Dive Deep* into the AI unit economics every week. (Ownership, Bias for Action, Dive Deep, Customer Obsession, Deliver Results — five LPs in 30 seconds.)"

**Q&A — be ready for:**
- "Why Amazon?" — Customer obsession at the largest scale. I've spent my career building for end-users; Amazon is the only major platform that treats customer obsession as an *operating system*, not a slogan.
- "What's your first 90 days?" — Listen (1:1 with every BU data lead, the Rufus team, the Bedrock team, the Q team, the Ads ML team, the supply chain team). Read (Andy's last 3 shareholder letters, the last 2 board memos, the Q roadmap, the Bedrock roadmap). Ship (one customer-visible AI product + the data contract standard).
- "Tell me about a time you Disagreed and Committed." — (Have a real story ready; this is the *backbone* test.)
- "Tell me about a time you had a data quality incident." — (Dive Deep + Ownership; have the war-room story ready.)
- "Tell me about a time you Frowned on a vendor and built in-house." — (Insist on the Highest Standards + Deliver Results.)
- "Are you willing to have your project killed?" — Yes. The 2023 Alexa downsizing was the right call. The data should drive the kill.

**The hidden checklist the Bar Raiser is scoring against:**

| LP | What they're looking for |
|---|---|
| **Customer Obsession** | Did you work backwards from the customer? |
| **Ownership** | Did you say "I" not "we"? |
| **Are Right, A Lot** | Did you defend the recommendation with math? |
| **Dive Deep** | Did you name the details, the SLOs, the unit economics? |
| **Bias for Action** | Did you name a 90-day ship target? |
| **Have Backbone** | Did you Disagree and Commit in a follow-up? |
| **Deliver Results** | Did you name a measurable outcome? |
| **Frugality** | Did you name the cost per AI feature? |
| **Earn Trust** | Did you name a self-critical moment? |
| **Insist on the Highest Standards** | Did you name what "good" looks like? |
| **Hire and Develop the Best** | Did you name a hire-or-fire decision? |
| **Learn and Be Curious** | Did you name a learning from a failure? |

## Watch for when you re-listen

- **Did you open with the answer in 30 seconds?** The opener is the only thing the Bar Raiser remembers.
- **Did you name a flywheel?** "Four flywheels" is the Amazon answer.
- **Did you name an LP?** Every answer should be LP-anchored.
- **Did you say "Dive Deep" or "Ownership"?** These are the cultural answers.
- **Did you end on time?** 3 minutes is 3 minutes.
