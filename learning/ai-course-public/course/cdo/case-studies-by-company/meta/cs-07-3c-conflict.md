# CS-07 (Meta) — The 3-C conflict: Zuckerberg wants superintelligence, the CFO wants cost discipline, Schultz wants data org

> **Mapping:** Original CS-07. Retargeted to Meta's leadership cast.
> **Goal:** 30 minutes. Mediate a 3-C conflict between Mark (CEO), the CFO, and Alex Schultz (new CDO).

## The brief

You are 6 months into the Director of Data role. Three C-level peers have a conflict that you have to resolve (or escalate). The board is watching.

**The conflict:**
- **Mark Zuckerberg (CEO):** "Ship superintelligence. The MSL cadence is the strategy. Llama 5, Llama 6, the path to AGI."
- **CFO:** "Cut the capex growth. $70-72B 2025 is the ceiling. Show me the ROI on every AI dollar."
- **Alex Schultz (CDO):** "Build the data org. The CDO mandate is real. I need headcount, budget, and product pod accountability."

Each C is right. Each C is also wrong.

**Your task:** Mediate. Find a path that:
- Ships visible superintelligence progress (Mark's win)
- Cuts capex growth or proves ROI (CFO's win)
- Builds the CDO mandate (Schultz's win)
- Doesn't break the data team or the pod relationships

## Solution sketch (do not copy — internalize and re-speak)

**Framing (3 min).** "Three legitimate constraints, not three competing priorities."

**Diagnosis (5 min).**
- Mark: superintelligence because the competitive clock is real (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind). Hidden cost: shipping unprofitable AI.
- CFO: capex discipline because the macro + the Reality Labs burn. Hidden cost: cutting the foundation cuts the AI ROI.
- Schultz: data org because the CDO mandate is the only way to scale analytics. Hidden cost: 500+ data people un-coordinated.

**Proposal (10 min).**

| Constraint | Path |
|---|---|
| Mark — superintelligence progress | Ship Llama 5 + 1 cross-product AI in 12 months; shared eval harness with MSL; cap on the cost per Llama version. |
| CFO — capex discipline | Cut $XM in non-AI capex; defer 1-2 non-AI Cloud projects; consolidate 2 internal systems; prove ROI per Llama version. |
| Schultz — data org | Build the cross-product platform team (50 FTE) with 10 platform hires, 5 governance, 1 chief of staff. Pod leads held accountable in scorecards. |

**Trade-offs (7 min).**
- Mark gives up: open-ended cadence → 12-month cap with 2 versions; cost-per-version cap.
- CFO gives up: $70-72B ceiling → $XM with the cuts. Some capex growth remains; ROI must prove it.
- Schultz gives up: 100% pod accountability → scorecard-based accountability; pod leads retain delivery ownership.

**Asks (5 min).**
- Mark: back the 12-month cap; defend the cost-per-version discipline to the board.
- CFO: approve the capex cuts; back the ROI tracking.
- Schultz: own the cross-product platform team; sign off on the pod scorecards.

**Q&A — be ready for:**
- "What if Mark wants Llama 6 too?" — We go with 1 cross-product AI on top of the Llama cadence. Scope is the trade-off.
- "What if the capex cuts slip?" — Phased plan; first $XM in 6 months, second $XM in 12.
- "What if a pod lead refuses the scorecard?" — Escalate to Schultz. The scorecard is non-negotiable.

## Watch for when you re-listen

- **Did you validate each C first?**
- **Did you reframe as joint problem?**
- **Did you name every trade-off?**
- **Did you end on time?**
