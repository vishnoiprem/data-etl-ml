# CS-03 (Meta) — $30M Director-of-Data budget across FB / IG / WhatsApp / Threads

> **Mapping:** Original CS-03. Scaled to Meta's BU economics.
> **Goal:** 30 minutes. Allocate $30M/year for cross-product data + AI. Defend in front of the Family of Apps analytics leads + the CFO.

## The brief

You are the new Director of Data. The CFO has approved $30M/year incremental for cross-product data + AI. The Family of Apps analytics leads want their pod's features. Schultz wants the CDO mandate delivered. The board wants AI.

**State today:**
- 5+ definitions of "engaged user" [CONFIRM]
- Andromeda + GEM in production
- Meta AI in WhatsApp/IG/FB/Messenger
- $70-72B 2025 capex; "notably larger" 2026

**Your task:** Allocate $30M. Justify every $3M. Name the ROI. Name the cuts. Measure the 12-month outcomes.

## Solution sketch (do not copy — internalize and re-speak)

**Diagnosis (5 min).** "The 5+ engaged-user definitions are a tax on every AI move. The Andromeda + GEM shipping is the proof point. The bottleneck is the cross-product data plane."

**Portfolio (10 min):**

| $M | Bet | Owner | ROI (12-month) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Cross-product semantic layer | CDO + FB/IG leads | $XM in saved eng time [CONFIRM] |
| 3 | Cross-product feature store | CDO + Andromeda lead | +X% Andromeda accuracy [CONFIRM] |
| 4 | Meta AI eval harness (shared with MSL) | CDO + MSL | $XM in faster Llama cadence [CONFIRM] |
| 3 | RAG + agent runtime for Meta AI | CDO + WhatsApp lead | $XM in Meta AI engagement [CONFIRM] |
| 4 | AI in support (WhatsApp Business) | CDO + WhatsApp lead | -X% handling time, +NPS [CONFIRM] |
| 3 | FinOps + data contracts | CDO | Enabler for all above |
| 9 | Stop doing: `[CONFIRM: 3-5 named Meta initiatives]` | | |
| 30 | Total | | $XM run-rate, payback [CONFIRM] |

**ROI (5 min).** Per Opinion 3 — 80/20 traditional ML / GenAI. Total run-rate: $XM. Payback: `[CONFIRM: 12-18 months]`. Risk-adjusted at 60%: $XM.

**Cuts (5 min).** The CFO buys discipline. Cut the features that don't move Andromeda / GEM accuracy or Meta AI engagement.

**Measurement (5 min).** Monthly: cross-product metric adoption, Andromeda / GEM accuracy, Meta AI engagement, WhatsApp Business handling time. Quarterly: business case review with Schultz and the CFO.

**Q&A — be ready for:**
- "Why not all-Meta AI?" — Per Opinion 3.
- "Why is foundations $3M?" — Without it, everything else fails.
- "What if the pod leads refuse to share features?" — Per Opinion 1. The cross-product feature store is the CDO mandate; the pod leads co-own it.

## Watch for when you re-listen

- **Did you name every $3M with an owner?** Without ownership, the budget is a wish.
- **Did you name the cuts?** Without cuts, the budget is over-commit.
- **Did you defend the ROI with a baseline?** Without baseline, no business case.
- **Did you end on time?** The CFO buys precision; running over signals you can't prioritize.
