# CDO / Head of Data — Daily 90-min Practice Manual

> **Course:** Data & AI Executive Leadership Masterclass
> **Duration:** 8 weeks · 40 lessons · 8 case studies · 4 mock rounds
> **Daily:** 90 minutes — 20 / 25 / 30 / 15

This is the daily drill. Every session follows the same shape:

- **20 min — Concept learning.** Read the day lesson. Take notes in your own words.
- **25 min — Real business case.** Do the case-study work. Time-boxed to 25 min.
- **30 min — Mock interview practice.** Speak out loud. Record. Listen.
- **15 min — Feedback and revision.** Rewrite the weakest answer.

## The 90-min split

| Block | Time | What you do | What you produce |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept | 20 min | Read the day lesson (one `.md` file). Highlight the 3-5 things you'd say out loud. | A 3-bullet summary in your own words |
| Case | 25 min | Do the case-study work (one `.md` file). Time-boxed. | A 1-page deliverable (the spec'd output) |
| Mock | 30 min | Pick 1-2 questions from the question bank. Answer out loud. Record. Listen. | A 60-90 second answer per question |
| Review | 15 min | Listen to the recording. Score against the rubric. Rewrite the weakest answer. | A rewritten answer in STAR (2 min) |

## The recording protocol

For every mock answer:

1. **Read the question once.** Don't prepare. Just start.
2. **Speak for 60-90 seconds.** Use STAR if behavioral. Use the framework if strategic. Use the math if executive.
3. **Stop. Listen to the recording.**
4. **Score against the rubric.** 0-10 per competency. 60+ = pass, 80+ = strong, 90+ = ready.
5. **Rewrite if below 80.** One rewrite per answer. Re-record if time.

The recording is the single most important artifact. The CDO who doesn't record is the CDO who doesn't know what they sound like.

## The rewrite protocol

For every answer below 80:

1. **Identify the gap.** Was it structure? Numbers? Voice? Pace? Examples?
2. **Pick the single biggest gap.** Don't try to fix everything at once.
3. **Rewrite the answer in STAR (2 min max).**
4. **Rehearse out loud 3 times.**
5. **Re-record.**
6. **Re-score.**

Three rewrites per answer is the cap. After that, the answer is what it is. Move on.

## The single rule

> **The mock is the exam. Everything else is preparation. If you can't score 90+ on the mock, you're not ready for the loop.**

## When to skip a day

- **Don't skip the mock.** The mock is the only artifact that simulates the real interview.
- **Don't skip the rewrite.** The rewrite is where the learning happens.
- **Can skip the concept** if you've already internalized the lesson — go straight to the case or the mock.
- **Can skip the case** if you're at the end of the week and you need more mock time.

## When to extend a week

- If a mock scores below 80, re-mock the same round next week before moving on.
- If a case-study deliverable isn't defensible in 30 minutes, re-do it.
- If a rewrite doesn't move the score, ask a coach for help.

## The feedback loop

Every day:

1. Read the rubric. (5 min)
2. Score the previous day's answers. (5 min)
3. Pick the 1 thing to improve today. (5 min)
4. Do the 90-min block. (90 min)
5. Log the score in your progress tracker. (5 min)

Total: 2 hours/day. 16 hours/week. 128 hours over 8 weeks. That's the conversion.
