# Lesson 3: Leadership Storytelling

> **Week 1 · Day 3 · 90 min**
> **Deliverable:** One STAR story in 2 minutes, recorded and scored.

## 🎯 Architect Level
Staff.

## 🧠 Concept

STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) is the standard structure for behavioral interview questions. Most candidates fail it in one of three ways:

1. **Situation overload** — 90 seconds of background, 10 seconds of action, 20 seconds of result. The interviewer tunes out.
2. **Action is "we"** — no individual ownership. *"We decided to..."* The interviewer can't tell what you did.
3. **Result is vague** — *"it was a great success."* No number, no baseline, no comparison.

The fix is to invert the time budget:

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Situation | 15s | One sentence: who, what, when, the business context |
| Task | 15s | One sentence: your specific responsibility, the constraint |
| Action | 60s | 3-5 distinct moves you personally drove |
| Result | 30s | One number, with baseline, with a forward beat |

That's 120 seconds for a 2-minute answer. The other 20% is buffer for the question, the setup, and the closing.

The Action beat is where most candidates lose the room. The moves must be **specific and yours**:

- ❌ "We aligned stakeholders" — vague
- ✅ "I spent two weeks with the CFO's team mapping every report to a single metric definition, and we got sign-off from all five BU heads at the executive committee"

The Result must be **a number with a baseline**:

- ❌ "It was a big improvement"
- ✅ "Month-end close went from 5 days to 2 hours; we saved $X million a year in analyst time; the board stopped asking which number was right"

## 🛠️ Build It

**Spec:** Pick one story. Speak it in 2 minutes. Record. Score against the rubric.

**Acceptance:**
- 2 minutes (110-130 seconds)
- 0 filler words in the Action beat
- 3-5 distinct moves in the Action, each one specific and yours
- Result has a number, a baseline, and a time period
- Closing forward beat (what the team learned, what it unlocked next)

**Starter:** Use Story 1 (Unified Data Platform transformation) or Story 7 (Cloud and Databricks cost optimization). Both have the numbers and the structure.

**Solution (2min STAR, Story 1):**
> *"S: Three years ago I inherited a fragmented data estate at an eight-billion-dollar retailer. Five business units, three cloud regions, twelve source systems, four ingestion patterns. Month-end close took five days. P1 incidents averaged four a month.*
>
> *T: I was asked to unify the platform and bring month-end close under one day.*
>
> *A: I did four things. First, I spent two weeks with each BU head mapping every report to a single metric definition — we got sign-off at the executive committee. Second, I instituted data contracts with the top five source systems, so we stopped the reconciliation wars. Third, I replatformed in waves — lakehouse on Delta Lake, with the existing BI workloads running in parallel — so we could migrate without a big-bang cutover. Fourth, I built a FinOps practice that cut our cloud and Databricks bill forty percent.*
>
> *R: Month-end close went from five days to two hours. P1 incidents dropped from four a month to one or two. We did it in `[CONFIRM: N months]` for less than the legacy platform cost. The team learned that the boring eighty percent — contracts, governance, observability — is what makes the cool twenty percent possible."*

## 🏛️ Architect Notes

- **Trade-off:** "We" vs "I". Use "I" for the moves you personally drove, "we" for the context. Never use "they."
- **Capacity / cost:** A 2-minute STAR is 280-320 words. The Action beat is 60 seconds, so 150-180 words. That's about 30-35 words per move for 5 moves.
- **When NOT to use:** Don't use STAR for "how would you..." or "explain..." questions. Those are PDCA (Day 2). STAR is for "tell me about a time..."
- **Production checklist:**
  - [ ] Situation is one sentence
  - [ ] Task is one sentence and names you
  - [ ] Action has 3-5 moves, each one specific and yours
  - [ ] Result has a number, a baseline, and a time period
  - [ ] Closing forward beat
  - [ ] Total time under 2:10

## 🌙 Reflect

- Which move in the Action was the hardest to keep specific? That's the one interviewers will probe.
- What's the one number you'd defend with a citation if pushed?
- Write 3 sentences you'd say out loud tomorrow. (Hint: the forward beat.)
