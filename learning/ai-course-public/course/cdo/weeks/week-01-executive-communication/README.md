## Deliverable for the week

- A 60-second and 2-minute version of "Tell me about yourself."
- One STAR story in 2 minutes (your strongest one — typically Story 1 or Story 7).
- A 3-minute CEO-level pitch of your data platform transformation.

## The single principle

> **Business Problem → Strategic Decision → Investment & Trade-offs → Execution → Measurable Outcome.**

Every answer in this week follows that shape. No exception.

## The pattern to break

The most common pattern that knocks CDO candidates out is the **technology-first** introduction: *"I'm a data engineer with 15+ years of experience in Spark, Kafka, Databricks..."* That's an IC pitch. The executive pitch is:

> *"I use data and AI to improve business performance. I lead teams that turn data into measurable outcomes — revenue, cost, risk. Here's one example..."*
