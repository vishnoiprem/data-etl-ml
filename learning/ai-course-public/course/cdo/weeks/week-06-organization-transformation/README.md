# Week 6 — Organization, People and Transformation Leadership

> **Week 6 · 5 lessons · 1 case study**
> **Objective:** Be able to design, staff, lead, and transform a 50-person data organization through 3 years of change.

## The single principle

> **The CDO is the designer of the org, the steward of the culture, and the architect of the transformation. The technology is the easy part.**

The first 5 weeks covered the *what* (strategy), the *how much* (ROI), the *how* (architecture), and the *license to operate* (governance). This week covers the *who* — the org, the people, the leadership, the change.

The CDO interview will probe leadership at three levels:

1. **Org design** — what teams, what decision rights, what headcount, what shape.
2. **People leadership** — how to hire, grow, retain, and let go of talent.
3. **Transformation** — how to take a fragmented org through a 3-year change without losing the business.

If you can't lead people through change, you don't have the CDO seat. You have the Head of Data seat, maybe.

**Case study (90 min, weekend):** A 50-person data org is fragmented across 5 BUs, with 3 different tech stacks and a -40% attrition problem. Transform it in 30 minutes.

## The deliverable for the week

A **3-year People and Organization Plan** — a 5-page document with:

1. The target org shape (Y1 / Y2 / Y3)
2. The headcount plan (Y1 / Y2 / Y3) with rationale
3. The hiring plan (roles, profile, timeline)
4. The retention plan (comp, growth, mission)
5. The culture traits (8 traits, each with a measurable behavior)
6. The transformation phases (3 phases, milestones, risks)
7. The vendor strategy and FinOps framework
