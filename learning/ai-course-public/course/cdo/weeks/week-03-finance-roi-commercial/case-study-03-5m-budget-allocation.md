# Case Study 3: The $5M Budget Allocation

> **Week 3 · Case Study · 90 min**
> **Deliverable:** A defensible $5M Data & AI budget allocation with ROI, risks, and cuts.

## The brief

You are the new CDO at a Series-D retailer with $2B revenue, 5 BUs. The CEO offers you a $5M annual budget for Data & AI. The CFO wants the ROI in 12 months. The board wants the AI mandate delivered. The 5 BU heads each have a $2M wish list.

**The brief:**
- Allocate the $5M
- Justify each $1M
- Name the ROI per allocation
- Name the cuts and why
- Measure returns over 12 months

You have 30 minutes to present to the CEO, CFO, and 5 BU heads. They will challenge every $1M.

## The structure (30 minutes total)

| Section | Time | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| The diagnosis | 5 min | The state of the company and the unit economics |
| The portfolio | 10 min | The 7 funded bets, line by line |
| The ROI | 5 min | The total return, the payback period |
| The cuts | 5 min | The 13 items cut, with reason |
| The measurement | 5 min | The 12-month outcome metrics |

## The scoring rubric (out of 100)

| Competency | Weight | What "great" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial and financial understanding | 40% | The CFO nods at the P&L logic |
| Strategic and business thinking | 25% | The portfolio matches the business model |
| Leadership and executive influence | 15% | The BU heads feel heard; the CFO sees the discipline |
| Technical judgment and architecture | 10% | The architecture choices support the ROI claims |
| Communication and structure | 10% | 30 minutes, no overrun, no jargon |

A score below 90 means Week 3 is not done. Re-record.

## Acceptance criteria

- 30 minutes, no overrun
- 0 acronyms in the opening 2 minutes
- All $5M allocated, every $1M justified
- 7 funded bets, each with named ROI
- 13 cuts, each with a reason
- 12-month measurement plan named
- "What if it doesn't work?" answered

## Solution sketch (do not copy — internalize and re-speak)

**Diagnosis (5 min):**
- $2B revenue, ~25% blended gross margin, ~6% EBITDA, 5 BUs, 3 warehouses, 4 definitions of "active customer"
- Unit economics: gross margin is the lever; inventory turn is the lever; NRR (B2B) is the lever
- The diagnosis is not "we need AI." The diagnosis is "we need the foundations that make AI investable."

**Portfolio (10 min):**

| $M | Bet | Owner | ROI (12-month) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | Demand forecasting (fresh) | VP Merch + CDO | $1.5-3M margin via -20% stockouts |
| 0.5 | Ops forecasting (workforce) | COO + CDO | $0.5-1M opex |
| 1.0 | Customer 360 + NBO | CMO + CDO | $2-5M revenue via +2-5% retention |
| 0.8 | Dynamic pricing (markdown) | VP Merch + CDO | $1-3M margin |
| 0.8 | Document intelligence (RAG) | CFO + CDO | $0.5-1M ops via -30% handling |
| 0.7 | Churn / retention (B2B) | CRO + CDO | $1-3M revenue |
| 0.7 | Data foundations (contracts, semantic layer, FinOps) | CDO | Enabler for all above |
| 5.0 | Total | | $6.5-16M run-rate, payback 9-15 months |

**ROI (5 min):**
- Total run-rate return: $6.5-16M (1.3-3.2× the budget)
- Payback: 9-15 months
- Working capital release: $1-3M one-time
- Risk-adjusted: assume 60% realization → $4-10M run-rate

**Cuts (5 min):**
- AI customer support — risk > return at current maturity
- Autonomous store replenishment — defer to Y2/Y3
- Real-time personalization infra — defer until customer 360 ships
- Voice / speech analytics — no data, no owner
- Marketing mix modeling — depends on attribution, defer
- 8 more (each with a one-line reason)

**Measurement (5 min):**
- Monthly: stockouts, markdowns, NRR, support cost, ops cost
- Quarterly: business case review with CFO
- Annual: portfolio review with CEO + board

**Q&A — be ready for:**
- "Why not more AI?" (because AI without foundations has 0% realization)
- "Why is foundations $700K?" (because without them, everything else fails)
- "What if the BU head refuses to co-own?" (escalate to CEO; the co-ownership is non-negotiable)
- "What if a bet doesn't deliver?" (kill it in 90 days, redirect to the next bet)

## What to watch for when you re-listen

- **Did every $1M have an owner?** If not, the CFO concludes the budget is a wish list.
- **Did you name the cuts?** A budget without cuts is over-commit.
- **Did you defend the ROI with a baseline?** If you can't say "today is $X, target is $Y," you have no business case.
- **Did you end on time?** If you run over, the CFO concludes you can't prioritize.

## Re-record until you score ≥ 90

A CDO who can't defend a $5M allocation in 30 minutes is not a CDO. The 30-minute defense is the most-asked executive interview question.
