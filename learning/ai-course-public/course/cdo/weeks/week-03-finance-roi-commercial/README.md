# Week 3 — Business Finance, ROI and Commercial Leadership

> **Week 3 · 5 lessons · 1 case study**
> **Objective:** Understand how businesses make money, and how data and AI investments create financial value.

## The single principle

> **Every data and AI investment must be defended in the language of the P&L, not the language of the platform.**

The CFO does not buy lakehouses. The CFO buys margin, growth, and risk reduction. If you cannot translate "we built a feature store" into "we will add 2 points of gross margin on the $X category," you do not have a business case — you have a hobby.

This week makes you fluent in three translations:

1. **Business finance → FY data model.** P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, working capital, unit economics.
2. **Data investment → FY ROI.** Cost-out, revenue-up, risk-down. Each quantified with a baseline and a counterfactual.
3. **AI portfolio → FY strategy.** Which bets fund which bets. The cuts are where the strategy lives.

By Friday, you will be able to take a $5M annual budget and defend the allocation, line by line, in front of the CEO and CFO. If you can't, you don't have a strategy — you have a wish list.

## The deliverable for the week

A **Data & AI investment business case** — a 5-page document with:

1. Executive summary (1 page)
2. The investment ask ($5M, by category)
3. The expected ROI per category
4. The 12-month outcome metrics
5. The "stop doing" list
6. The defensible answer to "what if it doesn't work?"
