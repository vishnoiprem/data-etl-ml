# Lesson 40: Full mock and review

> **Week 8 · Day 40 · 90 min**
> **Deliverable:** The 4 mock rounds, scored, with the 3 weakest answers rewritten.

## 🎯 Architect Level
Staff.

## 🧠 Concept

The full mock is the final exam. The 4 rounds simulate the loop:

1. **Mock 1: Hiring manager** (60 min) — behavioral + strategic + role-specific
2. **Mock 2: CTO / architect** (60 min) — architecture + trade-offs + judgment
3. **Mock 3: CEO / CFO** (45 min) — business + ROI + vision
4. **Mock 4: CDO / board** (60 min) — end-to-end case + governance + leadership

The single biggest mistake is to **skip the mock**. The candidate who has never done a full mock is the candidate who finds the gap in the real loop. The mock is where you find the gap.

The second biggest mistake is to **not score the mock**. Without a score, the mock is rehearsal. With a score, the mock is data. Use the rubric from `RUBRIC.md` and score each round.

The third biggest mistake is to **not rewrite the weakest answers**. The 3 weakest answers in the mocks are the 3 answers that will lose the offer. Rewrite them this week.

## 🛠️ Build It

**Spec:** Run the 4 mocks. Score each. Rewrite the 3 weakest answers.

**Acceptance:**
- 4 mocks completed (or scheduled)
- Each mock scored using the rubric
- 3 weakest answers identified
- 3 rewritten answers delivered
- 1 paragraph on the trade-off (mocking vs. confidence)

**Starter:** Use the 4 mock files in `mocks/`. Schedule 4 sessions this week.

**Solution (mock plan, abridged):**

| Mock | Date | Partner | Focus | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hiring manager | Day 40 morning | Peer coach | Behavioral + strategic | /100 |
| CTO / architect | Day 40 afternoon | Peer coach | Architecture + trade-offs | /100 |
| CEO / CFO | Day 41 morning | Peer coach | Business + ROI | /100 |
| CDO / board | Day 41 afternoon | Peer coach | End-to-end case | /100 |

For each mock:
- 5 min — opener
- 45-55 min — questions
- 5 min — closer (your "why this role" closing)
- 15 min — feedback + score

**Rewrite protocol:**
1. Identify the 3 weakest answers (one per mock)
2. For each, write the new answer in STAR (2 min)
3. Rehearse out loud
4. Record; listen; iterate
5. Re-mock the same question in the next session

**Trade-off (mocking vs. confidence):** The mock is a confidence builder, not a confidence killer. The candidate who scores 70 in the first mock and 90 in the fourth is the candidate who lands the offer. The candidate who refuses to mock is the candidate who lands the 70 in the real loop.

## 🏛️ Architect Notes

- **Trade-off:** Mock with a friend vs. mock with a coach. Friend is free, but a coach gives rubric-faithful feedback. Default: friend for the first 2 mocks, coach for the last 2.
- **Capacity / cost:** 4 mocks × 90 min = 6 hours. Add 2-3 hours per mock for prep + feedback + rewrite. Total: 14-18 hours this week.
- **When NOT to use:** Don't skip the mock because "I'm not ready." You'll never feel ready. Mock now, rewrite, mock again.
- **Production checklist:**
  - [ ] 4 mocks completed
  - [ ] Each mock scored
  - [ ] 3 weakest answers identified
  - [ ] 3 rewritten answers
  - [ ] Trade-off paragraph
