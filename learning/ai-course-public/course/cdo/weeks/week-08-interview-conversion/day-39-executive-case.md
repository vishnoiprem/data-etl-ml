# Lesson 39: Executive case

> **Week 8 · Day 39 · 90 min**
> **Deliverable:** The business case + ROI defense, rehearsed end-to-end in 30 minutes.

## 🎯 Architect Level
Staff.

## 🧠 Concept

The executive case is the CEO / CFO round. The interviewer is testing: "can this person own the business outcome, not just the technology?" The bar is ROI thinking, financial discipline, and strategic framing.

The 5 sections of the 30-minute defense (per Week 3's case study):

1. **Diagnosis** (5 min) — the state of the business and the unit economics
2. **Portfolio** (10 min) — the 7 funded bets, line by line
3. **ROI** (5 min) — total return, payback period
4. **Cuts** (5 min) — the 13 items cut, with reason
5. **Measurement** (5 min) — the 12-month outcome metrics

The single biggest mistake is to **not have a number for every $1M**. The CFO buys precision. The CDO who says "we expect significant return" loses the room.

The second biggest mistake is to **not have a baseline**. "We will save $5M" is half a story. "We will save $5M vs. doing nothing, vs. status quo, vs. a cheaper alternative" is the full story.

The third biggest mistake is to **not name the cuts**. A portfolio without cuts is a wish. The CFO buys the cuts.

## 🛠️ Build It

**Spec:** Rehearse the executive case end-to-end. Time yourself. Find the weak sections.

**Acceptance:**
- 30 minutes, no overrun
- All $5M allocated, every $1M justified
- 7 funded bets, each with named ROI
- 13 cuts, each with a reason
- 12-month measurement plan named
- "What if it doesn't work?" answered
- 1 paragraph on the trade-off (precision vs. humility)

**Starter:** Use Case Study 3 from Week 3. Time yourself.

**Solution (rehearsal plan, abridged):**

| Section | Target | Actual | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | 5 min | 4 min | Add the unit economics |
| Portfolio | 10 min | 12 min | Cut 1 bet's detail |
| ROI | 5 min | 4 min | Add the risk-adjusted number |
| Cuts | 5 min | 6 min | Trim 1 min |
| Measurement | 5 min | 4 min | Add 1 metric |

Re-record. Find the 2 weakest sections. Rewrite. Re-rehearse.

**Trade-off (precision vs. humility):** Use ranges, not point estimates. "$6.5-16M run-rate, 9-15 month payback" is credible. "$10M in year 1" is a trap.

## 🏛️ Architect Notes

- **Trade-off:** ROI precision vs. strategic value. Some investments don't have a clean ROI. The CFO who demands ROI on everything under-invests in foundations. The CDO has to defend the "no clean ROI" investments.
- **Capacity / cost:** A 30-minute case defense takes 6-8 hours to build + rehearse. Spread over 2-3 sessions.
- **When NOT to use:** Don't quote numbers you can't defend. Use ranges and [CONFIRM] markers.
- **Production checklist:**
  - [ ] 30 minutes, no overrun
  - [ ] All $5M allocated
  - [ ] 7 bets, named ROI
  - [ ] 13 cuts, named reason
  - [ ] 12-month plan
  - [ ] "What if it doesn't work?" answered
  - [ ] Trade-off paragraph
