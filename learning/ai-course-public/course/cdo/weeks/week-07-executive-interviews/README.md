# Week 7 — CEO, CTO, CFO and Board-Level Interviews

> **Week 7 · 5 lessons · 1 case study**
> **Objective:** Pass the executive interview loop — CEO, CTO, CFO, board, and the hard questions.

## The single principle

> **Executive interviews are not about the right answer. They are about the right posture — strategic, decisive, and accountable.**

The IC interview tests "can you build it?" The Director interview tests "can you lead it?" The executive interview tests "can you own the outcome?" That is a different muscle.

This week prepares you for the four most-different interview rooms in the loop:

1. **The CEO** — strategy, business, leadership, judgment. No jargon. No depth. Just clarity.
2. **The CTO** — architecture, technology choices, build-vs-buy, technical leadership. Depth where it matters.
3. **The CFO** — ROI, budget, financial discipline, risk. Numbers, not narratives.
4. **The board** — vision, governance, risk posture, business outcomes. Brevity, candor, and ownership.

If you can hold your own in all four, you can hold the CDO seat.
