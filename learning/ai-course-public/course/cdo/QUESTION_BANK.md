# Question Bank — 50 Master Questions

> **Use this every Friday.** Pick 5 random questions (one from each group), speak your answer out loud, record it, score against [`RUBRIC.md`](./RUBRIC.md).
> **For each question, the file gives:** the rubric competencies it tests, a 60-second answer skeleton, a 2-minute STAR frame, common follow-ups, and the resume story it should reference.

The 50 questions are grouped by the 5 rubric axes. Comms (10%) and Ownership (10%) are tested in every answer — they don't have a dedicated group.

---

## Group 1: Strategy and Business (Q1-10) — 25% of the rubric

### Q1. What is your data strategy?

- **Tests:** Strategic thinking (25), Commercial (15)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Start from the business decisions you need to improve. Then the capabilities. Then the owners. Then the ROI."*
- **2min STAR:**
  - S: The Makro / Xendit / SCB / DBS business problem
  - T: The strategy I built
  - A: How I aligned execs, chose investment, sequenced delivery
  - R: Measurable business outcomes (with numbers)
- **Follow-ups:** "How did you measure ROI?" "Who owned delivery?" "What did you stop doing?"
- **Story:** [STORY 1 — Unified Data Platform transformation](./STORY_BANK.md#story-1-unified-data-platform-transformation)

### Q2. How would you build an enterprise AI strategy?

- **Tests:** Strategic thinking (25), Technical judgment (20)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Identify 3-5 high-value use cases, prioritize by value-vs-readiness, sequence by data dependencies, govern from day one."*
- **2min STAR:**
  - S: Use-case inventory (churn, pricing, segmentation, document AI, support automation)
  - T: Build vs buy vs partner, governance model, sequencing
  - A: AI portfolio board, use-case lifecycle, kill criteria
  - R: 2-3 in production, 1-2 with measurable revenue impact
- **Follow-ups:** "How do you measure success?" "What about responsible AI?" "What did you cut?"
- **Story:** [STORY 4 — Dynamic pricing and recommendation](./STORY_BANK.md#story-4-dynamic-pricing-and-recommendation)

### Q3. What would you do in your first 90 days?

- **Tests:** Strategic (25), Leadership (20), Ownership (10)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Days 1-30 discover and align. Days 31-60 prioritize and prove value. Days 61-90 operationalize and scale."*
- **2min STAR:** See [Week 2, Day 10](./weeks/week-02-enterprise-data-ai-strategy/day-10-first-90-days.md)
- **Follow-ups:** "How do you handle resistance?" "What's your first win?" "How do you assess the team?"
- **Story:** [STORY 1 — first-90-days variant](./STORY_BANK.md#story-1-unified-data-platform-transformation)

### Q4. How do you prioritize AI investments?

- **Tests:** Strategic (25), Commercial (15)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Value × readiness / cost. Value = business impact. Readiness = data + people + process. Cost = build + run + opportunity."*
- **2min STAR:** See [Week 3, Day 15](./weeks/week-03-finance-roi-commercial/day-15-investment-prioritization.md)
- **Follow-ups:** "How do you handle sunk cost?" "What about moonshots?"
- **Story:** [STORY 4 — Dynamic pricing and recommendation](./STORY_BANK.md#story-4-dynamic-pricing-and-recommendation)

### Q5. How do you measure the value of a data platform?

- **Tests:** Strategic (25), Commercial (15), Ownership (10)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Three layers: adoption (who is using it), operational (reliability, latency, cost), business (revenue, cost savings, risk reduction)."*
- **2min STAR:**
  - S: Platform spend without clear ROI
  - T: 3-layer value measurement framework
  - A: Adoption dashboard, operational scorecard, business case tracking
  - R: 30% adoption, 99.9% reliability, $Xm value tracked
- **Follow-ups:** "How do you track adoption?" "What about indirect value?" "What did you stop measuring?"
- **Story:** [STORY 1 — value tracking](./STORY_BANK.md#story-1-unified-data-platform-transformation)

### Q6. How would you justify a $5 million investment?

- **Tests:** Commercial (15), Strategic (25)

- **Story:** [STORY 7 — Cloud and Databricks cost optimization](./STORY_BANK.md#story-7-cloud-and-databricks-cost-optimization)

### Q7. How would you build a three-year data roadmap?

- **Tests:** Strategic (25), Leadership (20)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Year 1 foundations. Year 2 scale. Year 3 differentiation. Each year anchored to a business outcome."*
- **2min STAR:** See [Week 2, Day 9](./weeks/week-02-enterprise-data-ai-strategy/day-09-strategy-execution.md)
- **Follow-ups:** "How do you handle the middle year?" "What if priorities shift?"
- **Story:** [STORY 1 — 3-year plan](./STORY_BANK.md#story-1-unified-data-platform-transformation)

### Q8. What would you do if AI adoption was low?

- **Tests:** Strategic (25), Leadership (20), Ownership (10)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Diagnose. Then invest in change management, not more models. The model is rarely the bottleneck."*

- **Follow-ups:** "What's the most common adoption barrier?" "How do you measure adoption?"

### Q13. Lakehouse vs warehouse — when would you use each?

- **Tests:** Technical (20)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Warehouse for governed BI. Lakehouse for ML and unstructured. Most enterprises end up with both."*
- **2min STAR:** See [Week 4, Day 16](./weeks/week-04-architecture-ai-engineering/day-16-data-platform-architecture.md)
- **Follow-ups:** "What about pure data lake?" "What's the cost difference?"

### Q14. How would you design a real-time analytics platform?

- **Tests:** Technical (20)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Flink/Kafka for the stream. Materialized views in ClickHouse/Druid. Define freshness SLAs per metric."*
- **2min STAR:** [STORY 5 — Customer 360 and segmentation](./STORY_BANK.md#story-5-customer-360-and-segmentation)
- **Follow-ups:** "How do you handle late-arriving data?" "What's the cost?"

### Q15. How would you handle 10x data growth?

- **Tests:** Technical (20), Commercial (15)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Tiered storage. Compression. Aggregation. Don't grow compute linearly with data."*
- **2min STAR:** [STORY 7 — Cloud and Databricks cost optimization](./STORY_BANK.md#story-7-cloud-and-databricks-cost-optimization)

### Q16. How would you improve data reliability?

- **Tests:** Technical (20), Leadership (20)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Data contracts. SLAs. Lineage. Observability. And a culture where on-call is a rotation, not a punishment."*
- **2min STAR:** See [Week 4, Day 18](./weeks/week-04-architecture-ai-engineering/day-18-reliability-scalability.md) and [STORY 6 — Production data-quality incident](./STORY_BANK.md#story-6-production-data-quality-incident)

### Q17. How would you reduce Spark and cloud costs?

- **Tests:** Technical (20), Commercial (15)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Right-size clusters. Spot. Auto-scaling. Photon. Z-ordering. Liquid clustering. And the unsexy answer: kill unused pipelines."*
- **2min STAR:** [STORY 7 — Cloud and Databricks cost optimization](./STORY_BANK.md#story-7-cloud-and-databricks-cost-optimization)
- **Follow-ups:** "What about reserved instances?"

### Q18. How would you build an enterprise RAG platform?

- **Tests:** Technical (20)
- **60s skeleton:** *"Ingestion → chunking → embedding → vector store → retrieval → reranking → grounded answer → evaluation. Off the event loop for audit. Per-collection filter allowlist. Real eval harness."*

## How to use this bank

1. **Daily warmup:** pick 1 random question. 60-second answer. Score.
2. **Friday review:** 5 questions (one per group). Full 2-minute answers. Record. Score.
3. **Weekend deep-dive:** the question from this week that scored lowest. Rewrite. Re-record.
4. **Mock rounds:** the 4 mock files in `weeks/week-08-.../mocks/` are scripted to draw from this bank.
