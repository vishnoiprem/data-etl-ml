# Data & AI Executive Leadership Masterclass

> **8 weeks · 40 lessons · 8 case studies · 4 mock interview rounds.**
> For senior Data & AI interviews: Chief Data Officer, Head of Data, Head of Data & AI, Director of Data Engineering, Director of AI, VP Data & Analytics.

This course is designed around the transition from **"I build and lead data platforms"** to **"I use data and AI to improve business performance, build organizational capabilities, manage investment, and deliver measurable outcomes."**

It is different from your `ai-fde` (Full-Stack Data Engineer) or `ai-native-de` tracks. Those courses teach you to deliver platforms. This course teaches you to **own** the enterprise data and AI strategy, investment decisions, organization, and business outcomes that an executive role demands.

## What you'll produce

By the end of 8 weeks, you will have:

1. **An executive introduction** — 60-second and 2-minute versions, tested aloud.
2. **Ten reusable leadership stories** — each with a 30-second summary, 2-minute STAR, technical deep-dive, measurable results, and likely follow-ups. Grounded in your real resume evidence. Anything unsubstantiated is marked `[CONFIRM]`.
3. **An enterprise data & AI strategy** — 3-year plan, 1-year roadmap, 90-day leadership plan, and the presentation to defend it to a CEO and board.
4. **A $5M investment business case** — allocation, ROI, TCO, NPV, payback, risk-adjusted return, and realized-vs-projected tracking.
5. **An architecture defense** — modern data + AI platform for 10M customers, with the trade-off analysis a CTO will push on.
6. **A governance and incident response playbook** — owners, stewards, SLAs, escalation, and the 4 most-likely crisis scenarios.
7. **An organization transformation plan** — 50-person org, low morale, inconsistent delivery, rising costs.
8. **A unified executive decision memo** — reconciling CEO speed, CFO cost, CTO security.
9. **Four mock interview rounds** — Hiring Manager, CTO, CEO/CFO, CDO/Board.
10. **A final executive interview playbook** — your introduction, stories, architecture, business cases, frameworks, 90-day plan.

## How to use this course

1. **Read the week README** on Monday. It states the week's objective and the deliverable.
2. **One day per weekday**, 90 minutes per day. Daily structure: 20 concept / 25 case / 30 mock / 15 review. See [`practice/cdo-prompts-90min.md`](./practice/cdo-prompts-90min.md).
3. **Speak every answer out loud.** Recording yourself is the only way to catch hedging, filler words, and weak structure.
4. **Use only metrics you can substantiate.** Every claim in the story bank that isn't in your resume is marked `[CONFIRM]`. Inventing numbers in a CDO interview is a career-ending move.
5. **Score yourself against the rubric every Friday** — see [`RUBRIC.md`](./RUBRIC.md). Target ≥ 90/100 in practice, with no major weakness in any core competency.
