## The 90-min daily drill

| Block | Min | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | 20 | Read the day lesson. Take 3 notes. |
| Case | 25 | Work the day's case study. Write a 1-page brief. |
| Mock | 30 | Speak 2-3 answers out loud. Record. Listen back. |
| Review | 15 | Rewrite the weakest answer. Score yourself. |

See [`practice/cdo-prompts-90min.md`](./practice/cdo-prompts-90min.md) for the full breakdown.

## Weekly review (every Friday, 30 min)

- Re-record the 3 hardest answers of the week.
- Re-score against the rubric (see [`RUBRIC.md`](./RUBRIC.md)).
- Update your personal Weaknesses Ledger (see `STORY_BANK.md` format).

## End-of-course deliverables (Week 8, Friday)

- Executive interview playbook: introduction + 10 stories + architecture + business cases + frameworks + 90-day plan.
- 4 recorded mock interviews, each scored against the rubric.
- Personal scorecard: target ≥ 90/100 in each of the 6 competencies.

---

**The most important principle throughout this course:**

**Business Problem → Strategic Decision → Investment & Trade-offs → Execution → Measurable Outcome.**

Your technical knowledge is an advantage. The next step is to demonstrate that you can make decisions beyond technology: what to invest in, what to stop, who should own delivery, how to manage risk, and how to generate business value.
