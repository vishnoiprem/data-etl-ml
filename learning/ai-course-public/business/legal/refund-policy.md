# Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## Our Commitment

We stand behind the quality of our courses. We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you're not happy, we offer a straightforward refund policy.

## 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

### How It Works

If you're not satisfied with your course purchase, you can request a full refund within **14 days** of your purchase date.

**No questions asked. No hard feelings. No hassle.**

### Eligibility

You're eligible for a refund if:
- Request is made within 14 days of purchase
- You have watched less than 50% of the course content
- You have not downloaded all bonus materials

**Note:** This is a satisfaction guarantee, not a "watch everything and get your money back" policy. We trust our students to be fair.

## How to Request a Refund

### Step 1: Email Us
Send an email to [refund email] with:
- Your full name
- Email used for purchase
- Order number
- Reason for refund (optional but helpful)

### Step 2: Confirmation
We'll confirm your refund request within 24-48 hours.

### Step 3: Processing
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

## What's Refunded

- ✅ Full course purchase price
- ✅ Taxes (if applicable)
- ❌ Transaction fees charged by payment processor (usually 2.9% + 30¢)
- ❌ Any add-ons or upsells purchased separately

## What's Not Refundable

- ❌ Coaching or consulting services (see separate policy)
- ❌ Custom or personalized products
- ❌ Downloaded digital products (PDFs, templates) after download
- ❌ Courses purchased more than 14 days ago
- ❌ Group/corporate licenses
- ❌ Subscription payments (unless within first 14 days)

## Subscription Refunds

For monthly or annual subscriptions:
- Full refund if canceled within 14 days of initial purchase
- No refunds for partial months/years after 14-day window
- Cancel anytime to stop future charges
- Pro-rated refunds not available

## Bundle and Package Refunds

If you purchased a bundle (multiple courses):
- Full refund if requested within 14 days
- Partial refunds based on individual course prices if you completed some courses
- Bonuses must be returned or value deducted from refund

## Chargebacks

### Before Filing a Chargeback
Please contact us first. We're responsive and want to resolve any issues. Most issues can be resolved with a simple conversation.

### Chargeback Policy
If you file a chargeback without contacting us first:
- We may dispute the chargeback
- Your access to all courses will be immediately revoked
- Future purchases may be restricted

We'd rather work with you than fight you. Please reach out first.

## Exceptions

We reserve the right to deny refunds in cases of:
- Suspected fraud or abuse
- Repeated refund requests
- Violation of Terms of Service
- Sharing course access with others

## How We Handle Refund Data

- Refund requests are processed by our support team
- We may analyze refund reasons to improve our courses
- Your data is handled per our Privacy Policy
- We do not penalize customers for requesting refunds

## Special Circumstances

### Medical or Personal Emergencies
If you experience a medical or personal emergency that prevents you from completing the course, contact us. We'll work with you on a case-by-case basis, even outside the 14-day window.

### Technical Issues
If you experience technical problems that prevent course access:
- Contact us immediately at [support email]
- We'll resolve the issue or provide a full refund
- Document the issue with screenshots if possible

## Changes to Refund Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes apply to purchases made after the "Last Updated" date. Previous purchases remain under the policy in effect at the time of purchase.
