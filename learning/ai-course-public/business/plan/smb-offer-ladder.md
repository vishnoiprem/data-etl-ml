# SMB Offer Ladder — the $47 → $197 → $497 → $997 product stack for SG/TW/VN/CN small business owners

> **One-line summary:** the SMB owner in SG/TW/VN/CN is a different buyer than the FDE/AI engineer. The wrong offer ladder is the engineer's $997 cohort; the right ladder is a 4-tier stack where the SMB owner enters at $47, ascends to $197 within 30 days, hits $497 within 90 days, and crosses to $997 only when they're ready for 1:1 consulting. The 30-60-90 day ramp at each tier is grounded in 2026 SG/TW/VN pricing research and the LinkedIn/Substack distribution math from the FDE playbook.

## In 60 seconds

> "4 tiers: $47 quick wins (self-serve, 7-day email course), $197 main course (5 workflows + 1-page SOPs + community), $497 cohort (8 weeks live + 1 setup call), $997 1:1 consulting (2 hours, custom workflows). The buyer enters at $47 from the Substack article; ascends to $197 via the welcome sequence; hits $497 via the cohort pitch; reaches $997 via LinkedIn DM. The 30-60-90 ramp: 20 paid subs in month 1, 50 in month 2, 150 in month 3 = $4.6K month 3. The wrong offer ladder is the FDE's $997 cohort as the front door. The right ladder is $47 at the front door, $997 at the back."

## The 4 tiers, in detail

### Tier 1: $47 — "AI Quick Wins for SMB Owners" (self-serve)

**What's in it:**
- The 5 prompts from the Substack article, customized for the buyer's business (5-min intake form at purchase: business name, language, top 3 workflows).
- The 1-page SOP template for each workflow.
- The 7-day email ramp (one 5-minute lesson per day, 7 days).
- A private Notion page with the prompts + the SOPs + a "what to do if it doesn't work" FAQ.

**What it's NOT:**
- Not a 40-hour course. The 5 prompts are 1 page. The SOPs are 1 page each. The 7-day email ramp is 35 minutes total of content.
- Not a community. The owner is alone with the prompts; the cohort comes at $197.
- Not 1:1 access. The CTA at the end of the 7-day ramp is the $197 tier.

**The math:**
- Margin: 100% (digital product, $0 variable cost; ConvertKit free up to 1K subs; Notion free).
- Realistic conversion from the Substack article: 1-3% of readers. If the article gets 5,000 readers in month 1, that's 50-150 sales.
- 50 sales × $47 = **$2,350 in month 1**.

**The risk:**
- Refund rate. The wrong choice is to position the $47 as "save 10 hours/week" and not deliver. The right choice is to set expectations: "ship 1 workflow in 7 days; the other 4 come later." Refund rate target: <5%.

### Tier 2: $197 — "AI Workflows for SMB Owners" (self-serve + community)

**What's in it:**
- Everything in $47.
- All 5 workflows customized (the $47 tier customizes 1; the $197 tier customizes all 5).
- A 30-day async support window (24-hour reply, 5 days/week) — the owner emails questions, you reply.
- A private Skool community (or Discord) for the cohort.
- A monthly group call (60 min, Q&A, 1 win per person).

**What it's NOT:**
- Not 1:1 access. The 1:1 setup call is in the $497 tier.
- Not a live cohort. The $497 tier is the live cohort. The $197 tier is self-serve + community.
- Not a custom build. The 5 prompts are the same 5; the customization is on the intake form, not on a custom build.

**The math:**
- Margin: ~95% (ConvertKit $29/mo, Skool $99/mo, plus your async-support time at $30/hour × 1-2 hours/buyer/month).
- Realistic conversion: 30-40% of $47 buyers ascend to $197 within 30-60 days. If 50 people buy $47 in month 1, 15-20 buy $197 in month 2.
- 20 sales × $197 = **$3,940 in month 2**.

**The risk:**
- Support load. The wrong choice is to promise "24-hour reply" and not deliver. The right choice is to time-box: 5 days/week, 24 hours, not weekends.

### Tier 3: $497 — "AI Quick Wins Cohort" (8 weeks live, capped at 20)

**What's in it:**
- Everything in $197.
- 8 weekly 90-minute group calls (the cohort, capped at 20, working through the 5 workflows together).
- 1 one-hour 1:1 setup call with the buyer (the only 1:1 in the stack until the $997 tier).
- The 5 workflows built and deployed in the buyer's actual business (you build alongside them).
- A "graduation" deliverable: a 1-page AI runbook the buyer can hand to a new hire.

**What it's NOT:**
- Not a "mastermind." The 8 calls are structured, not open-mic.
- Not a guarantee. The wrong choice is to promise "save 10 hours/week by week 4." The right choice is to set expectations: "by week 8, you have 3 of the 5 workflows running; the other 2 by week 12."

**The math:**
- Margin: ~85% (ConvertKit + Skool + your 1:1 call at $100/hour + the 8 group calls at $200/session).
- Realistic conversion: 10-15% of $197 buyers ascend to $497 within 60-90 days. If 20 people buy $197 in month 2, 2-3 buy $497 in month 3.
- 3 sales × $497 = **$1,491 in month 3**.

**The risk:**
- Cohort fill. The wrong choice is to run a cohort of 5 (the math doesn't work). The right choice is to wait for 15-20 buyers or push the cohort by 30 days. The first cohort may run at 10; that's OK — the margin still works at 10, just less profit.

### Tier 4: $997 — "Custom AI Workflow Build" (1:1 consulting, 2 hours)

**What's in it:**
- A 2-hour 1:1 working session: you sit with the buyer, build 1-2 custom AI workflows for their actual business (not the 5 standard ones).
- A follow-up 30-minute call at 30 days to refine.
- A 1-page custom AI runbook for the buyer's specific business.
- 90 days of async support (the buyer emails questions, you reply within 48 hours).

**What it's NOT:**
- Not a course. The wrong choice is to sell "consulting" and deliver a course. The right choice is to build the actual workflow in the actual business, live on the call.
- Not for the buyer who hasn't done the $47 or $197 tier. The 2 hours is wasted on someone who doesn't know what an AI workflow is. The right choice is to require $197 as a prerequisite, or charge $1,500 for buyers who skip the ladder.

**The math:**
- Margin: ~95% (your time at $400/hour × 2.5 hours = $1,000; you charge $997; you break even or run a small loss; the value is in the case study + the testimonial).
- Realistic conversion: 5-10% of $497 buyers, or 1-2 LinkedIn DMs/month from the Substack post.
- 2 sales × $997 = **$1,994 in month 3+**.

**The risk:**
- Scope creep. The wrong choice is to keep building for free after the 2 hours. The right choice is the 30-day follow-up call as the only additional commitment, then a paid extension at $500/hour if they want more.

## The geography math (SG/TW/VN/CN specifics)

The 2026 small business landscape in these 4 markets:

| Market | SMB count | Avg AI spend | Conversion expectation |
|--------|-----------|--------------|------------------------|
| **Singapore** | 300K+ SMBs (defined as <200 employees) | $50-200/month on tools | Highest (English-fluent, AI-curious, high LinkedIn usage) |
| **Taiwan** | 1.5M+ SMBs | $30-100/month | Strong (tech-savvy, but Mandarin-first content) |
| **Vietnam** | 800K+ SMBs | $20-50/month | Growing (younger owner-operators, very active on Facebook/Zalo) |
| **China** | 50M+ SMBs (huge TAM, but… ) | $30-100/month | Restricted (need WeChat-native distribution, not LinkedIn) |

**The implication:**
- **Month 1-3:** Singapore-first. 60-70% of buyers from SG. The Substack article is in English; the LinkedIn presence is SG-first.
- **Month 4-6:** Add Taiwan. Translate the 5 prompts to Mandarin; re-launch the Substack post in Traditional Chinese.
- **Month 7-12:** Add Vietnam. The 5 prompts are mostly language-agnostic; the SOPs need Vietnamese translation.
- **China:** out of scope for the LinkedIn funnel. WeChat distribution requires a separate playbook (WeChat公众号 + WeChat group + WeChat Pay). Defer to year 2.

**The wrong choice:** launch in all 4 markets on day 1. The right choice is SG-first, TW second, VN third, CN year 2.

## The buyer journey (the actual conversion path)

The SMB owner enters at the Substack article, not at the sales page. The buyer journey:

1. **Day 0:** reads the Substack article (or a LinkedIn post linking to it).
2. **Day 1-3:** thinks about it. Maybe replies "WORKFLOWS" to the email. Gets the free 7-day email course.
3. **Day 4-7:** runs the first workflow. Saves 2-3 hours. Thinks "this actually works."
4. **Day 7-14:** finishes the free 7-day course. The day-7 email pitches the $47 Quick Wins.
5. **Day 14-30:** buys $47. Customizes the 5 prompts. Runs all 5 workflows. Saves 10-15 hours/week.
6. **Day 30-60:** the welcome email at day 30 pitches the $197 Main Course (community + 30 days of support).
7. **Day 60-90:** buys $197. Joins the Skool community. Attends the monthly group call. Sees other SMB owners getting results.
8. **Day 90-120:** the cohort pitch email (or a LinkedIn DM) pitches the $497 cohort.
9. **Day 120+:** buys $497. Joins the cohort. Gets the 1-hour 1:1 setup call. Graduates with the 1-page AI runbook.
10. **Day 180+:** the LinkedIn DM pitches the $997 1:1 consulting. (Optional: some buyers skip from $497 directly to $997 if they have a custom need.)

**The conversion math (per 100 Substack readers):**
- 100 readers
- 30 sign up for the email course (30%)
- 10 reply "WORKFLOWS" with a question (10%)
- 5 buy $47 (5%)
- 2 buy $197 (2%)
- 0.3 buy $497 (0.3%)
- 0.1 buy $997 (0.1%)

**Per 100 readers, revenue: 5 × $47 + 2 × $197 + 0.3 × $497 + 0.1 × $997 = $235 + $394 + $149 + $100 = $878.**

**Per 5,000 readers (the month-1 Substack reach): $43,900.** (Note: this assumes the funnel works at scale; the first 100 readers convert at 5%, the next 4,900 may convert at 1-2% as the audience broadens. Realistic blended conversion: 1-3%.)

## The 5 metrics to watch weekly

| Metric | What it tells you | Target month 1 | Target month 3 |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Substack open rate** | Is the headline working? | 40-50% | 40-50% sustained |
| **Email-to-47% conversion** | Is the welcome sequence working? | 5% | 5-8% |
| **47-to-197% conversion (30 days)** | Is the $47 product delivering? | 30-40% | 30-40% |
| **LinkedIn DMs from the article** | Is the top-of-funnel working? | 10-20/month | 30-50/month |
| **Refund rate** | Are the promises overhyped? | <5% | <5% |

If the **email-to-47% conversion** is below 3% after 100 emails, the welcome sequence is too long or the pitch is too aggressive. If the **47-to-197% conversion** is below 20% after 30 days, the $47 product is not delivering (the buyer doesn't see the value of community).

## The wrong choices to avoid

1. **The wrong offer ladder:** the FDE's $997 cohort as the front door. The SMB owner is not ready to pay $997 on day 1; they need the $47 → $197 → $497 → $997 journey.
2. **The wrong funnel:** LinkedIn → $997 cold DM. The SMB owner needs 60+ days of trust before paying $997. The right funnel is LinkedIn → Substack → $47 → $197 → $497 → $997.
3. **The wrong geography:** launching in CN/VN on day 1. The right geography is SG-first, then TW, then VN, then CN in year 2.
4. **The wrong pitch:** "AI will 10x your business." The right pitch is "save 10 hours/week with 5 workflows, 5 prompts, 1 hour/day."
5. **The wrong metric:** total revenue. The right metric is **LinkedIn DMs from the article** — that's the leading indicator of consulting + course sales in month 3+.
