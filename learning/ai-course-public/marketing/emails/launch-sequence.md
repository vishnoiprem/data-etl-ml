## Sequence Notes

**Send times:**
- Day 0: 10 AM (waitlist reminder)
- Day 1: 9 AM (cart open) + 8 PM (social proof)
- Day 2: 10 AM (objection handler)
- Day 3: 10 AM (reminder) + 9 PM (final call)

**Total emails:** 6
**Expected conversion:** 3-8% of email list

**Tips:**
- Space emails out (don't spam)
- Use social proof heavily
- Address objections directly
- Create urgency (countdown works)
- Make CTA button obvious

**After cart closes:**
- Wait 30 days
- Run "Last Chance" email
- Move to evergreen funnel
