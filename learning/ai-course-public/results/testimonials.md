# Customer Testimonials & Case Studies

Collect, organize, and use powerful customer testimonials and case studies.

## Why Testimonials Matter

**Social proof is the #1 conversion factor.** When potential customers see others like them succeeding, they buy.

- 92% of consumers read reviews before buying
- 72% trust businesses more with positive reviews
- Testimonials increase conversion by 34% on average

**In the AI course space, you need:**
- Written testimonials (for sales pages)
- Video testimonials (for ads and YouTube)
- Case studies (for content marketing)
- Before/after stories (for emotional connection)

---

## How to Collect Testimonials

### When to Ask
- ✅ After they complete the course (Day 7-14)
- ✅ When they share a win in the community
- ✅ After they achieve a specific result
- ✅ When they email you with positive feedback
- ✅ 30, 60, 90 days after purchase (for long-term results)

### How to Ask

#### Email Template #1: Post-Course Completion

```
Subject: Quick favor? (2 min)

Hi [Name],

Congrats on finishing [Course Name]! 🎉

I noticed you [specific action they took, e.g., "built your customer service system" or "created 30 days of content"].

I'd love to know:

1. What's the #1 thing you learned?
2. What results have you seen so far?
3. Would you recommend this course? (Yes/no)
4. Any specific wins or results?

If you have 2 minutes, just hit reply. I read every one and feature the best ones (with permission) on my sales page.

As a thank you, I'll send you [bonus resource, discount code, etc.].

Thanks!
[Your name]
```

#### Email Template #2: Specific Result

```
Subject: Congrats on [specific result]! 🎉

Hi [Name],

I saw your message about [specific result they shared].

That's amazing! How long did it take?

If you have a minute, would you write 2-3 sentences about your experience? Just answer:

- What was your situation before?
- What changed after the course?
- What results have you seen?

I might feature your story (with your permission and first name) to inspire others.

As a thank you, here's [discount code / bonus / etc.].

Thanks!
[Your name]
```

#### In-Community Post

```
🎉 CELEBRATION TIME! 🎉

Who here has had a win since joining [Course Name]?

Drop a comment with:
- Your business type
- Your biggest result
- One sentence about your experience

I'll feature the best ones (with permission) to inspire others!
```

### What to Ask For

**Specific results:**
- "I saved 12 hours per week"
- "I replaced my $500/month VA"
- "I got 15 new customers from my AI-generated content"

**Specific transformations:**
- "I went from 60-hour weeks to 40-hour weeks"
- "I went from zero content to 30 days of content"
- "I went from overwhelmed to organized"

**Specific features they loved:**
- "The customer service prompts saved me hours"
- "The content calendar template was gold"
- "The 7-day action plan made it so simple"

## Video Testimonial Template

### What to Ask For
- 60-90 seconds long
- Shot on phone (good enough)
- Cover 3 things:
  1. Before the course (struggle)
  2. After the course (result)
  3. Recommendation (who should buy)

### Questions to Ask Customer

```
1. Tell me about your business before taking the course.
2. What was the biggest challenge you were facing?
3. What specific results have you seen?
4. What was your favorite part of the course?
5. Who would you recommend this course to?
6. Any final words for someone considering it?
```

### Example Script

```
"Hi, I'm [Name] from [City]. I run a [type] business.

Before this course, I was [struggle - e.g., drowning in customer emails, working 70 hours a week, no time to grow].

After taking [Course Name], I [result - e.g., saved 12 hours per week, created 30 days of content in 1 hour, automated my customer service].

My favorite part was [specific feature - e.g., the customer service prompts, the 7-day action plan, the templates].

I'd recommend this to [target audience - e.g., any small business owner, anyone who feels overwhelmed, anyone who wants to work less].

It's worth every penny. Don't wait like I did."
```

### Where to Use Video Testimonials
- Sales page (embedded)
- YouTube (in video descriptions)
- Social media (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)
- Ads (Facebook, YouTube)
- Email (welcome sequence)

## Permission & Legal

### Get Written Permission

**Email:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for the amazing testimonial! 

I have your permission to use it on my sales page, social media, and ads? (Yes/no)

And I can use your first name, location, and business type? (Yes/no/adjust)

If you'd like to review where it'll be used, I can send you a preview.

Thanks!
[Your name]
```

### When Customers Say Yes
- Add to testimonial database
- Save photo (if provided)
- Save full email (for context)
- Add to sales page immediately
- Tag them in your CRM for future launches

### What If They Say No?
- Thank them
- Don't use it
- Ask if they have feedback that doesn't require public sharing

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Don't fabricate testimonials** (it's illegal in many places)
❌ **Don't use without permission** (legal issues)
❌ **Don't edit to change meaning** (just fix grammar)
❌ **Don't use only from friends/family** (need real customers)
❌ **Don't wait to collect** (start from day 1)

✅ **Be specific** (numbers, timeframes, results)
✅ **Be diverse** (different industries, locations, types)
✅ **Be recent** (within last 6 months)
✅ **Be real** (use real names and photos with permission)
✅ **Be grateful** (thank every customer who provides one)
