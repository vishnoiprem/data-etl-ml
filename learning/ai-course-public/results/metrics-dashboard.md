## Key Ratios to Track

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
**Formula:** Total Marketing Spend / New Customers
**Target:** <$30 for $47 product, <$100 for $197 product

### Lifetime Value (LTV)
**Formula:** Average Order Value × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan
**Target:** 3x CAC minimum

### Email Conversion Rate
**Formula:** (Sales from email / Total emails sent) × 100
**Target:** >0.5% (1 sale per 200 emails)

### Refund Rate
**Formula:** (Refunds / Total Sales) × 100
**Target:** <5%

### Completion Rate
**Formula:** (Students who finish / Total students) × 100
**Target:** >50%

### Affiliate Revenue (if applicable)
**Formula:** Affiliate Sales / Total Sales
**Target:** 20-30% of revenue

## Red Flags (Address Immediately)

🚩 Conversion rate below 0.5%
🚩 Refund rate above 10%
🚩 Email open rate below 15%
🚩 Completion rate below 25%
🚩 CAC higher than LTV
🚩 No sales for 2+ weeks
🚩 Negative cash flow for 2+ months

---

## Green Flags (Double Down)

✅ Conversion rate above 3%
✅ Refund rate below 3%
✅ Email open rate above 30%
✅ Completion rate above 60%
✅ LTV:CAC ratio above 3:1
✅ Consistent weekly sales
✅ Organic growth (no ads needed)
✅ Students getting real results
✅ Testimonials flowing in

## Weekly Review Ritual

### Friday Afternoon (30 minutes)

**Minutes 0-10: Update the Numbers**
- Fill in all metrics from the week
- Pull from tools (Gumroad, ConvertKit, Google Analytics)
- Update spreadsheets

**Minutes 10-20: Analyze**
- What worked? What didn't?
- Why? (Look for patterns)
- Compare to last week, last month
- Compare to goals

**Minutes 20-30: Plan Next Week**
- Set 3 main goals for next week
- Identify experiments to try
- Schedule content
- Adjust strategy if needed
