# Hiring

The hiring loop is owned by the hiring manager with support from a recruiting
partner. This document describes the loop; refer to the recruiting portal for
templates.

## Loop

1. **Recruiter screen** — 30 min, scope and comp alignment.
2. **Hiring manager** — 60 min, deep dive on craft and judgment.
3. **Technical** — 60 min, role-specific technical assessment.
4. **Bar raiser** — 60 min, calibrated against the company bar.

For senior roles, add a **System Design** round between Technical and Bar
Raiser.

## Scorecards

- Scorecards are due **within 24 hours** of each interview.
- Scorecards are visible to the loop. Deliberation happens in the debrief.
- Decisions are made by the hiring manager with input from the loop.

## Offer

- Offers are extended by the recruiter after the debrief.
- All offers above the band require VP approval.
- The recruiting partner handles the negotiation.

## What we don't do

- We don't ask brainteasers.
- We don't ask candidates to write code on a whiteboard.
- We don't ghost. Every candidate gets a status update within 5 business
  days of every round.
