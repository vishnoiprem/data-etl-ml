## Send checklist (per supplier, in order)

For each of the 3 above:

- [ ] Open `priyam@nordic-sg.com` (or your preferred mailbox)
- [ ] Copy the RFQ block into a new email
- [ ] Fill in `[bracketed]` placeholders (WhatsApp number, etc.)
- [ ] **Attach:** Nothing on the first send. (Suppliers will ask for
      what they need; attachments on the first email look desperate.)
- [ ] **Send from** the company domain if possible (not a free
      webmail — improves reply rate)
- [ ] **Log the send** in `supplier_rfq_tracker.md` (next file):
      - `python3 mark_supplier_reply.py "Gold Star" --replied ...` (when reply arrives)

      from `02_supplier_outreach_emails.txt`)
