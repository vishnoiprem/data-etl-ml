## 4. Supplier Sourcing Playbook (Week 2-3)

### 4.1 The 5-message outreach (Alibaba)

**Day 1: Shortlist 5 suppliers**
- Search Alibaba: "smart watch fitness band" + Trade Assurance + Gold Supplier
- Filter: 3+ years, transaction > US$50K, response rate > 90%
- Save top 5 to a spreadsheet

**Day 2: First message to all 5**

```
Subject: Bulk order enquiry — Smart Fitness Band (UK Amazon FBA)

Hi,

I'm setting up a UK Amazon FBA business and need a long-term
supplier for smart fitness bands. Initial order: 500 units,
with monthly reorders of 1,000-2,000 units.

Specifications:
- Heart rate monitor + SpO2
- 1.47" HD display
- IP68 waterproof
- 7-day battery life
- Magnetic charging
- 5+ watch faces
- CE / RoHS certified (mandatory for UK)
- Branded gift box

Please quote:
1. FOB price per unit at 500 / 1,000 / 5,000 units
2. Sample cost + shipping to Singapore
3. Lead time from PO to FOB Shenzhen
4. Payment terms (TT preferred)
5. 3 trade references from UK/EU customers

I will be placing 1 sample order to verify quality before
committing to the bulk order. Thank you.

Best,
[Your name]
Nordic SG Pte Ltd
UEN: XXXXXXXK
```

**Day 3-4: Score replies on 5 axes**

| Axis | Weight | What to look for |
|---|---:|---|
| Price | 25% | Lowest at 1K units, must be < $9.50 |
| Lead time | 20% | < 21 days PO to FOB |
| Quality (samples) | 25% | Build quality, packaging, certifications |
| Communication | 15% | Response time, English fluency, transparency |
| References | 15% | Real customer references willing to be called |

**Day 5-7: Order samples ($30-50/each × 3 finalists)**
- Ship to SG (3-5 days via DHL)
- Inspect personally
- Run a 7-day battery test, swim test, app pairing test

**Day 8: Pick winner, place PO**

### 4.2 Negotiation tactics
- **Ask for 5-8% off at 1,000 units** even though you're starting at 500
- **Ask for free samples** if you're a "first-time buyer" — most will say yes for the testimonial
- **Ask for exclusive color/face combos** so you have something not on Amazon yet
- **Always pay 30/70 (TT 30% deposit, 70% on B/L copy)** — never 100% upfront

### 4.3 Quality control
- Hire a **QCC inspection agent** (US$200-300 per inspection) to check 10% of units before shipping
- Services: **QIMA** (qima.com), **AsiaInspection**, **V-Trust**
- Book inspection 7-10 days before your supplier's promised ship date
- The cost is 2-3% of the order, but it saves you from $5K of unusable inventory

## 6. Logistics & Shipping (Week 4-5)

### 6.1 Freight forwarding
- Use a **CN→UK FBA specialist**: Flexport, Freightos, Barrington Freight, or a CN-side forwarder like **ZhiYun Logistics** (zhihangfy.com)
- For 500 units (0.3-0.4 CBM), **LCL ocean freight** is cheapest: US$300-500, 30-40 days
- Alternative: **air freight** for first sample: US$8-12/kg, 5-7 days
- Recommended: ship first 100 units by air (sample + first sales), rest by sea

### 6.2 UK customs clearance
- EORI number: free, 1-day application at gov.uk
- Your CN supplier handles export declaration
- Your UK forwarder handles UK import declaration
- HS code: **8517.62.90** (smart watch, other)

### 6.3 FBA inbound checklist
- [ ] **Box labels:** each unit in a retail box, with EAN barcode (Amazon assigns if you don't have)
- [ ] **Carton labels:** 50 units per master carton, each with FNSKU label
- [ ] **Pallet:** standard UK pallet 1200×1000mm, max 25kg per box, max 600mm height
- [ ] **Appointment:** book delivery slot at Amazon's UK fulfilment centre (CWL1, MAN2, etc.)
- [ ] **Cost:** Amazon inbound shipping £0.40-0.60/kg via Amazon Partnered Carrier (use them first 90 days for discount)

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supplier ships defective units** | Medium | High ($5K loss) | QCC inspection before ship; sample testing |
| **Amazon suspends listing (IP complaint)** | Low | High | Brand registry + clean record; avoid Apple/Samsung keywords |
| **Price war from CN competitors** | High | Medium | Brand differentiation, PPC, A+ content |
| **FX rate swings (USD/SGD/GBP)** | Medium | Low | Multi-currency account; natural hedge across 3 currencies |
| **Black-hat competitor attacks listing** | Medium | High | Brand Registry + transparent metrics; report to Amazon |
| **UK recession reduces fitness spend** | Medium | Medium | Price elasticity is real; have a discount strategy ready |
| **Customs clearance delay** | Low | Medium | Use experienced forwarder with UK EORI |
| **Battery safety incident** | Low | High | CE/UKCA certified cells, MSDS on file, product liability insurance (£200/yr) |

### 8.1 Insurance
- **Product liability insurance:** £200-400/yr — mandatory for UK Amazon FBA
- **Marine cargo insurance:** 0.3% of cargo value — recommended for ocean shipments
- **Amazon A-to-Z guarantee reserve:** Amazon holds your money 7-14 days after delivery
