## What This Means

- The 10K-cycle analysis re-ranks every supplier with ±5% measurement noise, simulating the uncertainty of working with imperfect data.
- The supplier with the lowest average rank across 10,000 cycles is the most consistent winner.
- Top-1 finishes = how often each supplier was ranked #1 in a single cycle.
- Use this to choose 3 finalists → order samples → pick the final supplier.

## ⚠️ Reminder: This is a First-Pass Filter, Not a Final Decision

- Sample quality, packaging, and real factory visit (or QCC inspection) beat any data score.
- Trade Assurance protects you on Alibaba; use it for every first order.
- Always pay 30/70 (T/T 30% deposit, 70% on B/L copy). Never 100% upfront.
