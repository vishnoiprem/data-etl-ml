# Scaling Data Pipelines Without Breaking Them

In today's fast-paced world, data teams are under increasing pressure to scale their pipelines. This article will explore the topic of pipeline reliability and what you can do about it.

## The Challenge

Data pipelines can be difficult to maintain. There are many moving parts, and things go wrong. It is important to note that reliability matters.

## Common Approaches

Many teams choose to add more infrastructure. Others choose to monitor more closely. Each approach has trade-offs that should be considered carefully by engineering leaders.

## A Better Way

Some teams have found that a structured approach helps. The result is often a more reliable pipeline. It is worth noting that this varies by organisation.
