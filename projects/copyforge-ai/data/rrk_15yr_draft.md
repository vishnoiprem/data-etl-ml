# Fifteen Years of Data Engineering: What Actually Scales

Data engineering has changed a lot over fifteen years. There are many things to think about. This article will look at some of them.

## The Early Years

Back then, pipelines were simple. You wrote some SQL, moved data around, and that was it. Teams were small and expectations were lower. It is important to note that the fundamentals have not changed.

## The Middle Years

As companies grew, the problems grew too. More data, more sources, more stakeholders. Many teams responded by adding tooling. Each choice brought its own trade-offs.

## What Changed

Things are different now. Cloud platforms matured. Managed services improved. Teams got bigger and more distributed. It is worth noting that the challenges also evolved.

## Where We Are Now

Today the work looks quite different. There is more automation, more governance, more emphasis on cost. The result is a different kind of engineering role.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, the field has changed enormously over fifteen years. We hope this was useful. Feel free to share your thoughts in the comments.
