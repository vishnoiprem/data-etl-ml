Everything you need to build, launch, and grow your AI teaching business.

## 1. Course Hosting & Sales

### Option A: Gumroad (Recommended to Start)
**Cost:** Free (10% per sale)
**Best for:** Digital products, simple setup

**Features:**
- Upload videos, PDFs, files
- Built-in payment processing
- Simple checkout
- License keys
- Basic analytics
- Email updates to customers

**Setup time:** 30 minutes
**URL:** gumroad.com

### Option B: Systeme.io (Best Free Option)
**Cost:** Free (unlimited courses, emails, funnels)
**Best for:** All-in-one solution

**Features:**
- Unlimited courses
- Email marketing (unlimited)
- Sales funnels
- Landing pages
- Affiliate programs
- Webinars
- 0% transaction fees (on free plan)

**Setup time:** 1-2 hours
**URL:** systeme.io

### Option C: Teachable
**Cost:** $39-119/month
**Best for:** Professional course creators

**Features:**
- Beautiful course player
- Quizzes, certificates
- Student management
- Advanced analytics
- Marketing tools
- Custom domain

**URL:** teachable.com

### Comparison

| Feature | Gumroad | Systeme.io | Teachable |
|---------|---------|------------|-----------|
| Free tier | Yes (10% fee) | Yes (unlimited) | No |
| Transaction fee | 10% | 0% | 5% |
| Email included | No | Yes | No |
| Funnels | No | Yes | No |
| Best for | Beginners | All-in-one | Pros |

**My recommendation:** Start with Systeme.io. Move to Teachable at $5K MRR.
