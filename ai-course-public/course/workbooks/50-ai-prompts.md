# 50 AI Prompts for Small Business Owners

Save this PDF and use it as your daily AI playbook.

## Marketing Prompts (20)

### Social Media

**1. Instagram Caption Generator**
"Write 5 Instagram captions for [my product/service]. Target audience is [demographics]. Tone: [casual/professional/funny]. Include emojis and a call to action."

**2. Facebook Post Ideas**
"Give me 10 Facebook post ideas for a [type] business. Mix of educational, promotional, and engagement posts. Each should be 2-3 sentences."

**3. LinkedIn Thought Leadership**
"Write a LinkedIn post about [industry trend]. Position me as a thought leader. Use a personal story + lesson + takeaway structure. 150-200 words."

**4. TikTok Video Scripts**
"Give me 5 TikTok video ideas for [my business]. Each should hook viewers in 3 seconds and teach something useful. Under 60 seconds each."

**5. Twitter Thread**
"Write a 7-tweet thread about [topic relevant to my business]. Tweet 1 should be a strong hook. End with a call to action."

**6. Hashtag Research**
"Generate 30 relevant hashtags for [my industry/niche]. Mix of high-volume (1M+) and niche-specific (10K-100K) tags."

**7. Content Calendar**
"Create a 30-day social media content calendar for [my business]. 1 post per day. Mix of: educational (40%), behind-the-scenes (20%), promotional (20%), engagement (20%)."

**8. Reel/Short Ideas**
"Give me 10 Instagram Reel ideas for [my business]. Each should be educational, under 30 seconds, and solve a specific problem my customer has."

### Email Marketing

**9. Welcome Email Sequence**
"Write a 5-email welcome series for new subscribers. Email 1: welcome + deliver freebie. Email 2: my story. Email 3: best tips. Email 4: case study. Email 5: pitch my offer."

**10. Subject Lines**
"Write 10 email subject lines for [topic]. Aim for 6-10 words. Create curiosity, urgency, or promise a benefit. Under 50 characters each."

**11. Cold Outreach Email**
"Write a cold email to [type of prospect]. Subject: [topic]. 3 sentences max. Problem → Solution → CTA. Conversational tone."

**12. Re-engagement Email**
"Write an email to subscribers who haven't opened in 90 days. Acknowledge they might not be interested. Offer to unsubscribe OR give a special deal. Keep it short."

**13. Launch Email**
"Write a 3-email launch sequence for [my new product/service]. Email 1: announce. Email 2: features + benefits. Email 3: last chance + scarcity."

**14. Testimonial Request**
"Write an email asking happy customers for a testimonial. Make it easy: ask 3 specific questions they can answer in 2 minutes."

### Advertising

**15. Facebook Ad Copy**
"Write 3 Facebook ads for [product/service]. Each should have: hook (first line), problem, solution, social proof, CTA. Under 125 characters each."

**16. Google Ad Headlines**
"Write 15 Google Ads headlines (under 30 characters) and 4 descriptions (under 90 characters) for [my business]. Include keywords: [list]."

**17. Landing Page Headline**
"Write 5 landing page headlines for [my offer]. Each should focus on a different angle: benefit, curiosity, urgency, social proof, or transformation."

## Customer Service Prompts (15)

**18. FAQ Answer Generator**
"I run a [type] business. Here's a customer question: [question]. Write a helpful, friendly response that addresses their concern and positions my expertise. Under 100 words."

**19. Complaint Response**
"A customer is upset about [issue]. Write a response that: 1) apologizes sincerely, 2) takes responsibility, 3) offers a solution, 4) thanks them for feedback. Professional but warm tone."

**20. Refund Request Handler**
"Write a response to a refund request for [product/service]. Be understanding, offer alternative solutions first, but approve if they insist. Keep it short and empathetic."

**21. Pre-sale Question**
"A potential customer asks: [question]. Write a response that answers their question AND highlights why my [product/service] is the best choice. Use social proof if possible."

**22. Thank You Message**
"Write a thank you message to send after a customer buys [product/service]. Express genuine gratitude, tell them what to expect next, and ask for a review in 30 days."

**23. Follow-up After No Response**
"A customer stopped responding. Write a friendly follow-up message. Acknowledge they're busy, ask if they have questions, and offer to help. Under 50 words."

**24. Review Response (Positive)**
"Write a response to this 5-star review: [paste review]. Thank them, mention something specific, invite them back."

**25. Review Response (Negative)**
"Write a response to this 2-star review: [paste review]. Apologize, don't make excuses, offer to make it right offline, keep it short."

**26. Chatbot Training**
"Create training data for my customer service chatbot. Here's my business: [description]. Common questions: [list 10]. Write friendly, helpful responses for each."

**27. WhatsApp Quick Reply**
"Write 5 quick WhatsApp replies for common customer messages. Under 30 words each. Friendly and casual tone."

## Operations Prompts (10)

**28. Weekly Planner**
"Create my weekly business plan. Business type: [type]. Main goals: [list 3]. Hours available: [X]. Break it down day-by-day with specific tasks."

**29. Daily To-Do List**
"Here's my to-do list: [paste list]. Organize by priority, group similar tasks, and suggest which to delegate or automate. Format as time-blocked schedule."

**30. Meeting Agenda**
"Create an agenda for a [X] minute meeting about [topic]. Include: intro, main discussion points, decisions needed, action items. 5-7 items max."

**31. Meeting Summary**
"Summarize these meeting notes: [paste]. Format as: Key Decisions, Action Items (who, what, when), Next Steps. Bullet points only."

**32. Project Plan**
"Create a project plan for [project]. Timeline: [X weeks]. List all tasks, assign who does what, set deadlines, and identify dependencies."

**33. Standard Operating Procedure**
"Write an SOP for [task]. Format: Purpose, When to do it, Step-by-step instructions, Common mistakes, How to know it's done well. Beginner-friendly."

**34. Hiring Post**
"Write a job post for [position] at my [type] business. Include: role overview, responsibilities, requirements, what we offer, how to apply. Conversational tone."

**35. Vendor Email**
"Write a professional email to a potential vendor asking about [product/service]. Be specific, mention timeline, and ask for quote + references."

**36. Invoice Description**
"Write a professional invoice description for [service/product]. Include: what was delivered, value provided, and payment terms. Under 50 words."

**37. Contract Clause**
"Write a contract clause for [scenario]. Plain English, protect both parties, clear terms. 2-3 sentences max."

## Strategy & Growth Prompts (5)

**38. Competitor Analysis**
"Analyze these 3 competitors: [names]. For each, list: their positioning, pricing, strengths, weaknesses, and what I can learn from them. Format as comparison table."

**39. SWOT Analysis**
"Help me do a SWOT analysis for my [type] business. Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats. Be specific and honest. Suggest 3 action items for each."

**40. Pricing Strategy**
"I'm selling [product/service]. My costs are [$X]. Competitors charge [$Y]. Help me set pricing. Consider: value provided, market positioning, psychological pricing."

**41. Customer Avatar**
"Create a detailed customer avatar for [my product/service]. Include: demographics, psychographics, pain points, goals, objections, where they hang out online."

**42. Growth Ideas**
"My business is [description]. I have [X hours/week] and [$Y budget]. Give me 10 specific growth ideas ranked by ROI. Include time estimate and difficulty for each."

## Bonus: 8 Power Prompts

**43. The "Act As" Expert**
"Act as a [type of expert] with 20 years of experience. I'm going to ask you questions about [topic]. Give me specific, actionable advice — not generic stuff."

**44. The "Step-by-Step"**
"Give me a step-by-step plan to [achieve goal]. Include: what to do, when to do it, what tools to use, and how to measure success. Make it doable in [timeframe]."

**45. The "Pros and Cons"**
"Help me decide between [option A] and [option B]. For each, list 5 pros, 5 cons, and 3 questions I should ask myself. Then give your recommendation."

**46. The "Roast My Idea"**
"Here's my business idea: [describe]. Be honest and critical. What's wrong with it? What am I missing? How would you improve it?"

**47. The "Copy That Converts"**
"Here's a sales page I like: [paste or describe]. Analyze why it works. Then write a similar sales page for [my product]."

**48. The "Explain Like I'm 5"**
"Explain [complex topic] like I'm 5 years old. Use simple words, fun examples, and analogies. Make it so clear a child could understand."

**49. The "Find the Flaw"**
"Here's my plan: [describe]. Find 5 potential problems I haven't thought of. For each, suggest how to prevent it or fix it if it happens."

**50. The "Quick Win"**
"I have 15 minutes. Give me one specific thing I can do right now to [improve X / fix Y / grow Z]. Make it something I can finish before this timer goes off."

## How to Use This Prompt Pack

1. **Bookmark this page** for quick reference
2. **Copy and customize** each prompt for your business
3. **Save your best prompts** in a Google Doc for reuse
4. **Track what works** — note which prompts give you the best results
5. **Iterate** — tweak prompts based on the AI's responses

## Pro Tips

- **Add context:** "I run a bakery in Singapore" is better than just "my business"
- **Specify format:** "Give me a table" or "Use bullet points" or "Keep it under 100 words"
- **Give examples:** "Write 3 like this: [example]. Then 3 in a different style."
- **Iterate:** Don't like the first response? Say "Make it more friendly" or "Add more specifics"
- **Stack prompts:** Use the output of one prompt as input to the next
