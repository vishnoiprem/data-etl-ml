# CS-07 (Amazon) — The 3-C conflict: Andy wants AI everywhere, CFO wants cost discipline, AWS lead wants Bedrock to win

> **Mapping:** Original CS-07. Retargeted to Amazon's leadership cast.
> **Goal:** 30 minutes. Mediate a 3-C conflict between Andy (CEO), the CFO (Brian Olsavsky), and the AWS lead (Matt Garman).

## The brief

You are 6 months into the senior data/AI role. Three C-level peers have a conflict that you have to resolve (or escalate). The board is watching.

**The conflict:**
- **Andy Jassy (CEO):** "Ship Bedrock v2, ship Q v2, ship Rufus v2, ship the Anthropic partnership deepening. AI is the strategy."
- **CFO (Brian Olsavsky):** "Cut the burn growth. $100M data + AI platform is the ceiling. Show me the ROI on every AI dollar. Wall Street is watching the $77B capex."
- **AWS lead (Matt Garman):** "Bedrock is the moat. The Anthropic partnership is the moat. All other AI investment is secondary."

Each C is right. Each C is also wrong.

**Your task:** Mediate. Find a path that:
- Ships visible Bedrock + Q + Rufus progress (Andy's win)
- Cuts burn growth or proves ROI (CFO's win)
- Builds the Bedrock + Anthropic moat (AWS lead's win)
- Doesn't break the BU ML teams

## Solution sketch (do not copy — internalize and re-speak)

**Framing (3 min).** "Three legitimate constraints, not three competing priorities."

**Diagnosis (5 min).**
- Andy: AI everywhere because the competitive clock is real (Microsoft + OpenAI, Google + Gemini, Meta + Llama). Hidden cost: shipping unprofitable AI features.
- CFO: cost discipline because Wall Street + $77B capex + retail margin. Hidden cost: cutting the foundation cuts the AI ROI.
- AWS lead: Bedrock + Anthropic because the enterprise-AI flywheel is the AWS engine. Hidden cost: ignoring retail + devices.

**Proposal (10 min).**

| Constraint | Path |
|---|---|
| **Andy — visible AI progress** | Ship Bedrock v2 + Q v2 + Rufus v2 in 12 months; shared eval harness; cap on the cost per AI feature |
| **CFO — burn discipline** | Cut $XM in non-AI capex; defer 1-2 non-AI projects; consolidate 2 internal systems; prove ROI per AI feature |
| **AWS lead — Bedrock + Anthropic** | Build the cross-segment platform team (10 FTE) with 5 platform hires, 3 governance, 1 chief of staff, 1-2 data PMs. Pod leads held accountable in scorecards. |

**Trade-offs (7 min).**
- Andy gives up: open-ended AI cadence → 12-month cap with 3 ships; cost-per-feature cap.
- CFO gives up: $100M ceiling → $XM with the cuts. Some capex growth remains; ROI must prove it.
- AWS lead gives up: 100% Bedrock focus → federated with contract. BU pods retain delivery ownership; the center owns the contract + the feature store.

**Asks (5 min).**
- Andy: back the 12-month cap; defend the cost-per-feature discipline to the board.
- CFO: approve the capex cuts; back the ROI tracking.
- AWS lead: own the cross-segment platform team; sign off on the pod scorecards.

**Q&A — be ready for:**
- "What if Andy wants Bedrock v3 too?" — We go with 1 cross-segment AI on top of the cadence. Scope is the trade-off.
- "What if the capex cuts slip?" — Phased plan; first $XM in 6 months, second $XM in 12.
- "What if a pod lead refuses the scorecard?" — Escalate to the AWS lead. The scorecard is non-negotiable.

## Watch for when you re-listen

- **Did you validate each C first?**
- **Did you reframe as joint problem?**
- **Did you name every trade-off?**
- **Did you end on time?**
