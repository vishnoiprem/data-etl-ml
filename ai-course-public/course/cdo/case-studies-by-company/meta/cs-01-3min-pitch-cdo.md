# CS-01 (Meta) — The 3-min pitch to Alex Schultz on the new CDO mandate

> **Mapping:** Original CS-01. Retargeted to Meta's first CDO Alex Schultz.
> **Goal:** 3 minutes. Pitch how you'd operationalize the new "global AI analytics" CDO mandate.

## The brief

Alex Schultz, Meta's first CDO, has 30 minutes. The first 3 are yours. The room is the heads of analytics across FB, IG, WhatsApp, Threads, plus the MSL (Meta Superintelligence Labs) data lead. The mandate is "global AI analytics" — what does that mean in year 1?

**Context:**
- Schultz was CMO before. Moved July 2026 to be first CDO. Mandate is "global AI analytics."
- 500+ data people embedded across product pods.
- `[NEED SOURCE: 5+ definitions of "engaged user" across FB/IG/WhatsApp/Threads]`
- Andromeda + GEM in production. Llama 4 shipped April 2025. MSL formed June 2025.
- 3.54B DAP; 78,450 headcount; 2025 capex $70-72B; 2026 capex "notably larger."

**Your 3 minutes:**
- Min 1 — what "global AI analytics" means in concrete terms
- Min 2 — the first 90 days
- Min 3 — the ask

## Solution sketch (do not copy — internalize and re-speak)

**Min 1 — what it means (60s).** "Three things. One, a single source of metric truth across FB, IG, WhatsApp, Threads, and MSL. Right now `[CONFIRM: N]` definitions of 'engaged user' — that's a tax on every AI move. Two, a shared feature store and eval harness for the AI products — Andromeda, GEM, Llama, Meta AI. Three, the FinOps discipline on the $70-72B AI capex so the CFO knows the ROI per dollar."

**Min 2 — the 90 days (60s).** "Days 1-30: 1:1 with every analytics lead, every PM lead, every ML lead. Stakeholder map. No irreversible decisions. Days 31-60: contracts on the top 5 cross-product source systems; semantic layer MVP; 1 cross-product metric standardized. Days 61-90: 1 cross-product AI in production with named revenue impact; 1-year roadmap signed off at the AI leadership committee."

**Min 3 — the ask (60s).** "Three things. One, your visible sponsorship of the cross-product metric standard — without it, the product pods will route around. Two, the headcount: I need 10 platform hires, 5 governance, 1 chief of staff. Three, the product leads held accountable for data quality and adoption in their scorecards. The 10x test: would this raise the average of the team in 12 months? Yes."

## Q&A — be ready for

- "Why cross-product and not product-pod-embedded?" — Per Opinion 1. Federation is the destination; Y1 is hub-and-spoke. The cross-product standard is the seam.
- "What about MSL?" — Joint ownership. MSL owns the model layer; the CDO owns the data + feature layer. The seam is the eval harness.
- "What about the $70-72B capex?" — FinOps discipline. The CFO buys the math; the capex has to show ROI per AI project.

## Watch for when you re-listen

- **Did you name the cross-product concrete moves?** "Global AI analytics" is a phrase; "5 contracts, 1 semantic layer, 1 cross-product AI" is a plan.
- **Did you name the 90-day commitment?** Without it, the ask is a wish.
- **Did you use Meta language?** "Product pod," "DAP," "capex," "Andromeda / GEM" — not "data platform."
- **Did you end with the ask?** A pitch without an ask is a status report.
