# CS-07 (Stripe) — The 3-C conflict: Patrick wants speed, the CFO wants cost discipline, Emily wants data org

> **Mapping:** Original CS-07. Retargeted to Stripe's leadership cast.
> **Goal:** 30 minutes. Mediate a 3-C conflict between Patrick (CEO), the CFO, and Emily (Head of Data & AI).

## The brief

You are 6 months into the Head of Data Platform role. Three C-level peers have a conflict that you have to resolve (or escalate). The board is watching.

**The conflict:**
- **Patrick Collison (CEO):** "Ship Radar v4, ship the Issuing ML, ship the Stripe Assistant. The cadence is the strategy."
- **CFO:** "Cut the burn growth. Show me the ROI on every AI dollar. $20M data platform is the ceiling."
- **Emily Sands (Head of Data & AI):** "Build the data org. The mandate is real. I need headcount, budget, and product pod accountability."

Each C is right. Each C is also wrong.

**Your task:** Mediate. Find a path that:
- Ships visible Radar / Issuing / Assistant progress (Patrick's win)
- Cuts burn growth or proves ROI (CFO's win)
- Builds the data org mandate (Emily's win)
- Doesn't break the data team or the BU relationships

## Solution sketch (do not copy — internalize and re-speak)

**Framing (3 min).** "Three legitimate constraints, not three competing priorities."

**Diagnosis (5 min).**
- Patrick: speed because the competitive clock is real (Adyen, PayPal revival, Braintree). Hidden cost: shipping unprofitable ML.
- CFO: cost discipline because IPO-readiness + global growth + Issuing/Treasury capex. Hidden cost: cutting the foundation cuts the ML ROI.
- Emily: data org because the mandate is the only way to scale. Hidden cost: 150+ data people un-coordinated.

**Proposal (10 min).**

| Constraint | Path |
|---|---|
| **Patrick — visible progress** | Ship Radar v4 + Issuing ML v1 + Stripe Assistant v1 in 12 months; shared eval harness; cap on the cost per ML model |
| **CFO — burn discipline** | Cut $XM in non-AI capex; defer 1-2 non-AI Cloud projects; consolidate 2 internal systems; prove ROI per ML model |
| **Emily — data org** | Build the cross-BU platform team (10 FTE) with 5 platform hires, 3 governance, 1 chief of staff, 1-2 data PMs. Pod leads held accountable in scorecards. |

**Trade-offs (7 min).**
- Patrick gives up: open-ended cadence → 12-month cap with 3 ships; cost-per-model cap.
- CFO gives up: $20M ceiling → $XM with the cuts. Some capex growth remains; ROI must prove it.
- Emily gives up: 100% pod accountability → scorecard-based accountability; pod leads retain delivery ownership.

**Asks (5 min).**
- Patrick: back the 12-month cap; defend the cost-per-model discipline to the board.
- CFO: approve the capex cuts; back the ROI tracking.
- Emily: own the cross-BU platform team; sign off on the pod scorecards.

**Q&A — be ready for:**
- "What if Patrick wants Stripe Assistant v2 too?" — We go with 1 cross-BU AI on top of the cadence. Scope is the trade-off.
- "What if the capex cuts slip?" — Phased plan; first $XM in 6 months, second $XM in 12.
- "What if a pod lead refuses the scorecard?" — Escalate to Emily. The scorecard is non-negotiable.

## Watch for when you re-listen

- **Did you validate each C first?**
- **Did you reframe as joint problem?**
- **Did you name every trade-off?**
- **Did you end on time?**
