# Week 8 — Interview Conversion Bootcamp

> **Week 8 · 5 lessons · 1 case study · 4 mock interview rounds**
> **Objective:** Convert the interview loop into an offer. The 4 mocks are the final exam.

## The single principle

> **The interview is won in the preparation, not the interview. The mock round is where you find the gaps; the real round is where you close.**

The first 7 weeks built the substance. Week 8 builds the conversion — how to position yourself, how to simulate the loop, how to find the gaps, and how to close.

The 4 mock rounds are:

1. **Hiring manager** — the loop anchor. 60 min, behavioral + strategic + role-specific.
2. **CTO / architect peer** — the technical peer. 60 min, architecture + trade-offs + judgment.
3. **CEO / CFO** — the executive peer. 45 min, business + ROI + vision.
4. **CDO / board** — the panel. 60 min, end-to-end case + governance + leadership.

By Friday, you will have done 4 full mocks, scored each, and rewritten the weakest 3 answers. That is the conversion.

## The deliverable for the week

A **Conversion Package** — a 5-page document with:

1. The 1-page positioning statement
2. The 20 STAR stories (1 page each, abbreviated)
3. The architecture defense (1 page)
4. The business case + ROI defense (1 page)
5. The 4 mock scores + the 3 rewritten answers

## Scoring for Week 8 (out of 100)

| Competency | Weight | What "great" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Communication and structure | 25% | The room is with you; the answers are crisp |
| Strategic and business thinking | 20% | The CEO nods; the CFO sees the discipline |
| Leadership and executive influence | 20% | The board trusts you; the BU heads sign off |
| Commercial and financial understanding | 15% | The CFO buys the numbers |
| Ownership and measurable impact | 10% | Every answer ends with "and here's how I'd measure it" |
| Technical judgment and architecture | 10% | The CTO respects the choices |

A score below 90 on any mock means the loop is not ready. Re-record.
