# Mock 1: Hiring Manager

> **Week 8 · Mock 1 · 60 min**
> **Goal:** The hiring manager is the loop anchor. This is the highest-weight round.

## The setup

You are interviewing for the Head of Data role at a Series-D retailer (per Week 2's case study). The hiring manager is the current VP of Engineering who will be your peer. They have 4 goals in this round:

1. **Calibrate the bar.** Are you Staff / Director / VP / SVP? They're trying to find the right level.
2. **Test the strategy.** Will you be a strategic peer or an order-taker?
3. **Test the team fit.** Will the existing team rally behind you or revolt?
4. **Test the humility.** Will you be a peer or a competitor for their role?

## The structure (60 min)

| Section | Time | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Opener | 5 min | "Tell me about yourself" / "Walk me through your background" |
| Behavioral | 20 min | 3-4 STAR questions (one per core competency) |
| Strategic | 15 min | "What's your 3-year plan?" / "How would you approach the first 90 days?" |
| Role-specific | 15 min | "Tell me about the hardest [data platform / AI / org] problem you've solved" |
| Closer | 5 min | "Why this role?" / "What questions do you have for me?" |

## The questions you'll get

1. **Opener:** "Walk me through your background. Why are you looking at this role?"
2. **Behavioral — leadership:** "Tell me about a time you had to lead through ambiguity. What did you do?"
3. **Behavioral — failure:** "Tell me about a project that didn't land. Why? What did you change?"
4. **Behavioral — conflict:** "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a peer. How did you handle it?"
5. **Strategic:** "You inherit our data org tomorrow. What's your first 90 days? Your first year?"
6. **Strategic:** "Where do you see the biggest risk in our data and AI strategy?"
7. **Role-specific:** "Tell me about the largest data platform you've built. What was the architecture? What were the trade-offs?"
8. **Role-specific:** "Tell me about a time you had to defend a technology choice to a CTO or board. What was the outcome?"
9. **Closer:** "What questions do you have for me?"

## The scoring rubric (out of 100)

| Competency | Weight | What "great" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic and business thinking | 25% | The 3-year plan is coherent; the 90-day plan is specific |
| Leadership and executive influence | 20% | The stories show decisive leadership; the team-rally moment is real |
| Technical judgment and architecture | 20% | The architecture story is detailed; the trade-offs are named |
| Communication and structure | 15% | The answers are crisp; the STAR frames are clean |
| Ownership and measurable impact | 10% | Every answer ends with a number and a lesson |
| Commercial and financial understanding | 10% | The cost / ROI / capex shows up in the right answers |

A score below 90 means the loop is not ready. Re-mock.

## What the hiring manager is really testing

| What they say | What they mean |
|---|---|
| "Tell me about a time you had to lead through ambiguity." | "Will you be paralyzed when the data is missing?" |
| "Tell me about a project that didn't land." | "Are you self-aware enough to learn from failure?" |
| "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a peer." | "Will you be a team player or a solo operator?" |
| "What's your first 90 days?" | "Will you listen first, or will you walk in with a 90-day plan you wrote last week?" |
| "What's the biggest risk in our strategy?" | "Have you done the homework on us?" |
| "What's the largest platform you've built?" | "Are you big enough for our scale?" |
| "What questions do you have for me?" | "Are you genuinely curious, or are you performing curiosity?" |

## How to prepare

1. **Build your 3-year plan** for the retailer (use Week 2's case study, adapted to the company)
2. **Build your 90-day plan** (use Day 10)
3. **Pick 3 STAR stories** that cover: leadership-ambiguity, failure-learning, conflict-resolution
4. **Pick 1 architecture story** (the largest platform you've built)
5. **Do the homework on the company.** Read the last 2 quarterly reports, the last 6 months of press, the engineering blog, the open job reqs. Have 2-3 specific observations.
6. **Prepare 3 questions for the hiring manager.** The best questions are about the org, the strategy, the culture — not the comp.

## How to run the mock

- 60 min, recorded, with a peer or coach playing the hiring manager
- Use the question list above
- After 60 min, score using the rubric
- Identify the 2 weakest answers
- Rewrite + re-mock

## Sample 2-3 questions to ask the hiring manager

1. "What's the biggest surprise about this role that isn't in the job description?"
2. "What does the data team need most from the next leader that they don't have today?"
3. "Where do you and the CEO disagree on the data and AI strategy?"
4. "What's the one decision the previous leader made that you wish they'd made differently?"

## Re-record until you score ≥ 90

The hiring manager round is the loop anchor. A score below 90 here is a no-offer. Re-mock, rewrite, re-mock.
