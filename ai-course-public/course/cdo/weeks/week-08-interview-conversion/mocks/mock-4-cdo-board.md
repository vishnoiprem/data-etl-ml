# Mock 4: CDO / Board

> **Week 8 · Mock 4 · 60 min**
> **Goal:** The CDO / board panel is the final round. This is the conversion round.

## The setup

This round is run by a panel: the current CDO (if internal) or an external advisor + a board member + the CHRO. The panel is testing:

1. **Can you hold your own in a board-level conversation?**
2. **Can you defend an ambiguous case with no right answer?**
3. **Can you be the executive, not the implementer?**
4. **Can you operate at the altitude the role requires?**

This is the round where "altitude" beats "depth." Lead with the framing, defend in the follow-up, end with the recommendation.

## The structure (60 min)

| Section | Time | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Opener | 5 min | "Tell me about yourself" / "Why this role at this altitude?" |
| End-to-end case | 25 min | A full business case (e.g., Week 8 Case Study 8) — value, cost, recommendation, risks |
| Governance & risk | 15 min | "How would you handle [breach / model failure / regulator]?" |
| Leadership & altitude | 10 min | "How do you operate at the board level? What does your cadence look like?" |
| Closer | 5 min | "What would you do in your first 100 days? What questions do you have?" |

## The questions you'll get

1. **Opener:** "Walk us through your background. Why this role? Why at this altitude?"
2. **End-to-end case:** "A potential acquirer has approached us about buying the data business. $XM, 15% of valuation. What do you recommend? Walk us through your reasoning."
3. **End-to-end case follow-up:** "What if the board wants to sell? What if the CEO wants to hold? What if the buyer won't do license-back?"
4. **Governance:** "We're SOC 2 + ISO 27001. We're 18 months from the AI Act. Walk us through your governance posture."
5. **Risk:** "A model in production has produced biased outcomes. Walk us through your first 24 hours."
6. **Altitude:** "How would you present to our board? What's the cadence? What are the 3-5 KPIs?"
7. **Leadership:** "Tell us about a time you had to deliver bad news to a board. What was the outcome?"
8. **Closer:** "What would you do in your first 100 days? What questions do you have for us?"

## The scoring rubric (out of 100)

| Competency | Weight | What "great" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic and business thinking | 30% | The end-to-end case has a reframe, a math, a recommendation, a risk |
| Commercial and financial understanding | 20% | The value / cost math is sound; the range is honest |
| Leadership and executive influence | 20% | The altitude is right; the peer posture is right |
| Governance and risk | 20% | The breach / model-failure response is rehearsed |
| Communication and structure | 10% | 60 min, no overrun, the panel is engaged |

A score below 90 means the loop is not ready. Re-mock.

## What the panel is really testing

| What they say | What they mean |
|---|---|
| "Why this role at this altitude?" | "Can you operate at the board level, or are you still a Director?" |
| "Walk us through the case." | "Can you reframe, math, recommend, risk — in that order?" |
| "What if the board wants to sell?" | "Will you fold to pressure, or will you defend your recommendation?" |
| "Walk us through the governance posture." | "Do you understand the AI Act? Do you have a model registry?" |
| "First 24 hours of a model failure?" | "Have you actually done this, or are you reading the playbook?" |
| "How would you present to our board?" | "Can you write a 10-slide deck? Can you present it?" |
| "Bad news to a board?" | "Are you willing to deliver bad news, or do you hide it?" |
| "First 100 days?" | "Will you listen first, or will you walk in with a 100-day plan you wrote last week?" |

## How to prepare

1. **Rehearse the ambiguous case** (use Case Study 8 from Week 8)
2. **Rehearse the AI Act posture** (use Day 24)
3. **Rehearse the crisis response** (use Day 25)
4. **Build the board deck outline** (use Day 34)
5. **Pick 1 "bad news to a board" story** that is real and decisive
6. **Build your 100-day plan** (use Day 10)

## How to run the mock

- 60 min, recorded, with a peer or coach playing the panel
- Use the question list above
- After 60 min, score using the rubric
- Identify the 2 weakest answers
- Rewrite + re-mock

## The "first 100 days" answer

The CDO who has thought about altitude says:

"Days 1-30: discover and align. 1:1 with every C-level peer, every BU head. Read the last 6 months of board materials. Map the data estate. No irreversible decisions.

Days 31-60: prioritize and prove value. One quick win — month-end close from 5 days to 2 hours. One foundational investment — data contracts on the top 5 source systems. Both signed off at the executive committee by day 60.

Days 61-100: operationalize and scale. The quick win in production with a measurable outcome. The 1-year roadmap signed off at the executive committee. The board reporting cadence set. One signature initiative in flight.

The single most important thing is the executive stakeholder map. By day 30, I need to know the 5 people who will block or unblock me, what each one cares about, what's their relationship to the data team today, and the one thing I'd need from each to succeed."

## Re-record until you score ≥ 90

A score below 90 here is a no-offer. The board panel is the altitude bar. Re-mock, rewrite, re-mock.
