# Mock 3: CEO / CFO

> **Week 8 · Mock 3 · 45 min**
> **Goal:** The CEO and CFO are testing business, ROI, and strategic alignment. This is the executive round.

## The setup

The CEO and CFO are running this round together (often with the CHRO or one board member). The dynamic is:

- **CEO:** strategy, leadership, vision, board narrative
- **CFO:** ROI, cost discipline, financial rigor, risk

You have 45 minutes, with 2-3 questions from each, plus a closer.

## The structure (45 min)

| Section | Time | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| CEO opener | 5 min | "Tell me about yourself" / "Why this role?" |
| CEO strategic | 10 min | "What's your 3-year plan?" / "What's the biggest risk?" |
| CEO leadership | 5 min | "Tell me about a time you led a transformation" |
| CFO business | 10 min | "What's the ROI on your $5M budget?" / "Where can you cut 20%?" |
| CFO discipline | 10 min | "How do you measure data quality?" / "How do you prioritize?" |
| Closer | 5 min | "What do you need from us?" / "What questions do you have?" |

## The questions you'll get

1. **CEO opener:** "Tell me about yourself. Why this role? Why now?"
2. **CEO strategic:** "What's your 3-year plan for data and AI here? Year by year."
3. **CEO risk:** "What's the biggest risk you see in our data and AI strategy? How would you mitigate it?"
4. **CEO leadership:** "Tell me about the largest transformation you've led. What was hard? What did you learn?"
5. **CFO business:** "We have $5M for data and AI. How would you allocate it? What's the ROI?"
6. **CFO cuts:** "Where can you cut 20% without losing capability?"
7. **CFO discipline:** "How do you measure the value of data quality? Give me a number."
8. **CFO prioritization:** "We have 20 data and AI projects. How do you pick the 7? Walk me through the framework."
9. **Closer:** "What do you need from us to succeed? What questions do you have?"

## The scoring rubric (out of 100)

| Competency | Weight | What "great" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial and financial understanding | 30% | The CFO nods at the math; the cuts are credible |
| Strategic and business thinking | 25% | The CEO nods at the plan; the year-by-year is clear |
| Leadership and executive influence | 20% | The transformation story is real; the team-rally moment is authentic |
| Communication and structure | 15% | The answers are crisp; the math is clean |
| Ownership and measurable impact | 10% | Every answer ends with a number and a lesson |

A score below 90 means the loop is not ready. Re-mock.

## What the CEO and CFO are really testing

| What they say | What they mean |
|---|---|
| "Tell me about yourself." | "Can you be concise? Can you tell a story, not a chronology?" |
| "What's your 3-year plan?" | "Can you think in years, not quarters?" |
| "What's the biggest risk?" | "Are you self-aware enough to see the risks we don't see?" |
| "Tell me about a transformation you've led." | "Have you actually done this, or are you reading the books?" |
| "How would you allocate $5M?" | "Can you do math under pressure? Can you make hard cuts?" |
| "Where can you cut 20%?" | "Are you a cost-disciplined operator, or a big-spender?" |
| "How do you measure data quality?" | "Do you have a number, or do you have a poster?" |
| "How do you pick the 7?" | "Do you have a framework, or are you winging it?" |
| "What do you need from us?" | "Are you a peer, or a subordinate?" |

## How to prepare

1. **Rehearse the executive case** (use Day 39 + Case Study 3)
2. **Build your 3-year plan** for the company (use Week 2 + Day 06)
3. **Pick 1 transformation story** that's big and authentic
4. **Prepare the math** — $5M, 7 bets, 13 cuts, $6.5-16M run-rate, 9-15 month payback
5. **Prepare the "what do you need from us" answer** — the 3 asks (sponsorship, budget, accountability)

## How to run the mock

- 45 min, recorded, with a peer or coach playing the CEO and CFO
- Use the question list above
- After 45 min, score using the rubric
- Identify the 2 weakest answers
- Rewrite + re-mock

## The "what do you need from us" answer

The CDO who has thought about the relationship says:

"Three things. One, your visible sponsorship of the data foundation work — the BU heads need to see you and the CFO backing the consolidation of the 3 warehouses to 1; otherwise it slips to 18 months and the AI mandate dies. Two, the $XM Y2 budget approved at the board meeting — I want the number locked, not a target. Three, the BU heads held accountable for data quality and adoption in their scorecards — without that, the platform is a tax, not an asset."

## Re-record until you score ≥ 90

A score below 90 here is a no-offer. The CEO and CFO are the business bar. Re-mock, rewrite, re-mock.
