# Week 5 — Data Governance, Security and Responsible AI

> **Week 5 · 5 lessons · 1 case study**
> **Objective:** Be able to defend the governance, security, and responsible AI posture of an enterprise data platform under regulatory and crisis pressure.

## The single principle

> **Governance is not the team that says "no." Governance is the team that makes "yes" safe, fast, and auditable.**

The CDO who treats governance as compliance loses. The CDO who treats governance as a product (with the business as the customer) wins. The same applies to security and responsible AI.

This week prepares you for the three highest-stakes executive moments:

1. **The regulator** — "show me your data lineage, your access controls, your model registry."
2. **The CISO** — "show me how you'd respond to a breach, a leak, a model failure."
3. **The board** — "show me how you're governing AI for fairness, safety, and trust."

If you can answer all three, you can hold the CDO seat.

## The deliverable for the week

A **Governance, Security and Responsible AI Playbook** — a 5-page document with:

1. The 6 governance pillars (contracts, catalog, lineage, access, retention, audit)
2. The data quality scorecard (per domain, per source)
3. The 5 security controls (IAM, encryption, lineage, audit, key mgmt)
4. The AI governance framework (registry, eval, guardrails, human oversight)
5. The crisis playbook (detection, war-room, comms, post-mortem)
6. The AI Act / GDPR / CCPA compliance posture
