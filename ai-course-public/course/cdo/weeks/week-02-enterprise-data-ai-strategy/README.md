## Deliverable for the week

- A 1-page enterprise data strategy.
- An operating model org diagram.
- A 3-yr strategy deck for a fragmented retailer under AI pressure (Case 2).

## The single principle

> **Data strategy = business decisions to improve + required capabilities + accountable owners + measurable ROI.**

A data strategy that doesn't name a business decision is a technology plan. A technology plan does not get funded by a CEO.
