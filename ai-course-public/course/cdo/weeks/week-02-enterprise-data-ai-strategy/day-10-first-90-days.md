# Lesson 10: First 90 days as Head of Data

> **Week 2 · Day 10 · 90 min**
> **Deliverable:** A 90-day plan you can present to a CEO.

## 🎯 Architect Level
Staff.

## 🧠 Concept

The 90-day plan is the most-asked question in CDO and Head of Data interviews. It also has the most-bad answers. The bad answers share a shape: they describe what the *team* will do. The good answers describe what *you personally* will do.

The three phases:

- **Days 1-30: Discover and align.** Listen. Read. Meet every stakeholder. Build a mental map. You don't make any decisions that aren't reversible.
- **Days 31-60: Prioritize and prove value.** Make the first 1-2 decisions. Pick one quick win and one foundational investment. Get them in front of the executive committee.
- **Days 61-90: Operationalize and scale.** The first decision is in production. The 1-year roadmap is signed off. The team is restructured if needed. The board reporting cadence is set.

The single most important thing in the first 30 days is **executive stakeholder mapping**. You need to know, by day 30:

- Who are the 5 people who will block or unblock you?
- What does each one care about?
- What's their relationship to the data team today?
- What's the one thing you'd need from each of them to succeed?

The second most important thing is to **find the quick win**. A 30-day quick win is not a 30-day AI deployment. It's something like: "month-end close from day 5 to day 2" or "a single source of truth for revenue". It must be visible, measurable, and attributable to your first 30 days.

## 🛠️ Build It

**Spec:** Build a 90-day plan for a Series-D retailer where you're the new Head of Data.

**Acceptance:**
- 3 phases (1-30, 31-60, 61-90)
- 5-7 named actions per phase
- Every action is something you personally do
- 1 quick win named for phase 2
- 1 foundational investment named for phase 2
- 1-year roadmap presented by day 90

**Starter:** Use Story 1's first-90-days arc as the reference.

**Solution (90-day plan, abridged):**

**Days 1-30: Discover and align**
- 1:1 with every C-level peer (CEO, CFO, CTO, COO, CRO)
- 1:1 with every BU head
- Read the last 6 months of board materials
- Read the last 12 months of incident postmortems
- Read the last 2 quarterly business reviews
- Map the data estate end-to-end (sources, pipelines, semantic layer, consumers)
- Build a stakeholder map with 5 blockers and 5 allies
- **No irreversible decisions**

**Days 31-60: Prioritize and prove value**
- Pick the quick win: reduce month-end close from 5 days to 2 hours
- Pick the foundational investment: data contracts with the top 5 source systems
- Get both signed off at the executive committee
- Start delivery on the quick win (4-6 weeks)
- **One measurable outcome by day 60**

**Days 61-90: Operationalize and scale**
- Quick win in production with measurable outcome
- 1-year roadmap signed off at the executive committee
- Org restructure (if needed) communicated
- Board reporting cadence set (quarterly data review)
- **One signature initiative in flight**

## 🏛️ Architect Notes

- **Trade-off:** Listen vs. act. In phase 1, listen. In phase 2, act. The most common failure is to act in phase 1 and discover too late.
- **Capacity / cost:** A 90-day plan costs 0 incremental dollars. It's a calendar of your time, not a budget.
- **When NOT to use:** Don't present a 90-day plan to a board. Present to the CEO and the executive committee. The board sees the 1-year plan.
- **Production checklist:**
  - [ ] 3 phases, 5-7 actions per phase
  - [ ] Every action is something you personally do
  - [ ] 1 quick win named for phase 2
  - [ ] 1 foundational investment named for phase 2
  - [ ] 1-year roadmap presented by day 90
  - [ ] No irreversible decisions in phase 1
