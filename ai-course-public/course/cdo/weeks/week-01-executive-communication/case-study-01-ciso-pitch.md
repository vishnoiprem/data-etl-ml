# Case Study 1: Present your unified data platform transformation to a CEO in three minutes

> **Week 1 · Case Study · 90 min**
> **Deliverable:** A recorded 3-minute pitch, scored against the rubric.

## The brief

You are interviewing for a Head of Data role at a Series-D retail company with $2B revenue. The CEO is a former operator, ex-McKinsey, ex-Shopify. She has 12 minutes between back-to-back meetings and has agreed to give you 3 minutes to explain what you did at your last role.

**She has already read your resume.** She knows the technology. She does not need you to explain Kafka or Delta Lake.

**What she wants to know:**

1. Did you move a number that mattered?
2. Did you do it in a way she can trust?
3. Should she hire you?

You have 3 minutes. You have the same technology stack she does — Databricks, Snowflake, Airflow, dbt. What you have that she doesn't is the *pattern recognition from doing this before*.

## The structure

Use the PDCA structure from Day 2 (Problem → Decision → Action → Impact), compressed to 3 minutes:

| Beat | Time | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 15s | The business problem, framed in dollars or risk |
| Decision | 30s | What you chose to do, and the alternative you rejected |
| Action | 90s | 3-4 specific moves you personally drove |
| Result | 45s | One number, with baseline, with a forward beat |

That's 3 minutes. The opening 15 seconds decide whether the CEO leans in or checks her phone.

## The scoring rubric (out of 100)

| Competency | Weight | What "great" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic and business thinking | 25% | CEO feels the business problem in the first 15 seconds |
| Leadership and executive influence | 20% | The Action beat shows you moved people, not just systems |
| Technical judgment and architecture | 20% | The Decision names the rejected alternative; the reader trusts the trade-off |
| Commercial and financial understanding | 15% | The Result is a number a CFO would write down |
| Communication and structure | 10% | Crisp structure, 0 filler, ends on time |
| Ownership and measurable impact | 10% | The number is yours, with a baseline |

A score below 90 on this case study is a signal that Week 1 is not done. Re-record.

## Acceptance criteria

- 175-190 seconds, no overrun
- 0 filler words
- 0 acronyms in the opening 30 seconds
- One number with a baseline
- A rejected alternative named
- A forward beat (what it unlocked next, or what you'd do differently in hindsight)
- Recorded audio (not just written) — the audio is the deliverable

## Starter

Use Story 1 (Unified Data Platform transformation). The 3-minute version from Day 2 is the template; compress it to fit 3 minutes (Day 2 was 3 minutes by design, so reuse and tighten).

## Solution sketch (do not copy — internalize and re-speak)

- **Hook (15s):** A Series-D CEO can relate to the board-meeting reconciliation problem. *"Three years ago, the board asked for month-end close on day five of the new month. Finance was still re-keying numbers. Five business units, three cloud regions, twelve source systems, four ingestion patterns."*
- **Decision (30s):** Replatform in waves, not refactor; lakehouse not lake. Name the rejected alternative.
- **Action (90s):** 4 moves: metric standardization at the exec committee, data contracts with the top 5 source systems, lakehouse replatform in 4 waves, FinOps practice that cut cloud 40%.
- **Result (45s):** Month-end close 5 days → 2 hours. P1 incidents 4/mo → 1-2/mo. The forward beat: the team learned that the boring 80% of the work is what makes the cool 20% possible.

## What to watch for when you re-listen

- **Did you hedge?** Words like "I think", "perhaps", "kind of", "sort of" — kill them.
- **Did you use filler?** "Um", "uh", "so", "you know" — kill them.
- **Did you overrun?** If yes, your Action beat is too long. Cut the third move.
- **Did you underrun?** If yes, your Hook is too short. The CEO wants the problem to land.
- **Did the Result feel earned?** If the CEO doesn't nod when you say the number, the Action beat didn't build the case.

## Re-record until you score ≥ 90

A CDO who can't deliver a 3-minute CEO pitch is not a CDO. The pitch is the most important 3 minutes in the interview loop.
