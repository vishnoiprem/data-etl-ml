## How to read this

- **Mean / P50**: typical monthly ROI across 1000 random SMB scenarios
- **P05**: worst-case 5% of scenarios (downside). If this is > -1 month of agent cost, you're safe.
- **P95**: best-case 5% (upside). This is the headline number for marketing.
- **% positive**: fraction of scenarios where the agent nets positive ROI
- **% >= 3x payback**: fraction of scenarios where the agent returns >= 3x its cost (the 'good deal' threshold)
