# Lesson 1.1 Script: Welcome and What You'll Achieve

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Goal:** Set expectations, build excitement, get them to start

---

## HOOK (0:00 - 0:30)

"In the next 75 minutes, I'm going to show you exactly how to save 10 or more hours every single week using AI.

And here's the best part: you don't need any technical skills. You don't need to spend money on expensive software. And you don't need to become an AI expert.

By the end of this course, you'll have a complete AI system that handles the repetitive parts of your business — so you can focus on what actually matters."

---

## INTRO (0:30 - 1:30)

"Hi, I'm [Your Name], and I help small business owners like you use AI to work less and earn more.

I created this course because I kept meeting business owners in Singapore, Vietnam, and across Asia who were working 60, 70, 80 hours a week — not because they had to, but because they didn't know how to leverage AI.

I saw them spending hours every day on tasks that AI could do in minutes:
- Writing the same customer emails over and over
- Creating social media posts one by one
- Answering the same questions again and again
- Organizing their thoughts, tasks, and schedules manually

So I distilled everything I teach my private clients into this simple 5-module course."

---

## PROMISE (1:30 - 3:00)

"Here's exactly what you'll be able to do after this course:

**In Module 1**, you'll get a quick win in the next 10 minutes — I'll show you how to use AI to solve a real business problem right now.

**In Module 2**, I'll introduce you to the 3 free tools that will do 90% of the work for you. No paid software needed.

**In Module 3**, you'll learn how to automate your customer service. Imagine if 80% of your customer questions were answered automatically — and better than you could answer them yourself.

**In Module 4**, I'll show you how to create 30 days of marketing content in just 1 hour. Plus how to write email sequences that sell while you sleep.

**In Module 5**, I'll give you a day-by-day action plan. In just 5 hours of work spread over 7 days, you'll have your entire AI system built and running."

---

## HOW TO USE THIS COURSE (3:00 - 4:00)

"Here's how to get the most out of this course:

First, watch it through once all the way. Don't stop to implement. Just get the big picture.

Then, go back and follow the 7-day action plan in Module 5. Do one module per day.

Each module is just 15 minutes, so you can watch during your lunch break or after the kids are in bed.

I've also included a PDF workbook with every module. Print it out or keep it open on your screen — it'll help you implement as you go.

And if you get stuck, post in our private community. I'll be there answering questions personally."

---

## CTA (4:00 - 5:00)

"Alright, ready to get started?

In the next lesson — Lesson 1.2 — I'm going to give you your first quick win. You'll have AI solving a real business problem in the next 10 minutes.

It's going to be fun. Let's go."

**[End of Lesson 1.1]**

---

## Production Notes

**Visual elements to include:**
- Your face on camera (builds trust)
- Screen recording for demos
- Text overlays for key points
- Simple intro/outro animations

**B-roll suggestions:**
- You working on laptop
- Business owners using AI
- Time-lapse of tasks being completed

**Energy level:** High, enthusiastic, confident but not salesy
**Pace:** Slightly faster than normal conversation — keep momentum
**Tone:** Friendly, like talking to a friend over coffee
