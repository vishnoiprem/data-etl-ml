# AI Leader: Gen AI & Agentic AI for Leaders & Founders

> **From AI Strategist to AI Leader — an actionable workshop to deliver measurable ROI with LLMs.** 3 sections, 17 lectures, 3h 48m.
>
> **Not a technical course.** No prerequisites beyond a desire to deliver business impact. This is a briefing: frameworks and decision rubrics, not code.

---

## What this directory is

The **workshop artifacts** from a 17-lecture executive briefing on Gen AI and agentic AI. Five framework templates you fill in and actually use, plus section maps to the lectures.

This is deliberately a different shape from the engineering courses in this repo (`../ai-coder-coding-agents/`, `../ai-builder-n8n/`). Those hand you `workflow.json` and running tests. This one hands you canvases and rubrics — because the deliverable for an AI leader is a decision, not a deploy.

## The three sections

### Section 1 — From AI Strategist (Lectures 1–6, ~90 min)

Establish vocabulary and a repeatable strategy frame.
- **Lectures:** Your Path to Real Impact; Core AI Terms; Frontier vs
  Open-Source; Commercial Applications; Gen AI Strategy Framework; AI Strategy
  for Executives.
- **Artifact:** [Vocabulary](frameworks/00-vocabulary.md) →
  [AI Strategy Canvas](frameworks/01-ai-strategy-canvas.md).
- **Outcome:** You can evaluate any AI proposal with one page of structure and
  reject the ones that can't fill in box 6.

### Section 2 — AI Decision Maker (Lectures 7–10, ~70 min)

The technical choices, framed commercially.
- **Lectures:** RAG and Fine-Tuning; AI Agents vs Workflows; AI
  Decision-Making (cross-functional); Leading AI Decisions (framework).
- **Artifact:** [Technique Selection](frameworks/02-technique-selection.md).
- **Outcome:** You can pick the cheapest technique that clears the bar, and
  defend it to a board that thinks "AI" means one thing.

### Section 3 — AI Leader (Lectures 11–17, ~120 min)

Delivery: teams, roadmaps, ROI, adoption, workforce.
- **Lectures:** Project Ideation; Building AI Teams; Project Roadmaps; AI
  Leadership (adoption); Delivering AI ROI.
- **Artifact:** [Roadmap & ROI](frameworks/03-roadmap-and-roi.md).
- **Outcome:** You can staff it, sequence it, prove the ROI, and get it
  adopted — or kill it on schedule.

## How to use this directory

1. **Read [Vocabulary](frameworks/00-vocabulary.md) first** if any of the terms
   are unfamiliar. It's one page and it's the foundation.
2. **Fill in the Strategy Canvas** for a live project. Use real numbers — a
   canvas with placeholder figures teaches you nothing.
3. **Use the technique table** to challenge "we need an agent for this." It's
   the highest-value 15 minutes in the briefing.
4. **Run the monthly delivery review** at the end of the ROI framework on a
   real project that's already in flight.

## What this directory is NOT

- **Not a course with code.** There are no files to run. That's deliberate —
  the audience is business leaders, and shipping them a Python repo would be a
  category error.
- **Not vendor-neutral marketing copy.** The frameworks include the hard parts
  — kill criteria, adoption failure, workforce positioning — not just the
  upsell.
- **Not a substitute for technical judgement.** These frameworks tell you
  *what question to ask* and *what to refuse*. They don't make the technical
  call for you; they make sure the right people make it, with the right
  questions.
- **Not dated content.** The specific model names and prices go stale within
  months. The *decision structure* — cheapest technique first, agent-vs-workflow,
  kill criteria, adoption-before-features — is durable. That's what these
  artifacts are built around.

## Source / provenance

The course outline for this directory is a published 17-lecture, 3h48m executive
briefing titled *"AI Leader: Generative AI & Agentic AI for Leaders & Founders"*
— *"From AI Strategist to AI Leader."* The source describes it as "not a course
at all: it's a briefing… designed by leaders, for leaders."

**The instructor/creator is not recorded in the source material I was given** —
fill it in here if you know it.

The frameworks here are **authored from the lecture titles and the stated
learning objectives**, plus widely-documented industry practice on AI strategy,
RAG-vs-fine-tune trade-offs, and AI org design. No lecture footage, transcript,
or slides were available while writing these files.

**Practical consequence:** treat these as a strong, opinionated starting point —
a leader's toolkit to adapt — not as the instructor's exact frameworks. The
structure (three roles: Strategist → Decision Maker → Leader) is taken from the
course; the specific canvases and tables are my synthesis. The one thing I'd
hold sacred is the 90-day-proof-before-scale rule and the kill-criterion
requirement; those reflect the most common way AI programmes fail, regardless of
who teaches them.
