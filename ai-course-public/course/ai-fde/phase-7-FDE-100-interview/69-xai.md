## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Grok scenario
**Q:** *"Customer wants Grok for a custom internal assistant."*
**A (winner):** *"Grok Enterprise API with prompt-engineering, function-calling to internal tools. Gov / SOC2 controls. Eval: 1,000 enterprise Q&As, internal rubric. Pilot: 1 BU, 8 weeks. Rollout with audit + cost budgets."*
**Why it worked:** Right control + rollout.

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Grok scenario
**Q:** *"Grok gave a wrong answer in a customer chat."*
**A (loser):** "Apologize."
**What they should have said:** *"Pull the trace, root-cause (retrieval? generation? context?). Disable, write a regression test, retrain the prompt / fine-tune, redeploy. Customer comms: incident report."*
