# Deloitte (AI/Engineering practice)

## Company Snapshot
- **HQ**: NYC
- **Team Size**: ~1,500+ AI engineers
- **Comp**: $140K–$190K; TC $180K–$320K
- **What they deploy**: Agent / copilot deployments for federal + commercial
- **Travel**: 60–90%

## Interview Process
1. Recruiter
2. Case + coding
3. Loop — case, scenario

## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Deloitte case
**Q:** *"Federal client wants AI for document review."*
**A (winner):** *"Discovery: 100K docs in agency's DMS. Pilot on FOIA review with retrieval-augmented classification. FedRAMP ATO on GovCloud. Compliance: Privacy Act, NIST. Pilot 1 office, 6 months. Eval: % reviewed automatically, accuracy."*
**Why it worked:** Right federal compliance + concrete.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Federal case
**Q:** *"Agency worried about FOIA compliance."*
**A (loser):** "We'll be careful."
**What they should have said:** *"Human-in-loop mandatory for any FOIA response. Audit log per redaction. Pre-publish review by agency counsel. Privacy Act + NIST 800-53 controls. Compliance officer sign-off."*
