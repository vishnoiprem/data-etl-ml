## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Slalom case
**Q:** *"Retailer wants AI chatbot for their website."*
**A (winner):** *"Discovery (1 week): top customer questions + handle rates. Pilot: 1 use case (order status) on Azure OpenAI. Eval: deflection, CSAT. Rollout 1 use case/quarter. Compliance: PII handling."*
**Why it worked:** Right structured Slalom arc.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Slalom case
**Q:** *"Customer says AI doesn't fit their stack."*
**A (loser):** "Use the customer's stack."
**What they should have said:** *"Discovery — map their stack, anchor the AI to existing data sources (CRM, OMS), work in their cloud of choice, even if it takes longer. Reduces adoption friction."*
