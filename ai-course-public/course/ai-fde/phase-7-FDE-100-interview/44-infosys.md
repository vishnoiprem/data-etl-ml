## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Topaz scenario
**Q:** *"Bank wants Topaz for customer service automation."*
**A (winner):** *"Topaz + Agentic AI building blocks, integrated with bank's CRM (Salesforce). Knowledge bases curated from policy + past tickets. Tool calls to core banking. Pilot 1 use case (lost card) in 8 weeks. Expand by 1 use case every 2 weeks. Eval per use case: deflection, CSAT."*
**Why it worked:** Infosys-native (Topaz), Salesforce integration, use-case-by-use-case rollout.

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Topaz scenario
**Q:** *"Customer unhappy with bot."*
**A (loser):** "Train more."
**What they should have said:** *"Sample transcripts, categorize failures. Common: misunderstanding intent (intent classifier gap), wrong tool call (descriptions unclear), wrong tone (response style). Each has a different lever."*
