## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Autonomy scenario
**Q:** *"Customer wants AI-assisted target identification."*
**A (winner):** *"Model-In-The-Loop (MITL) — AI proposes hypotheses, human commander decides. CBJR / RMF compliance. Audit trail per decision. Train on synthetic data + red-team sim. ITAR + NIST controls."*
**Why it worked:** Right MITL pattern + ITAR/compliance.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Autonomy scenario
**Q:** *"Customer wants fully autonomous weapon."*
**A (loser):** "Sure."
**What they should have said:** *"DoD policy requires human in the loop for lethal action. We deliver decision-support, not autonomous engagement. This aligns with DoD Directive 3000.09."*
