## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Customer scenario
**Q:** *"Bank wants Wipro to build a GenAI knowledge assistant."*
**A (winner):** *"Discovery week on-site, map 50 most-asked questions. RAG over bank policies (Azure AI Search). Tool calls to core banking APIs for balance / payments. Audit log, RBAC by line of business. Eval: 200 Q&A with banker ratings. Pilot 1 BU in 8 weeks."*
**Why it worked:** Concrete, metrics-driven, named Azure AI Search.

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Customer scenario
**Q:** *"Customer says responses are wrong."*
**A (loser):** "Add more docs."
**What they should have said:** *"Categorize. Likely: doc freshness, conflicting policies, retrieval misses. Add doc versioning + retrieval eval. Bring domain SMEs into the loop."*
