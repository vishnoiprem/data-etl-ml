## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### PwC case
**Q:** *"Insurance client wants GenAI for claims intake."*
**A (winner):** *"PwC framework: opportunity sizing → MVP scoping → pilot. Use OCR + LLM for first-notice-of-loss extraction; human review; compliance (state DOI rules). Pilot 1 line of business, 3 months. Eval: cycle time, accuracy."*
**Why it worked:** Right big-4 framework.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### PwC
**Q:** *"Customer doesn't trust AI outputs."*
**A (loser):** "Show metrics."
**What they should have said:** *"Confidence-score on outputs; human-in-loop for low confidence; explainability per output; user research to find trust gaps; iterate with customer champions."*
