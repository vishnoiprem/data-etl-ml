## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Healthcare scenario
**Q:** *"Life-sciences client wants patient-journey analytics."*

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Healthcare scenario
**Q:** *"Customer wants to identify patients by name."*
**A (loser):** "Sure, let me check if that's allowed."
**What they should have said:** *"No — by HIPAA + de-identification policy. We work at the cohort level with tokens. If they need re-identification, that's a covered entity requirement + BAA + audit trail. I'd engage our compliance team."*
