## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Consulting case
**Q:** *"Bank wants AI FDE for KYC automation."*
**A (winner):** *"Discovery: map current KYC process (touches, controls). Pilot: NLP for adverse-media screening + risk classification. Pilot 1 country, 8 weeks. Add analyst review UI. Eval: false-negative rate, cycle time savings. Compliance: BSA / OFAC."*
**Why it worked:** Right big-4 structured analysis.

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Consulting case
**Q:** *"Customer wants fully automated KYC."*
**A (loser):** "We'll build it."
**What they should have said:** *"KYC requires regulated judgments. We build decision-support with full audit. Final sign-off stays with the analyst. Aligns with FATF guidance."*
