## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Contact-center case
**Q:** *"A bank wants real-time coaching for service reps."*
**A (winner):** *"Cresta Agent Assist + Knowledge + Insights. Real-time transcription via streaming ASR; retrieval over knowledge base + customer's history; suggestions surfaced in rep's UI (<500ms latency budget). Insights: weekly theme mining of calls to surface new knowledge gaps. Eval: A/B test coaching on vs off for 4 weeks; measure CSAT, AHT, FCR."*
**Why it worked:** Cresta-native (Agent Assist / Knowledge / Insights), named KPIs (AHT, FCR).

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Contact-center scenario
**Q:** *"Reps say suggestions are wrong."*
**A (loser):** "Better model."
**What they should have said:** *"Sample 100 wrong suggestions. Categorize — wrong intent? wrong entity? right intent but missing policy update? Often it's knowledge-base rot (docs changed, KB didn't). Fix: KB freshness SLA + rep feedback loop."*
