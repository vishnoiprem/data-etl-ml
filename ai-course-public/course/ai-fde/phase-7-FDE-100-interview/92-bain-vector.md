## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Bain case
**Q:** *"PE portfolio company wants AI-driven due diligence."*
**A (winner):** *"Use public/private data sources, ML for risk signals, anomaly detection. Pilot 1 deal type. Validate against Bain's historic deal data. Quantify value: time saved per deal, missed-risk avoided."*
**Why it worked:** Right Bain framework + Bain data asset.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Bain case
**Q:** *"PE customer worried about hallucinated risk."*
**A (loser):** "Trust the AI."
**What they should have said:** *"Every risk finding has a citation + confidence score; human analyst always reviews before sharing with deal team; regression tests against historical deals."*
