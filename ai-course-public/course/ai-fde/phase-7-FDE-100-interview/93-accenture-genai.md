## Company Snapshot
- **HQ**: Dublin / NYC
- **Team Size**: ~2,000+ AI/Gen-AI engineers globally
- **Comp**: $130K–$180K; TC $170K–$300K
- **What they deploy**: Largest FDE-like practice globally; enterprise copilot builds
- **Travel**: 60–90%

## Interview Process
1. Recruiter
2. Case-style + coding
3. Loop — case ×2, scenario

## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Accenture case
**Q:** *"Bank wants GenAI for call summarization at scale."*
**A (winner):** *"Discover: 10K call transcripts; identify use cases. Pilot: call-summary tool with custom template, integration with bank's QM system. Pilot 1 call center, 3 months. Eval: agent NPS, supervisor time saved."*
**Why it worked:** Right Accenture delivery arc.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Accenture case
**Q:** *"Customer wants AI deployed by Friday."*
**A (loser):** "Sure."
**What they should have said:** *"Realistic timeline: 6 weeks minimum for safe deployment. Offer an off-the-shelf copilot in 1 week as a stopgap; full custom in 6 weeks."*
