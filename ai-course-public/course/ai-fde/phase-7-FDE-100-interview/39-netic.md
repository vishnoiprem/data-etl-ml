## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Service scenario
**Q:** *"Customer wants to automate inbound calls for an HVAC business."*
**A (winner):** *"Voice agent (Vapi / Retell / Netic-native) → intent extraction (book, quote, reschedule, escalate) → tool calls to ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro. Live transfer to human for complex cases. Eval on 200 historical calls: booking accuracy, transfer rate. Pilot 1 location, 30 days."*
**Why it worked:** Netic-native (Vapi/Retell/ServiceTitan), service-industry-aware.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Service scenario
**Q:** *"Voice agent sounds robotic."*
**A (loser):** "Use a better TTS."
**What they should have said:** *"Two tracks: (1) shorter responses (humans don't speak in paragraphs), (2) latency reduction so first audio comes in <500ms. Often latency is the bigger issue than TTS quality."*
