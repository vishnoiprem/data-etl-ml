# FDE/FDSE Interview Prep — 100 Companies Index

This directory contains per-company interview prep files for **99 employers of Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) / Forward Deployed Software Engineers (FDSEs)**, grouped into five tiers.

Each file contains:
- Company snapshot (HQ, team size, comp, what they deploy, travel)
- Interview process (rounds, length, who's on the loop)
- **✅ SUCCESS STORIES** — actual Q&A from candidates who got offers, plus "why it worked"
- **❌ REJECTION STORIES** — actual Q&A from candidates who didn't, plus "why it failed" + the better answer
- **📚 Reading list** — the specific articles/docs/papers to read

## How to Use

1. **Pick 3–5 target companies** from the tier that matches your background. Do not prepare for 10 — the reading is depth-limited to ~2 weeks per company.
2. **Read the company file end-to-end** (snapshot → process → success → rejection → reading list).
3. **For each Q in the success section, practice answering out loud** for 60–90 sec. Record yourself.
4. **For each rejection section, write the better answer in your own words**, then say it. This is the highest-value part of the directory.
5. **Drill the reading list items in the last 2 weeks** before each loop.

### Choosing a tier by background

| Your background | Start with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Data engineering | Tier 4 (fintech/regulated) | Your production instincts are the differentiator there, not ML novelty |
| ML engineering | Tier 1 (frontier labs) | Direct competition on depth; your evals/ops experience is the edge |
| Enterprise architecture | Tier 1 + Tier 3 | Labs for the systems thinking, SIs for volume and safety |
| Solutions / consulting | Tier 1 + Consulting tier | Labs for technical credibility, consulting for customer-facing reps |
| Singapore / APAC | Tier 1 (OpenAI SG) + Tier 3 | OpenAI has an SG FDE role; NTT/Wipro/Infosys have SG delivery |

## Cross-Cutting Themes

1. **Coding + customer scenario + system design** is the universal 3-act structure across all 99 companies.
2. **The tier shapes the interview, not just the company.** Tier 1 asks about model behavior and eval rigor. Tier 3 asks about stakeholder management and delivery milestones. Tier 4 asks about compliance and audit. Tier 5 asks about domain depth.
3. **Rejections cluster around**: skipping root-cause analysis, dismissing customer concerns, not knowing the company's actual products/SKUs, not measuring success, and over-engineering before clarifying requirements.
4. **Wins cluster around**: specific past deployments, named product primitives, week-by-week rollout plans, evaluation-first thinking, root-cause methodology, and stakeholder empathy.
5. **The "what would you actually ship in week 1" question appears in every tier.** Prepare one answer per target company that names their product, their customer, and the first deployable artifact.
