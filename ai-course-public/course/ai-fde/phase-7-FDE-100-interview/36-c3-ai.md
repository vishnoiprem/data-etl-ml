## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### App platform case
**Q:** *"Customer wants C3 AI for predictive maintenance on 50 plants."*
**A (winner):** *"C3 AI Reliability / Inventory app, ingest from OSIsoft PI + SAP, model on top with C3 AI Ex Machina for citizen data scientists. Phase 1: 1 plant, 8 weeks, baseline + 1 failure mode. Phase 2: scale to 10 plants, federated model. Phase 3: 50 plants, with local adapters. MLOps via C3 AI's Model Management. Eval: precision/recall on failure prediction vs current methods."*
**Why it worked:** C3-native (apps, Ex Machina, Model Management), phased rollout.

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
