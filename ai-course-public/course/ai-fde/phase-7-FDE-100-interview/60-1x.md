## ✅ SUCCESS STORIES

### Home scenario
**Q:** *"Customer wants NEO in elder-care."*
**A (winner):** *"NEO with home-specific skills (fetch, remind, log vitals). Strict privacy: no cloud egress of video, on-device processing. Family app with opt-in data sharing. Pilot: 1 home, 2 weeks, with family consent. Compliance: GDPR + medical device regs. Safety: force-limited manipulation, fall-stop."*
**Why it worked:** Addressed privacy + medical compliance + safety.

---

## ❌ REJECTION STORIES
### Home scenario
**Q:** *"Family says robot felt creepy."*
**A (loser):** "Make it cuter."
**What they should have said:** *"Often it's pacing (too eager) or speech timing (interrupts). Tune interaction rhythm. Add 'do not disturb' modes. Train family on its use. Iterate on UX research."*
