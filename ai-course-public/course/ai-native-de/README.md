# AI-Native Data Engineering and Production AI Systems

> **Promise:** Build the production capability to design, defend, and debug retrieval-augmented systems at staff level. From first principles to a working enterprise assistant.

**Audience:** Senior data engineering leaders targeting Meta AI-Native DE, Senior Technical Design, and Google AI/ML infrastructure roles.

**Bar:** Exceptional professional capability — runnable code, defensible tradeoffs, clear written communication. **Not a promise of top-1% status or interview success.** Hiring outcomes depend on factors outside this course.

---

## Start here

1. Read [`SYLLABUS.md`](SYLLABUS.md) — the 18 modules, 21-day schedule, and rubric.
2. Set up the environment with the [Setup commands](SYLLABUS.md#setup-apple-silicon-macos) in the syllabus.
3. Open [`lessons/m01-foundations/L1-ai-native-de-101.ipynb`](lessons/m01-foundations/) and run it top to bottom.
4. After each lesson, do the **mock interview** at the bottom of the lesson doc. Speak your answer out loud.

## What you can do this week

1. **Read the syllabus** (15 min).

3. **Open the M1 notebook** and execute it top to bottom (60 min).
4. **Speak the 90-second interview script** out loud, three times (15 min).
5. **Write down one thing you couldn't explain cleanly** and make that your first flashcard.
