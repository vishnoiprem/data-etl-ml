# 6-Month Scaling Plan - Weeks 13-26 (Months 4-6)

## Overview

**Goal:** Scale from $5-10K/month to $20-40K/month
**Time commitment:** 4 hours/day (28 hours/week)
**Total time:** ~336 hours over 84 days
**Starting position:** 75-150 students, 600+ email list, 1-2 courses, validated offer
**Expected outcome:** 250-500 students, $20-40K monthly revenue, 2,000+ email list, multiple products

**This is where most course creators fail.** They launch and stop. You're going to build systems, hire help, and create leverage.

## Key Success Factors for Months 4-6

### 1. Hire Help Early
- Don't try to do everything yourself
- First hire: VA ($400-600/month)
- Second hire: Video editor ($500-800/month)
- ROI is immediate (frees 15-20 hours/week)

### 2. Build Multiple Revenue Streams
- Don't rely on 1 product
- Add high-ticket offers (cohort, premium, corporate)
- Each stream should be 20-30% of revenue

### 3. Systematize Everything
- Document every process
- Create templates for everything
- Automate what you can
- Batch similar tasks

### 4. Focus on Unit Economics
- Know your CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- Know your LTV (Lifetime Value)
- Target LTV:CAC ratio of 3:1 or better
- Cut anything below 2:1

### 5. Premium Positioning
- Raise prices as you add value
- Move upmarket over time
- Corporate > Premium > Cohort > Main course > Mini
- Don't compete on price

### 6. Build Strategic Partnerships
- Cross-promote with complementary creators
- Joint webinars (both audiences win)
- Affiliate relationships
- Guest appearances

### 7. Corporate Training is Gold
- 1 client = 25-50 course sales
- Recurring (quarterly training)
- Less price-sensitive
- Builds massive credibility
- Referrals from one company to many

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### ❌ Don't Do This

**1. Trying to do everything yourself**
- You'll burn out by Month 3
- Hire help by Month 4 latest

**2. Adding too many products too fast**
- Confuses your audience
- Dilutes your marketing
- Master 1-2 products before adding more

**3. Ignoring data**
- "Feel" is not strategy
- Track everything
- Make decisions based on numbers

**4. Underpricing**
- Raise prices every 90 days
- $47 → $67 → $97 → $197 over 6 months
- Value > price

**5. Neglecting email list**
- Email is still highest ROI channel
- 1,000+ engaged subscribers = $10K/month possible
- 2,000+ = $20K/month possible

**6. Not doing sales calls**
- High-ticket requires sales calls
- Corporate requires sales calls
- Practice, get better, close more

**7. Giving up too soon**
- Month 4-5 is "the dip"
- Most people quit here
- Push through, breakthrough is coming
