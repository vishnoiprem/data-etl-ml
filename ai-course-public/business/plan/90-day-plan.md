# 90-Day Plan - Complete Day-by-Day Roadmap

## Overview

**Goal:** Build profitable AI teaching business from $0 to $3,000-10,000/month
**Time commitment:** 4 hours/day (28 hours/week)
**Total time:** 252 hours over 90 days
**Start with:** $0 budget
**Expected outcome:** 30-100 students, $3K-10K revenue, 500+ email list

## If You Fall Behind

### Common Issues + Solutions

**"I can't find time"**
- Wake up 1 hour earlier
- Block 4 hours on calendar
- Remove distractions (phone, social media)

**"I'm overwhelmed"**
- Focus on ONE task per day
- Use 25-min Pomodoro sessions
- Take breaks

**"No one is buying"**
- Check: Is your offer clear?
- Get on sales calls
- Improve sales page
- Increase marketing

**"Imposter syndrome"**
- Remember: You know more than your students
- Focus on helping, not credentials
- Start before you feel ready
