# 90-Day Plan

## Week 1: Research & Validation
- [ ] Pick niche
- [ ] Interview 10 potential customers
- [ ] Validate demand
- [ ] Set up free accounts (Gumroad, ConvertKit, Carrd)

## Week 2: Course Build
- [ ] Write course outline
- [ ] Script all 5 lessons
- [ ] Set up recording setup

## Week 3: Record & Edit
- [ ] Record 5 video lessons
- [ ] Edit in CapCut
- [ ] Create PDF workbook in Canva

## Week 4: Sales Page & Emails
- [ ] Build sales page
- [ ] Write 5-email sequence
- [ ] Upload course to Gumroad

## Week 5: Pre-Launch
- [ ] Post daily on LinkedIn/TikTok
- [ ] Build lead magnet
- [ ] Email warm list
- [ ] Pitch 10 podcasts

## Week 6: Launch Prep
- [ ] Host free workshop
- [ ] Collect 100+ emails
- [ ] Open waitlist

## Week 7: LAUNCH
- [ ] 5-day challenge
- [ ] Daily emails + posts
- [ ] DM warm leads
- [ ] Target: 10 sales

## Week 8: Optimize
- [ ] Survey customers
- [ ] Collect testimonials
- [ ] Refine course

## Weeks 9-12: Scale
- [ ] 3x weekly content
- [ ] 1 podcast guest/week
- [ ] Test paid ads
- [ ] Build $97 mini-course
- [ ] Target: 30 total sales
