## Target Audience

### Primary

**Small business owners in Singapore, Taiwan, Vietnam**

**Demographics:**
- Age: 28-55
- Business: 1-10 employees
- Revenue: $50K-500K/year
- Location: Urban (Singapore, Taipei, HCMC, Hanoi, Bangkok)
- Tech comfort: Basic (uses email, social media, basic software)

**Psychographics:**
- Working 50-70 hours/week
- Overwhelmed with repetitive tasks
- Curious about AI but doesn't know where to start
- Wants to grow but can't find time
- Budget-conscious
- Values time over money

**Pain points:**
- "I'm drowning in admin work"
- "I have no time to grow my business"
- "I know I should use AI but it's overwhelming"
- "I can't afford to hire anyone"
- "I'm falling behind competitors"

### Secondary

**Freelancers and solopreneurs in the same markets**

**Demographics:**
- Age: 25-45
- Solo or 1-2 people
- Revenue: $20K-200K/year
- Service-based (consultants, coaches, designers, writers)

## Brand Voice

### Personality
- **Friendly** - Like a knowledgeable friend
- **Encouraging** - "You've got this!"
- **Practical** - No fluff, just results
- **Confident** - We know what works
- **Approachable** - No tech snobbery

### Tone
- Conversational, not corporate
- Direct, not preachy
- Optimistic, not hype-y
- Honest, including about limitations

### Language style
- **Use:** "you," "let's," "here's how"
- **Avoid:** "one must," "it is recommended," jargon
- **Sentence length:** Mix of short and medium
- **Contractions:** Always use them (you're, don't, can't)

**Secondary:** #3498DB (Bright Blue)
- Conveys: Innovation, technology, energy

- Conveys: Urgency, importance, action

## Brand Guidelines

### Do's
✅ Use friendly, conversational tone
✅ Show real results with specifics
✅ Include actionable steps
✅ Use "you" and "your" frequently
✅ Be honest about limitations
✅ Celebrate student wins
✅ Use Asian examples and contexts

### Don'ts
❌ Use corporate jargon
❌ Promise "get rich quick"
❌ Make it sound harder than it is
❌ Use Western-only examples
❌ Hype without substance
❌ Be condescending to beginners
❌ Ignore small business owner reality

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### LinkedIn
- Professional but personal
- Long-form posts (500-1500 words)
- Personal stories + tactical tips
- Tag relevant people
- Use 3-5 hashtags max

### TikTok / Reels
- High energy, fast cuts
- Show your face
- 30-60 seconds
- Text overlays for key points
- Trending sounds when relevant

### YouTube
- Educational, how-to style
- 8-15 minute videos
- Strong thumbnails with faces
- SEO-optimized titles
- End with clear CTA

### Email
- Personal, like writing to a friend
- Use first name
- Short paragraphs
- One CTA per email
- P.S. line for important info

### Twitter/X
- Concise, punchy
- Threads for long-form
- Quote-tweet relevant content
- Engage with AI community
- Build in public
