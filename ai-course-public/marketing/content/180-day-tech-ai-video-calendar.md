- [ ] Do 1-2 practice takes

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### Content Pitfalls

❌ **"I'll wait until it's perfect"** — Just start. Day 1 quality > Day 180 delayed.

❌ **"Daily is too much, I'll do weekly"** — Weekly doesn't build the habit or audience.

❌ **"I need to learn more first"** — You learn by teaching. Start now.

❌ **"What if I run out of topics?"** — You won't. There's always something new in AI.

❌ **"My first 10 videos will be bad"** — Yes, and that's the point. Ship anyway.

### Production Pitfalls

❌ **Spending 8 hours per video** — Batch, simplify, delegate editing.

❌ **Perfectionist thumbnails** — A good thumbnail > perfect thumbnail.

❌ **No hook in first 5 seconds** — You lose 70% of viewers immediately.

❌ **Monotone delivery** — Energy matters. Practice enthusiasm.

❌ **Boring visuals** — Code + face + graphics. Mix it up.

### Growth Pitfalls

❌ **"Just make great content"** — That's necessary but not sufficient. SEO + consistency matter.

❌ **Ignoring analytics** — Data tells you what's working.

❌ **Not engaging with comments** — Community = growth.

❌ **No cross-promotion** — One platform = limited reach.

❌ **Giving up before 100 videos** — Algorithm kicks in around 50-100 videos.

## Burnout Prevention (CRITICAL)

### Sustainable Schedule

- **Record in batches** (2 days/week, not daily)
- **Edit in batches** (1 day/week with editor)
- **Post 7 videos/week** (daily for audience)
- **Take 1 week off every quarter**

- **Morning recording** (when energy is high)
- **Afternoon admin** (low-energy tasks)
