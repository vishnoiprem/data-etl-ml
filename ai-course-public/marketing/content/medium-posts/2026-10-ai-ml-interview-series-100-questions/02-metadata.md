## The publishing strategy: one mega-article + 11 sub-articles

**The approach that works on Medium in 2026:**

1. **Publish the series landing page** as the kickoff article (this file's `01-series-overview.md`). This becomes the "table of contents" that links to all 11 sub-articles.
2. **Publish each of the 11 articles on a separate day** (Tue/Wed/Thu morning, 8-10am US time). Spacing them out 1 week apart gives each article its own distribution window.
3. **Cross-link every article back to the series overview** so readers can navigate the full set.
4. **Submit to a curated publication** (Toward Data Science, The Startup, AI Practitioners) for 2-5× more reach.

**The cadence:** 1 article per week for 11 weeks (Nov 2026 - Jan 2027). Or front-load by publishing 2-3/week for 4 weeks (Nov-Dec 2026).
