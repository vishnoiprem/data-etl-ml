## COPYWRITING NOTES

- **Tone:** Direct, no-BS, technical. Developers hate marketing fluff. Be specific.
- **Length:** Long-form. Developers read. Don't be afraid of detail.
- **Specificity wins:** "12 real projects" beats "many projects". "78% got jobs" beats "most students succeed".
- **Social proof matters:** Testimonials, stats, company logos. Use real numbers.
- **Price anchoring:** Show all 3 tiers. The middle one will get the most clicks.
- **Risk reversal:** 30-day refund removes the risk. Mention it 2-3 times.
- **Urgency (ethical):** Cohort has limited spots, next one starts soon. Don't fake scarcity.
