## Production Tips

**Filming:**
- Use natural light (face a window)
- Clean background
- Phone at eye level
- Speak naturally, like talking to a friend
- Energy: high for educational, calm for story

**Editing:**
- Use CapCut (free)
- Add captions (80% watch on mute)
- Use trending sounds (when relevant)
- Keep cuts fast (every 2-3 seconds)
- Add text overlays for key points

**Posting strategy:**
- Post 1-2 videos per day
- Best times: 7-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-9 PM
- Engage with comments in first hour
- Use 3-5 hashtags
- Keep hook under 3 seconds
- Total video: 30-60 seconds
