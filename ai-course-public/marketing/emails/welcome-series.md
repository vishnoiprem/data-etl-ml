Quick check-in: have you tried using AI for your business yet?

If yes, awesome. If not, no judgment — but let's fix a common mistake I see.

**The #1 Mistake: Using AI like Google**

Most people open ChatGPT and type something like:
- "How to do marketing"
- "Best social media tips"
- "Email template"

Then they get generic, useless responses and think AI is overhyped.

**Here's the fix:** Give AI context.

**❌ Bad prompt:** "Write a social media post"

See the difference?

**The formula:** Role + Context + Format + Example

## Email Sequence Notes

**Timing:**
- Email 1: Immediately on purchase
- Email 2: 24 hours later
- Email 3: 3 days later
- Email 4: 5 days later
- Email 5: 7 days later

**After Email 5:**
- Move to weekly newsletter
- Pitch next offer in email 8-10
- Re-engagement if no activity in 30 days

**Segmentation:**
- Tag students who reply → send personal follow-up
- Tag students who complete 100% → pitch advanced course
- Tag students who don't open → re-engagement sequence

**Subject Line Tips:**
- Keep under 50 characters
- Use [First Name] when possible
- Create curiosity or promise benefit
- Test emoji vs no emoji

**From Name:** Use personal name (e.g., "John from AI Business School" not "AI Business School")
