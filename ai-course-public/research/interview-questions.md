# Customer Interview Script & Guide

Complete guide to conducting customer research interviews that validate your course idea.

## Why Customer Interviews Matter

Before building a course, you need to know:
- ✅ Is this a real problem?
- ✅ Are people actively seeking solutions?
- ✅ What have they tried?
- ✅ What would they pay?
- ✅ How do they describe the problem (use their words in marketing)?

**Rule of thumb:** Talk to 10 potential customers before building anything.

---

## Interview Logistics

### Who to Interview
- 10 people who match your target audience
- Mix of demographics (age, business type, location)
- Include 3-5 who've already bought similar courses
- Include 3-5 who haven't (to understand objections)

### How to Find Them
- LinkedIn (search by job title, industry)
- Facebook groups (your niche)
- Your email list
- Twitter/X
- Instagram
- Reddit
- Friends, family, colleagues
- Cold outreach (DM, email)

### Outreach Template

```
Hi [Name],

I'm researching a course I'm thinking about creating on [topic].

Would you have 15-20 minutes for a quick chat? I'd love to learn about your experience with [problem].

No selling. Just research. And I'll send you a free [lead magnet] as a thank you.

[Your name]
```

### Logistics
- **Tool:** Zoom, Google Meet, or phone
- **Length:** 15-20 minutes
- **Recording:** Always ask permission first
- **Note-taking:** Have template ready, or have someone take notes
- **Incentive:** Free lead magnet or $25 gift card

## The 16-Question Interview Script

### Opening (2 minutes)

**1. Tell me about your business/role.**
- Let them talk. Don't interrupt.
- Listen for: business type, size, location, stage

**2. What's a typical week look like for you?**
- Understand their context
- Listen for: tasks, challenges, time spent

---

### Current Situation (5 minutes)

**3. How are you currently handling [topic/problem]?**
- Understand current state
- Listen for: tools, processes, frustrations

**4. What tools or systems are you using?**
- Identify what's working, what's not
- Listen for: satisfaction, alternatives tried

**5. How much time per week do you spend on [problem]?**
- Quantify the pain
- Listen for: hours, impact on business/life

---

### Pain Points (5 minutes)

**6. What's the most frustrating part?**
- Dig deep into emotional pain
- Listen for: language they use (capture verbatim!)

**7. What have you tried that didn't work?**
- Understand failed solutions
- Listen for: why they failed, what was missing

**8. How much is this problem costing you? (time, money, stress)**
- Quantify impact
- Listen for: real numbers, opportunities lost

**9. If this problem got worse, what would happen?**
- Future-pace the problem
- Listen for: fears, consequences

---

### Desired Outcome (5 minutes)

**10. If you had a magic wand, what would the ideal solution look like?**
- Let them dream
- Listen for: features, outcomes, transformation

**11. What would success look like in 30 days?**
- Make it concrete
- Listen for: specific, measurable outcomes

**12. How would you measure that success?**
- Understand their definition of done
- Listen for: metrics, milestones

---

### Buying Behavior (3 minutes)

**13. Have you bought any courses or tools on this topic before?**
- Understand their buying history
- Listen for: what they bought, why, results

**14. What did you like/dislike about them?**
- Learn from competitors
- Listen for: features, delivery, price, support

**15. What would make you buy a course on this? (if they haven't)**
- Understand decision criteria
- Listen for: price, format, credentials, guarantees

**16. If I created a course that [specific outcome] in [timeframe], would you consider buying? At what price?**
- Direct buying intent
- Listen for: yes/no, price sensitivity

---

## Bonus Questions (If Time)

**17. Where do you go to learn about [topic]?**
- Identify marketing channels
- YouTube, blogs, podcasts, groups, etc.

**18. Who do you trust in this space?**
- Find potential partners/influencers
- People they follow, admire

**19. What's your biggest fear about [solution]?**
- Uncover hidden objections
- Listen for: risks, concerns

**20. Is there anything else I should know?**
- Give them space to share
- Often reveals gold

---

## Closing

**Thank them:**
- "This was incredibly helpful. Thank you."

**Set expectations:**
- "I'm planning to launch in [timeframe]. I'll send you early access and a free [lead magnet]."

**Ask for referrals:**
- "Do you know 2-3 other people who might have similar challenges? I'd love to talk to them too."

## Analysis After All Interviews

### Create Summary Spreadsheet

**Columns:**
- Name
- Business type
- Location
- Biggest pain (verbatim)
- Current solution
- Time/money cost
- Willingness to pay
- Likelihood to buy (1-10)
- Notes

### Identify Patterns

**After 5 interviews, look for:**
- Common pain points (mentioned 3+ times)
- Common language (use in marketing)
- Common objections (address in sales page)
- Average willingness to pay
- Typical buyer profile

### Make the Decision

**After 10 interviews, decide:**

✅ **GO** if:
- 7+ people confirm the problem
- Average willingness to pay ≥ your course price
- 3+ people say they'd buy from you
- Clear pain point with no good solution
- You can reach these people

❌ **PIVOT** if:
- Most people don't have the problem
- They're not willing to pay
- Existing solutions are "good enough"
- You can't reach them

🟡 **REFINE** if:
- Mixed signals
- Niche down or broaden
- Adjust price or positioning
- Re-interview different segment

## Pro Tips for Great Interviews

### Before the Interview
- Research them (LinkedIn, website, social)
- Prepare but don't over-script
- Have your questions open
- Test recording/tech

### During the Interview
- Listen 80%, talk 20%
- Ask "why?" 5 times in a row
- Don't sell or pitch
- Take verbatim quotes
- Stay quiet after questions (let them think)
- Don't fill silences
- Follow the energy (if they light up on a topic, dig in)

### After the Interview
- Send thank you within 24 hours
- Send promised resource (lead magnet)
- Add to spreadsheet within 1 hour
- Review recording for missed details
- Note follow-up actions

### Questions to Ask Yourself
- Did I learn something new?
- Did I capture their exact words?
- Am I clear on their problem?
- Do I know what they'd pay?
- Did they offer to help further?

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Leading the witness**
- "Wouldn't you love a course on X?" (Biased)
- ✅ "What would make you buy a course?" (Open)

❌ **Selling instead of listening**
- "Our course has X, Y, Z features" (Salesy)
- ✅ "Tell me more about that" (Curious)

❌ **Asking hypotheticals**
- "Would you pay $X for Y?" (Hypothetical)
- ✅ "What's the most you've paid for [similar]?" (Actual)

❌ **Interviewing the wrong people**
- People who don't match your target audience
- ✅ Interview people who match your ideal student

❌ **Not recording**
- You'll forget details
- ✅ Always ask permission to record

❌ **Stopping at 3 interviews**
- Sample size too small
- ✅ Minimum 7, ideal 10-15

---

## Red Flags (Don't Build the Course)

🚩 **"That's interesting, but I probably wouldn't buy"**
- No urgency, no pain

🚩 **"I can find that on YouTube for free"**
- Don't perceive enough value

🚩 **"I already have a solution that works"**
- No gap in market

🚩 **"I'd buy it if it was $20"**
- Price sensitivity, not enough value

🚩 **"Sounds cool, but I'm too busy"**
- Wrong priority, won't complete

---

## Green Flags (Build the Course!)

✅ **"I've been looking for something exactly like this!"**
- Desperate for solution

✅ **"How much does it cost?"**
- Ready to buy

✅ **"When can I sign up?"**
- High intent

✅ **"I can definitely see myself using this"**
- Visualizes themselves as student

✅ **"I'd pay $X for this easily"**
- Values your solution

✅ **Multiple people describe problem the same way**
- Pattern = real market

---

## After Interviews: Next Steps

### If You Have Green Flags (Build!):
1. Use their exact words in sales page
2. Address their objections in FAQ
3. Create course promise from their desired outcomes
4. Price based on their willingness to pay
5. Give them early access (build advocates)

### If You Have Mixed Signals (Refine!):
1. Identify which segment loved it most
2. Niche down to that segment
3. Adjust positioning/price
4. Re-interview 5 more people in target niche
5. Decide go/no-go

### If You Have Red Flags (Pivot!):
1. Go back to problem selection
2. Talk to different audience
3. Try different problem
4. Validate before building

## When to Do Interviews

### Timeline
- **Best time:** Week 1 of your 90-day plan
- **Schedule:** 2-3 per day, Mon-Fri
- **Time of day:** Morning (9-11 AM) or evening (7-9 PM)
- **Duration:** 15-20 min each

### Frequency
- **Before course creation:** 10 interviews
- **Before launch:** 5 more (beta students)
- **After launch:** 3 per month (ongoing research)

## Final Checklist Before Building

After 10 interviews, you should be able to answer:

✅ Who is my ideal student? (Demographics, psychographics)
✅ What's their #1 pain? (In their words)
✅ What have they tried? (Why it failed)
✅ What outcome do they want? (Specific, measurable)
✅ What will they pay? ($X average)
✅ How can I reach them? (Channels, communities)
✅ Why me? (Your credibility/advantage)
✅ When do they want this? (Urgency)
✅ What objections will they have? (List 3-5)
✅ What format do they prefer? (Video, text, live)

If you can answer all 10, you have a validated course idea.

**Now go build it!** 🚀
