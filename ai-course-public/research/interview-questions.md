# Customer Interview Questions

## Opening (2 min)
1. Tell me about your [business/role]?
2. What's your biggest challenge with [topic] right now?

## Current Situation (5 min)
3. How are you currently handling [task]?
4. What tools are you using?
5. How much time does it take per week?

## Pain Points (5 min)
6. What's the most frustrating part?
7. What have you tried that didn't work?
8. How much is this problem costing you (time/money)?

## Desired Outcome (5 min)
9. If you had a magic wand, what would the ideal solution look like?
10. What would success mean to you in 30 days?
11. How would you measure that success?

## Buying Behavior (3 min)
12. Have you bought any courses/tools on this topic before?
13. What did you like/dislike about them?
14. What would make you buy a course on this?

## Closing (2 min)
15. Is there anything else I should know?
16. Can I follow up with you when I launch?
