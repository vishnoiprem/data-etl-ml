## Gaps in Market

### 1. Asia-Specific Content
- All major AI courses use Western examples
- No one teaching with Asian business context
- No local case studies

### 2. Multi-Language
- Almost all in English
- Vietnamese, Thai, Chinese underserved
- Huge opportunity

### 3. Affordable Pricing
- Most courses $200+
- Asian market needs $47-97 entry points
- Price-sensitive but huge volume

### 4. Local Community
- No Asian-focused AI communities
- English-language communities exclude many
- Major opportunity

### 5. Local Case Studies
- All use US/EU businesses as examples
- Need Asian F&B, retail, services examples
- Relatable = converts
