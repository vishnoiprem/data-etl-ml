## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't use "AI enthusiast" anywhere — it's the #1 turnoff for serious recruiters
- ❌ Don't pin a tweet longer than 290 characters (will get cut off)
- ❌ Don't use more than 3 hashtags
- ❌ Don't ask for a job in a pinned tweet — frame as "available for engagements"
- ❌ Don't paste your rate in DM 1 (give context first)
- ❌ Don't use emojis in the first 3 words (lowers reply rate)

## Metric to track

Pin a tweet → after 7 days check:
- **Impressions** (visibility)
- **Profile visits** (intent signal)
- **DMs received** (lead quality)
- **Followers gained** (network effect)

If Variation A gets 5x profile visits but no DMs → try Variation B (more technical).
If Variation D gets the most DMs → double down on case studies.
If Variation E resonates → rotate in for APAC-focused weeks.
If Variation F engages CN-side → increase Mandarin-friendly content cadence.
