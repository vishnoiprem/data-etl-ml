## ⚠️ Critical issue: your account is protected

Your tweets are **not publicly viewable**. This is a major problem for cold outreach because:
- Recruiters can't find you via X search
- When you DM someone, they can't see your posts to vet you
- AI hiring tweet replies from you are invisible to the original poster's wider audience
- Your network can't engage with your content (which would warm up your account)

**Action item:** Open X → Settings → Privacy & Safety → Posts → **un-check "Protect your posts"**. Then your tweets become public.

## 4-week X warmup plan

### Week 1: profile + signal
- [ ] Unprotect account
- [ ] Update location to HCMC/Vietnam
- [ ] Update bio (above)
- [ ] Pin availability tweet
- [ ] Follow 50-100 AI/data founders, CTOs, recruiters in your target regions
- [ ] Like 10-20 AI/data posts per day
- [ ] Reply (substantively, not just emojis) to 3-5 AI posts per day

### Week 2: network reactivation
- [ ] DM 30-50 of your existing 634 follows (use warm-network-dm.md)
- [ ] Post 1-2 original tweets per day (case studies, lessons, project demos)
- [ ] Engage with 5-10 posts per day

### Week 3: cold outreach
- [ ] DM 50-100 new accounts per day (AI hiring tweets, founders, recruiters)
- [ ] Reply to every "we're hiring" tweet with a useful take + soft pitch
- [ ] Post 2-3 tweets per day

### Week 4: scale
- [ ] 100-200 DMs/day
- [ ] 3-5 tweets per day
- [ ] Start a thread series (e.g., "How we shipped X in Y days")
- [ ] Cross-post to LinkedIn for double reach

## Engagement rules (avoid getting suspended)

- **DM limit:** 50-100/day max. 200+ = risk of suspension.
- **No copy-paste walls** — X detects identical text across many DMs.
- **Vary your DMs.** Use warm-network-dm.md templates but change 2-3 words per message.
- **Engage publicly first** before DMing. Like + reply to their posts for 2-3 days, then DM.
- **Don't DM new follows immediately.** Wait 1-2 days after they follow you back.
