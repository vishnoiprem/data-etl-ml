## Bulk-send protocol

### Day 1-3 (warm up)
- DM 10-20 of your most-engaged follows (people who liked/replied to your posts)
- **No copy-paste — personalize the first 2-3 words** with their name or recent post
- Send 5-10/day max while warming up

### Day 4-7 (volume)
- DM 30-50/day of your 634 follows
- Group by category: founders, recruiters, eng managers, old colleagues
- Track who replies, who ignores, who refers

### Day 8+ (scale)
- DM 50-100/day
- Add: 20-30 cold DMs to AI hiring tweets (the 5 handles from previous scan)
- Add: 10-20 DMs to new follows who post AI/data content

### Total target: 1,000 DMs over 14 days
- **Expected replies:** 100-300 (10-30% reply rate on warm)
- **Expected intros:** 10-30 (10% of replies = intros)
- **Expected contracts:** 1-3 (10% of intros close)

## Reply handling

**If they reply "what do you do?":**
> We staff remote AI/data engineering pods (6-20 senior engineers, Vietnam-based) for US/EU startups and Series A-C companies. RAG/agents/MLOps, Databricks/Snowflake, React+Python+AWS. Async-first, US-quality, Vietnam-rate ($40-70/hr blended). Pod or single eng, your call.

**If they reply "not hiring but might know someone":**
> Awesome — would love an intro if you hear of anything. I'll send my one-pager (services + rates + 2 case studies) so you can forward easily.

**If they reply "what's your rate?":**
> Depends on engagement model: pod ($40-70/hr blended per engineer), single eng ($50-100/hr), or fixed-price project (varies). Founder/principal rate is $80-150/hr. Payoneer + Vietnam LLC.

**If they reply "we just raised / building / hiring":**
> Congrats! Quick 15-min call this week? I can share 2 case studies on [their stack] and a 1-week scoping plan. Drop a time: [Calendly link / your email].
