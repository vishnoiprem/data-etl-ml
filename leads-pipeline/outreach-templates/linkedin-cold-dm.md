## Cadence

- **Day 1-3:** 10-20 DMs to people who liked/commented on your recent posts (warm)
- **Day 4-7:** 30-50 DMs/day, mix of warm + cold
- **Day 8+:** Scale to 50-100/day once reply rate > 5%

## Personalization checklist (every DM)

Replace ONE of these with their name/context:
- Their company name
- Their recent post/tweet/news
- Their specific stack or role
- Their location/region

Never send the template literally. The first 2-3 words MUST be theirs.

## Follow-up sequence (if no reply)

- **Day 3:** Soft bump — "Hey [name] — did the previous note reach you?"
- **Day 7:** Value bump — share a relevant case study link
- **Day 14:** Final — "Closing the loop — happy to send the one-pager if useful"

After 3 touches, move on. Don't spam.
