## DM rules

- **3 lines max.** X is not email.
- **No links in first DM** (X shows them as previews and cuts off).
- **Mirror their tone.** If they wrote casual, you write casual.
- **Reference something specific** (their tweet text, their product, an event they mentioned).
- **Reply within 2h** if they engage — X is real-time.
- **Don't pitch in DM 1.** Open a question or offer something.
