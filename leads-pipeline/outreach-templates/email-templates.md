## General rules

- **3-4 lines max.** No essays. Decision-makers skim.
- **One concrete number per email** (rate, headcount, ramp time).
- **Send 9-11am recipient local time** (your evening in Vietnam = US/EU morning).
- **Personalize one detail** (a recent tweet, product launch, stack callout).
- **No attachments in first email** unless they ask — most platforms want resume uploaded separately.
- **Subject <60 chars.** No emojis. No "Hello," openings.
