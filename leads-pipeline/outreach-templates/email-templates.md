## General rules

- **3-4 lines max body.** No essays. Decision-makers skim.
- **One concrete number per email** (rate, headcount, ramp time, cloud count).
- **Send 9-11am recipient local time** (your evening in Vietnam = US/EU/CN morning).
- **Personalize one detail** (a recent tweet, product launch, stack callout).
- **No attachments in first email** unless they ask.
- **Subject <60 chars.** No emojis. No "Hello," openings.
- **Lead with Avilx, then Prem.** Avilx is the brand, Prem is the principal.
- **Use `avilx.com`, not avilx.io/ai/etc.** — domain is locked.
- **Always include cloud count + Databricks Champion + global delivery** for credibility.
- **China-targeted emails**: include at least one Chinese phrase, mention CNY invoicing + ICP/MLPS support.
