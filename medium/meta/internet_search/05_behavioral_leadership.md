# Behavioral, Ownership and "leadership" questions

Terminology: Meta's own DE prep PDF calls the behavioral interview the **Ownership** interview (30 minutes). No fetched DE-PA source used the phrase "leadership screen"; the closest match is the Ownership round or the separate 30 min behavioral round on Aced. Treat them as the same family of questions.

No Singapore or APAC behavioral reports were found. Region is Other (or not stated) for everything below. Ids B01... are used by `06_by_topic.md`.

## What Meta says it tests (official)

Source: https://d3no4ktch0fdq4.cloudfront.net/public/course/files/Meta_DE_onsite_prep_guide.pdf (undated). "Data engineers need to take initiative and influence their fellow data engineers and XFN partners in their role, so we'll ask questions about your past experiences where you've been able to demonstrate this." Tip 5: "Share why you want to work at Meta" with specific reasons rooted in your experience. Blind zuvy6qgj (E4 offer, Seattle, 2025-04-05) says the round assesses five signals with 1 to 2 questions each, and advises quantified impact stories.
