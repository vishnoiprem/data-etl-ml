# Project Naming

All AcmeCorp projects are assigned a **constellation** codename at intake.
This keeps external comms and dashboards non-leaky, and makes standups more
fun.

## How codenames are assigned

- The PM files an intake ticket with the project description.
- The naming committee assigns the next available constellation in alphabetical
  order.
- Codenames are immutable for the life of the project.

## Current in-flight projects

- **Andromeda** — billing platform rewrite.
- **Cassiopeia** — customer-facing analytics.
- **Lyra** — internal LLM tools (see the `ai-engineering/` portfolio).
- **Orion** — identity and access management cleanup.

## What codenames are not

- Codenames are not load-bearing. Don't encode the codename in URLs, IAM
  policies, or schema names.
- Codenames do not imply priority. The priority is on the roadmap.
