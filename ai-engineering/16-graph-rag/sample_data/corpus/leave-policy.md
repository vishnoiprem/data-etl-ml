# Leave Policy

Beyond PTO, AcmeCorp offers several leave categories. This is the summary;
see HR for the full legal text.

## Parental Leave

- **Primary caregiver**: 16 weeks paid.
- **Secondary caregiver**: 8 weeks paid.
- Leave can be taken any time in the first 12 months after birth or adoption.
- Job protection is unconditional; the role is held for the duration of leave.

## Medical Leave

- Job-protected medical leave per FMLA (up to 12 weeks unpaid in a 12-month
  period).
- Short-term disability may apply; HR coordinates with the insurer.
- For non-FMLA situations, AcmeCorp provides up to 6 weeks unpaid
  job-protected leave, manager-approved.

## Bereavement

- Up to **5 paid days** for an immediate family member.
- Up to **2 paid days** for an extended family member.
- Additional unpaid time is manager-approved.

## Jury Duty

Paid time off for jury duty, no PTO drawdown. Provide the summons to HR.
