# PTO Policy

AcmeCorp offers paid time off (PTO) to all regular full-time employees. The
policy is designed to be simple, generous, and predictable.

## Accrual

- Full-time employees accrue **20 days of PTO per year**.
- Accrual is monthly (1.67 days / month) and begins on **day 1** of employment.
- Part-time employees accrue pro rata based on scheduled hours.

## Carryover and Payout

- Up to **5 days** of unused PTO carry over into the next calendar year.
- Anything above the 5-day carryover cap is **paid out** in the first paycheck
  of February at the employee's then-current rate.
- There is no waiting period to use PTO.

## Requesting Time Off

- Submit requests via the HR portal at least **2 weeks in advance** for blocks
  of 3+ days.
- Same-day or next-day requests are allowed for emergencies, with manager
  approval.
- Manager approval is required for any block of PTO; HR does not approve
  directly.

## Holidays

AcmeCorp observes 11 fixed US holidays plus 2 floating holidays chosen by the
employee. Floating holidays do not count against the PTO balance.
