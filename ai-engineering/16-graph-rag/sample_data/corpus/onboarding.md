# New Hire Onboarding

Welcome to AcmeCorp. This document covers your first 30 / 60 / 90 days.

## Week 1 — Setup

- **Day 1**: Laptop pickup from IT, account provisioning, badge issuance.
- **Day 2**: SSO enrollment, password manager install, Slack + Email creation.
- **Day 3-5**: Team intros, manager 1:1, code/repo access.

For the full day-1 IT checklist, see `it-runbooks.md`.

## Weeks 2-4 — Ramp

- Your manager assigns a **buddy** in week 2.
- You'll shadow 2-3 teammates in your first month.
- You are expected to ship a small, well-scoped change in your first 30 days.

## Days 30-90 — Ownership

- 30-day check-in: how is onboarding going? blockers?
- 60-day check-in: are you ramping into a defined area of ownership?
- 90-day check-in: are you operating at the level of a peer?

## What to read first

Every new hire is expected to read, in order:

1. This document (onboarding.md)
2. `it-runbooks.md` — covers the day-1 setup
3. `expense-policy.md` — you'll file your first expense in week 1
4. `pto-policy.md` — for time-off planning
5. `on-call.md` — even if you're not on-call yet
