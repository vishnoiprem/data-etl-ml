# Expense Policy

AcmeCorp reimburses ordinary, necessary business expenses. This document is
short on purpose — when in doubt, ask your manager.

## §1. Filing

- File expenses within **30 days** of incurring the cost.
- Use the Expensify integration in the expense system. Upload receipts at
  attach time.
- Per-line receipts are required for any item **above $75**.

## §2. Categories

- **Travel**: flights, hotels, ground transport.
- **Meals**: per-diem $75/day domestic, $100/day international.
- **Software**: anything that improves work output, manager-approved.
- **Other**: describe in the notes.

## §3. International Travel

International travel requires **VP-level approval**, in advance. The path is:

1. Submit the request via the expense system at least **14 days** before departure.
2. Your **manager** reviews and approves first.
3. **Finance** reviews second.
4. **VP** provides final approval.

This step is non-negotiable. Any international trip taken without VP approval
will not be reimbursed, and may be a disciplinary issue.

For IT preparation specific to international travel (VPN, hardware key,
encrypted backup), see `it-runbooks.md` → Travel IT Prep.

## §4. Reimbursement Timing

Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle after approval.
