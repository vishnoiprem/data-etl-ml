# Module 17 — Frontier Ideas in AI

## Course Promise

> "Understand where AI research is heading next."

The research frontier: JEPA (predict in embedding space, not pixel space), world models (learn an internal model of the environment), and recursive self-improvement (the systems that improve themselves).
