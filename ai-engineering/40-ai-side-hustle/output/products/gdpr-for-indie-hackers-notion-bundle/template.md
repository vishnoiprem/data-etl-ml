# GDPR For Indie Hackers — Notion Bundle

**The complete GDPR compliance workspace for an indie SaaS, in 15 pre-built Notion databases.** Clone the workspace, customize 6 fields per database, and you have a working GDPR program that closes EU enterprise deals and survives a regulator inquiry. No spreadsheets to build. No templates to design.

---

## What you get

**1. Master Dashboard** — The landing page. Every active sub-processor, every open ROPA gap, every pending data subject request, and a green/red status indicator for all 6 documents. One glance = you know where you stand.

**2. Document Vault** — All 6 GDPR documents pre-written and ready to customize (Privacy Policy, DPA, Data Flow Diagram, ROPA, Sub-processor List, Breach Runbook). Each is a Notion page with: scope, owner, last reviewed date, version. Add your company name + sign-off, done.

**3. Sub-Processor Database** — One row per third-party vendor that touches personal data. Pre-populated with the 12 most common SaaS vendors (AWS, Stripe, OpenAI, etc.) and the DPA status field. Add your own, fill in the gap, move on.

**4. ROPA Database** — Record of Processing Activities. One row per data category (user account, payment, analytics, etc.) with: lawful basis, retention period, security measures, sub-processors. Required by Article 30; pre-structured so you don't have to invent the schema.

**5. Data Subject Request Tracker** — Database for every access, deletion, export, and correction request. Fields: request type, date received, user email, deadline (30 days), status, completion date. Pre-built views: pending (red), completed (green), overdue (orange).

**6. Breach Response Runbook** — Notion page with the 6-step breach response process (Detect → Contain → Assess → Notify → Eradicate → Recover → Post-mortem). Laminated equivalent in the cheat sheet; the Notion version is the live one with editable fields.

**7. Friday GDPR Review** — Templated page with the 8-item weekly checklist. Duplicate every Friday, walk through it, check the boxes. The auditor can pull 52 weeks of these in 5 minutes.

**8. Privacy Policy Generator Output** — Pre-structured Notion page with all 11 sections required by GDPR Articles 13-14. The page is the Privacy Policy; export to HTML or markdown and publish.

**9. DPA Template Database** — Master DPA template plus per-customer addendums. Each customer DPA is a Notion page linked to the sub-processor list and the data flow diagram. One folder, one place to find any DPA.

**10. Data Flow Diagram Workspace** — draw.io diagram embedded in a Notion page. Shows every data flow: where customer data enters, where it's stored, where it's processed, where it leaves, who has access. Click any box to see the database it links to.

**11. Cookie Consent Tracker** — Database of every cookie and tracker in use. Fields: name, purpose, provider, consent category (essential / analytics / marketing), banner status. Required for GDPR cookie consent; auto-flags any non-essential cookie without consent.

**12. Marketing Email Audit** — Database of every email list, signup source, and consent mechanism. Required for GDPR Article 7 (consent). Pre-formatted to flag EU users without double opt-in.

**13. Sub-Processor Change Log** — One row per sub-processor change (added, removed, DPA renewed). Pre-built view shows the last 12 months of changes; some customer contracts require 30-day advance notice.

**14. Annual ROPA Review** — Templated page for the annual ROPA review. Walks through every data category, every lawful basis, every retention period. Pre-formatted to update the review date and identify gaps.

**15. Customer DPA Question Bank** — 30 of the most common DPA questions from EU enterprise customers with the answer an enterprise privacy officer expects to hear. Read this before you sign your first B2B EU contract.

## How to use it

**Day 1 (1 hour):** Duplicate the template workspace into your Notion. You'll have 15 empty databases. The structure is pre-built — you just need to add data.

**Week 1 (4-6 hours):** Fill in the 6 documents. They're pre-written; you add your company name, scope, and owner. Fill in the sub-processor inventory (start with the 12 pre-populated vendors). Draw your data flow diagram.

**Week 2-3:** Fill in the ROPA. Connect the Friday GDPR Review template to your weekly calendar block. Run your first review. Set up the cookie consent tracker by walking through your site and listing every cookie.

**Week 4:** Run your first internal audit. Send your largest EU prospect (or persona) your Privacy Policy, DPA, and data flow diagram. Fix the gaps they flag. By the end of week 4 you have a working GDPR program.

**Day 30:** You can sign a B2B EU contract. The customer's privacy officer opens your Notion workspace, navigates the 15 databases, and finds 80% of what they need without a follow-up email. You close the deal.

## What's NOT included

- **The actual legal review.** The DPA template and Privacy Policy are pre-written, but they're templates, not legal review. If your customers have specific contractual requirements, have a lawyer check.
- **The cookie banner.** The Cookie Consent Tracker is the database. The actual consent banner (Cookiebot, Osano) is a separate product. The tracker helps you audit what's set.
- **The data flow diagram tooling.** The Notion page is the home; the diagram itself is in draw.io or Lucidchart. The Notion page embeds the diagram and links to the related databases.

## Why a Notion bundle, not a PDF

A PDF of "here are the 6 GDPR documents" is what every consultant sells. You print it, file it, never look at it again. A Notion workspace is something you actually use — every Friday for the review, every vendor onboarding for the DPA, every data subject request for the tracker. The audit becomes a 30-minute export instead of a 3-week panic.

The compliance programs that survive 12 months are the ones that live where the work lives. Notion is where you already do everything else. Put compliance there too.
