# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone — regardless of background, identity, or experience level —
to feel safe and welcome contributing to this project.

## Our standards

**Positive behavior:**
- Be respectful. Assume good intent.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the work.

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, insults, or political flamewars.
- Publishing others' private info without permission.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct.

Reporting: open a private issue or contact the maintainer via the email listed
in `Settings → General` on GitHub.
