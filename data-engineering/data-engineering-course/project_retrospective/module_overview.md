# Module 01 — Project Retrospective Questions

> **1 module · 6 lessons · 2 videos · ~4 hours**

The "Project Retrospective" question is the most
under-prepared question in the EM interview loop. By the
end of this module, you will have 3 fully-written,
5-minute retrospectives on real projects in your career —
ready to be pulled into any interview, in any of the 5/3/90
formats.

---

## Module goals

1. **Understand what interviewers test.** Judgment,
   learning, technical depth, self-awareness, and the
   ability to lead a project to a measurable outcome.
2. **Mine your career for PR topics.** 10-15 candidate
   retrospectives from the 5 categories, with at least 3
   written out in full.
3. **Internalize the structure.** CAR + Lessons + What I'd
   Do Differently — 5 minutes, 4 beats, 1 takeaway.
4. **Rehearse at multiple lengths.** The 5/3/90-second
   versions of the same retrospective, and how to cut
   without losing the impact.
5. **Avoid the common pitfalls.** The 8-10 patterns that
   make retrospectives land flat, and the specific
   rewrites that fix them.

---

## Suggested schedule

- **Day 1 (90 min):** Lessons 01, 02, 03. The theory and
  the structure.
- **Day 2 (60 min):** Lesson 04 (read) + Lesson 05 (read
  carefully, twice). Internalize the worked example.
- **Day 3 (60 min):** Lesson 06. Read the pitfalls. Then
  start mining your own candidates.
- **Day 4 (2-3 hours):** Write 3 retrospectives. Use the
  structure from Lesson 03. Use Lesson 06 as your quality
  checklist.
- **Day 5 (1-2 hours):** Rehearse each in 5/3/90-second
  versions. Record yourself. Cut. Iterate.

---

## Deliverables (the artifacts you should ship)

By the end of this module you will have produced:

- [ ] **3 written retrospectives** (each in 5-minute
      format, with numbers, lessons, and a "what I'd do
      differently" beat)
- [ ] **A candidate list** (10-15 PR topics, tagged by
      category and quality)
- [ ] **Rehearsed 5/3/90-second versions** of each
      retrospective (recorded, listened to, cut)
- [ ] **One pitfall self-audit** (your top 3 pitfalls
      and how you'll avoid them in the real interview)

If you ship those 4 things, you have a full PR prep
package. The rest is rehearsal.

---

## What this module assumes

You have 3+ years of engineering work experience. You
have at least one project you can point to where you
made a meaningful decision. If you have <1 year, the
mechanics still apply, but you'll get more value from
the `data-engineering/` foundations first.

You have read or skimmed `behavioral_interviews/` first.
The PR question is a specific variant of the general
"tell me about a project" prompt — the structure here
builds on the CAR / SOAR / STAR mechanics from that
track. If you haven't done that work, do it first.
