## What this lecture covers

- Welcome to *AWS Glue - The Complete Masterclass*.
- The 30-second pitch: AWS Glue is a serverless ETL service. The course covers Glue end-to-end plus the AWS services Glue depends on (S3, IAM, KMS, SNS, CloudFormation).
- What you'll build: 3 production-grade ETL pipelines (batch, streaming, data-quality), 5 CloudFormation templates, and 3 role-play scripts.

## Narration

> "Hi, I'm Prem. Welcome to the course. AWS Glue is one of the most-used ETL services in the cloud. Companies like JPMorgan, Vanguard, Capital One, FINRA — they all run petabyte-scale ETL on Glue every day. By the end of this course, you'll be able to build a complete Glue pipeline from scratch, debug it when it breaks, and deploy it via CloudFormation. We'll also cover Glue Data Quality and Glue DataBrew, which are the no-code and low-code extensions of Glue. Let's get started."

## Key bullets

- Course is hands-on. Every section has at least one lab.
- The downloadable resources (4 files) include the sample data (`city_temperature.csv`), the CloudFormation template, the IAM trust policy, and the Glue Job script.
- The 3 role plays are the synthesis: you'll diagnose a failing Glue Job, debug a streaming job, and pitch Glue Data Quality to a manager.
