Section 1 is what you're in now — the introduction, plus a 30,000-foot view of the AWS services Glue depends on. Section 5 is the heart of the course — Glue Catalog, Crawlers, Jobs, Triggers, Workflows, and **Glue Studio** (the visual drag-and-drop ETL builder). Section 6 is CloudFormation templates. Section 7 is the first pipeline lab. Section 8 is debugging — the 5 most common Glue failures, and the 3 role plays. Section 9 is Glue Streaming. Section 10 is Glue Data Quality. Section 11 is Glue DataBrew. By the end, you'll have built 3 pipelines, deployed 5 CloudFormation templates, run 11 quizzes, and completed 6 assignments."
