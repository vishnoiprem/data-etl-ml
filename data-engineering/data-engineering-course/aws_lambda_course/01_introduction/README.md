# Section 1 — Introduction

Welcome to **AWS Lambda, Python (Boto3) & Serverless — Beginner to Advanced**.
This section is the shortest in the course — about 6 minutes across just two
lectures — but it sets the tone for everything that follows.

**L01 — Course Introduction and Download Content Slides** walks you through
the full course roadmap: how the 16 sections build on each other, the 3
hands-on enterprise use cases (banking JSON pipeline, serverless CRUD API,
Bedrock GenAI), and the 4 downloadable resources. You'll also see a Mermaid
flow of the section arc so you always know where you are.

**L02 — Course Pre-Requisites** covers exactly what you need on your laptop
and in your AWS account before you write your first Lambda function — and
what you do *not* need (no prior Lambda or serverless experience, no Python
background).

By the end of section 1 you should know what the course covers, what you'll
build, and whether your environment is ready. Section 2 dives into Lambda
fundamentals.
