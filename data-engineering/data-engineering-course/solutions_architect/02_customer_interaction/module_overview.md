# Module 02 — Customer Interaction Interviews

> **12 lessons · 7 videos · ~8-10 hours of reading + 4-6 hours of mock practice**

> **Author:** [Prem Vishnoi](https://medium.com/@premvishnoi) · <pvishnoi@avilx.com>

The core of the SA interview. Customer interaction — discovery,
demo, objection handling, whiteboarding — is the round most
engineering candidates under-prepare for and the round that
downlevels more senior candidates than any other.

This module is structured around the 4 most common customer-
interaction rounds:

- **Discovery interviews** (Lessons 02, 05, 06, 07) — the
  5-question framework, pain-point mining, mock transcripts.
- **Demo interviews** (Lessons 03, 08, 10) — live demo vs
  recorded, what to do when things break, the whiteboard demo.
- **Objection handling** (Lessons 04, 09) — the 6-8 most
  common objections, with worked responses.
- **The wraparound skills** (Lessons 01, 11, 12) — active
  listening, the whiteboarding process, follow-up after the
  interview.

If you only have 6 hours, do Lessons 02, 04, 06, 08, and 10
out loud with a friend. If you have 10+ hours, do all 12.

## How to read this module

1. **Lessons 01-05 in one sitting (~3 hours).** This is the
   theory. Read all 5 in order; they build on each other.
2. **Lesson 06 with a friend (~90 min).** Run the mock
   discovery out loud. You play the candidate; your friend
   plays the customer. Record it. Listen back. Compare to
   the analysis in the lesson.
3. **Lesson 07 with a different friend (~90 min).** Same
   pattern, different scenario. The 2 mocks compound.
4. **Lesson 08 with a third friend (~60 min).** The demo
   mock. Practice the 3-phase structure.
5. **Lesson 09 (~60 min).** The objection mock. Practice
   the 30-second and 2-minute responses.
6. **Lesson 10 (~60 min).** The whiteboard demo. Practice
   drawing under pressure.
7. **Lessons 11-12 (~30 min).** Wraparound skills. Read,
   don't rehearse — these are habits you'll build over
   time.
8. **End-of-track exercise:** the discovery script in
   `exercise.md`. By the time you finish this module, you
   should be able to run a 30-minute discovery call
   without notes, on any scenario in your specialty.

## What "passing" this module looks like

By the end of Module 02, you should be able to:

- Run a 30-minute discovery call that surfaces the customer's
  real pain, identifies the decision-maker, and ends with a
  clear next step.
- Run a 15-minute live demo that follows the 3-phase
  structure and handles at least 1 break in the demo
  gracefully.
- Handle the 8 most common objections (price, security,
  integration, "competitor X," "we already use Y," "we need
  time to evaluate," "we don't trust the cloud," "we need
  on-prem") with a 30-second response and a 2-minute
  response.
- Whiteboard a defensible architecture under pressure, with
  a customer playing the role of the skeptical reviewer.

If you can do all 4 things, the customer-interaction round
of the interview is in the bag. Move on to Module 03 and
Module 05.
