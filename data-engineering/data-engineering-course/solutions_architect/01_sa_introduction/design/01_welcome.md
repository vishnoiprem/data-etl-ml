# 01 — Welcome to the SA Interview Course

> **Lesson 1 of 8 — SA Interview Introduction** · ~10 min

A short, honest welcome. By the end of this lesson you should
know who this track is for, the 8-lesson arc of the first
module, and the single decision you'll have made by Sunday
night: **is the SA interview track the right next step for
your career?**

---

## 1. Who this track is for

You are one of three kinds of engineers:

- **A senior engineer interviewing at AWS, GCP, Azure, Salesforce,
  Snowflake, Databricks, Confluent, HashiCorp, MongoDB, or any
  other vendor with a pre-sales SA role.** You've passed the
  hiring bar for the role in principle; you need the SA-specific
  rounds.
- **An engineer considering a move into the SA career track**
  (e.g., a current AWS engineer eyeing the SA Associate path, or
  a current SE at a smaller vendor eyeing a hyperscaler SA role).
  You need the full mental model.
- **An EM, principal engineer, or staff architect interviewing
  for a "technical SA" role at a hyperscaler** — these roles are
  the senior+ version of the SA role, often with 0 reports and a
  larger deal portfolio. You need most of this track, plus the
  executive-communication content in Module 02.

If you're a junior engineer (0-3 years), this track will help
you understand the SA job but the bar is set for senior+. Most
SA Associate programs at AWS require 3+ years of equivalent
experience. Use this track for the *mental model* and apply
when you hit the experience bar.

---

## 2. What this module does

In 8 lessons, you'll build the *decision framework*: enough
information to know (a) whether the SA role is the career you
want, (b) what the interview loop looks like at the companies
you're targeting, and (c) what the first 90 days of preparation
should focus on.

What this module does **not** do:

- It does not teach you how to discover a customer's pain
  (that's Module 02, Lesson 02).
- It does not teach you how to design an architecture under
  pressure (that's Module 03 + cross-link to
  `../system_design/`).
- It does not teach you how to tell an SA-flavored STAR story
  (that's Module 05).

What it *does* do, and does well, is set the stage so those
later modules land. Without Lesson 02's "3 flavors" mental
model, you won't know which of Module 02's mock discovery calls
applies to you. Without Lesson 04's loop walk-through, you won't
know whether the behavioral round (Module 05) or the customer
interaction round (Module 02) is your weakest link.

## 4. The decision you'll have by Sunday

By Lesson 02, you'll have a clear answer to a question most
candidates never ask:

> *"Which of the 3 SA flavors — pre-sales, post-sales, or
> technical SA — do I actually want?"*

This matters because the interview loop and the prep differ
by flavor. A pre-sales SA loop at AWS is heavily weighted
toward discovery, demo, and objection handling (Module 02 in
this track) and *lightly* weighted toward architecture
diagramming (Module 03 + cross-link). A post-sales SA loop at
Salesforce is heavily weighted toward implementation, technical
depth, and customer enablement — closer to a delivery role
than a sales-adjacent role.

Pick the flavor that matches the *kind of work you actually
want to do*, not the flavor with the highest comp. If you
want to be in a customer conversation every day, pre-sales.
If you want to be in a customer implementation every day,
post-sales. If you want to be in a deep technical conversation
with a CTO about cloud architecture, technical SA.

The interview you prep for should match the role you'll
*enjoy* doing.

---

## 5. How to use this module

**If you have 2 hours:** Read Lessons 01, 02, 04. Skip the rest.

**If you have 4 hours:** Read all 8 lessons in order.

**If you have 6+ hours:** Read all 8 lessons, take notes, do
the self-check at the end of each lesson, and write down your
answer to the "which flavor" question from Section 4.

**Do not** skip Lesson 02 even if you think you know what an
SA is. The 3-flavor model is the foundational mental model for
the rest of the track.

---

## 6. What "passing" the module looks like

By the end of this module, you should be able to answer — out
loud, without notes — these 4 questions:

1. **What's the difference between a pre-sales SA, a
   post-sales SA, and a technical SA at a hyperscaler?** (Lesson 02)
2. **What's the SA interview loop at AWS, and how does it
   differ from Google Cloud's?** (Lesson 04)
3. **If I had to summarize the SA mindset in 5 words, what
   would it be?** (Lesson 06 — answer: *start with the
   customer's problem, end with their outcome*)
4. **What's the most common misconception about SAs, and
   why is it wrong?** (Lesson 08 — answer: "SAs don't code,"
   which is wrong because technical SAs in fact code proof-of-
   concepts daily and pre-sales SAs build reference
   architectures for proposals)

If any of those 4 don't have a clean 30-second answer, go back
to the relevant lesson and re-read.

---

## Try it

Set a timer for 90 seconds. Answer, out loud:

> *"Why do I want to interview for an SA role instead of
> a senior engineer or staff engineer role? What about my
> background makes this the right next move?"*

This is the answer you'll give to the "Tell me about yourself"
question on every SA loop, Module 04 walks through the
details. If your 90-second answer is vague ("I want to work
with customers") you'll get downleveled. If it's specific
("I've been the de facto SE on my last 3 deals — I want to
make that the whole job instead of doing it on top of
engineering"), you'll pass.

Write down your answer. Iterate. By the time you reach
Module 05's behavioral mock (Lesson 11), your answer should
be tight enough to deliver in 60 seconds without filler.
