# Module 01 — SA Interview Introduction

> **8 lessons · 6 videos · ~3 hours of reading + 1-2 hours of self-assessment**

> **Author:** [Prem Vishnoi](https://medium.com/@premvishnoi) · <prem.vishnoi@example.com>

The on-ramp to the Solutions Architect role as a career. In 8
lessons, you get the entire mental model: what an SA actually
is, the 3 flavors of the role, the interview loop, the AWS SA
path specifically, the customer-first mindset, compensation, and
the common misconceptions that send good candidates home with a
"thanks but no thanks."

If you only have one weekend, do Lessons 01-04. By Sunday night
you'll know whether this is a career you want to pursue or one
you've ruled out. Both are good outcomes.

## How to read this module

1. **Lessons 01-02 in one sitting (~45 min).** Set the mental
   model: what SA is, what it isn't, what the flavors look like.
   Don't decide anything yet.
2. **Lesson 03 (~20 min).** Treat this as a story, not a tutorial.
   It will calibrate your expectations for what the role
   actually feels like day-to-day.
3. **Lesson 04 (~25 min).** The interview loop is the spine of
   the rest of the track. Read this once now, then refer back
   to it before each module.
4. **Lesson 05 (~20 min).** If you're targeting AWS specifically,
   read the AWS SA Associate section twice.
5. **Lesson 06 (~25 min).** This is the lesson that defines the
   *kind* of person the SA role rewards. Read it before you
   start practicing mock discovery calls.
6. **Lesson 07 (~15 min).** Comp ranges — calibrate your targets
   before you interview.
7. **Lesson 08 (~15 min).** The misconceptions are also the things
   *interviewers* watch for. Knowing what they're watching for
   is half the battle.
8. **End-of-track exercise:** the discovery script in
   `exercise.md`. This is the deliverable. If you can run it
   out loud without notes, you're ready for Module 02.
