# Module 06 — Interview Tips & Frameworks

> **9 lessons · 9 videos · ~3-4 hours of reading + 2-3 hours of practice**

> **Author:** [Prem Vishnoi](https://medium.com/@premvishnoi) · <prem.vishnoi@example.com>

The closing module. The 9 lessons that tie together the
customer-interaction work from Module 02, the technical
content from Module 03, and the behavioral stories from
Module 05. Two sub-modules:

- **6a. Tips (7 lessons):** the practical execution —
  pausing, tradeoffs, checking in, phone interviews,
  getting unstuck, the 24-hour checklist, the whiteboard.
- **6b. Frameworks (2 lessons):** the SA-specific answer
  frameworks — the 4 C's and PREP.

## How to read this module

1. **Lessons 01-04 (~2 hours).** The 4 most-practiced
   tips. Read in order; they build on each other.
2. **Lesson 05 (~30 min).** When your mind goes blank.
   Read this even if you don't think you need it.
3. **Lesson 06 (~20 min).** The 24-hour checklist.
   Read this 24 hours before your interview.
4. **Lesson 07 (~30 min).** The whiteboard rules. Read
   this once; refer back before any whiteboard round.
5. **Lesson 08 (~30 min).** The 4 C's framework. This
   is the *architecture* framing; practice it on 2-3
   scenarios.
6. **Lesson 09 (~20 min).** The PREP framework. This
   is the *answer* framing; practice it on 2-3
   behavioral questions.

By the time you finish this module, you should have
*all the meta-skills* layered on top of the technical,
customer, and behavioral content from Modules 02-05.

---

## What "passing" this module looks like

By the end of Module 06, you should be able to:

- Pause deliberately in 5 specific places in a
  customer-interaction round.
- Articulate tradeoffs using the 4-part structure.
- Check in with the interviewer using the 3 standard
  check-in questions.
- Deliver clean answers on phone-only interviews.
- Recover from a mind-blank using 1 of 3 moves.
- Run the 24-hour checklist before any interview.
- Use the whiteboard with organization, labels, and
  cleanup.
- Apply the 4 C's to any architecture question.
- Apply PREP to any behavioral answer.

If you can do all 9, you have the *meta-skills*
layered on top of the *content* from Modules 02-05.
The interview rounds become *conversations*,
not performances.
