# Module 05 — Behavioral Interviews for SAs

> **11 lessons · 8 videos · ~6-8 hours of reading + 4-6 hours of story mining + 2-3 hours of mock practice**

> **Author:** [Prem Vishnoi](https://medium.com/@premvishnoi) · <prem.vishnoi@example.com>

The behavioral round for SA interviews, calibrated for the
*customer-facing* signals that SA interviewers watch for.
This module builds on `../behavioral_interviews/` (the
general DE behavioral track) and adds the SA-specific
layer on top.

---

## The 3 differences from the general behavioral track

| Dimension | General DE behavioral | SA behavioral |
|---|---|---|
| **Protagonist of stories** | Your team, your manager | **Your customer** (or external stakeholder) |
| **Signals tested** | Engineering judgment, technical leadership, cross-functional collaboration | **Customer focus, deal influence, customer outcomes** |
| **Failure mode** | Internal-engineering stories | **Customer-isolated stories** (no customer mentioned) |

The senior SA move is to have *every* STAR story
feature a customer or external stakeholder as the
protagonist. Internal-engineering stories get
downleveled; customer-flavored stories pass.

## How to read this module

1. **Lesson 01 (~15 min).** The 12 most-asked SA
   behavioral questions. Read once; come back to it.
2. **Lesson 02 (~30 min).** The 5 story categories and
   the worksheet. Do the worksheet — this is the
   highest-leverage exercise in the module.
3. **Lesson 03 (~20 min).** The STAR refresher tailored
   to SA. Internalize the customer-as-protagonist rule.
4. **Lessons 04-10 (~3 hours).** 7 worked STAR stories.
   Read each, then write your own version of each,
   pulling from your own experience.
5. **Lesson 11 (~30 min).** The full mock. Run it with
   a friend playing the Sr. SA at Salesforce.
6. **End-of-track exercise:** the story-bank deliverable
   in `exercise.md`. By the end of this module, you
   should have 8-10 customer-flavored STAR stories,
   each with a 90-second version, ready to deploy.

---

## What "passing" this module looks like

By the end of Module 05, you should be able to:

- Tell 8-10 customer-flavored STAR stories, each
  90-120 seconds long, with specific impact numbers.
- Adapt each story to multiple questions (one story can
  answer 2-3 different behavioral questions).
- Avoid the 5 most common SA behavioral failure modes
  (internal-engineering stories, no customer named,
  no quantified outcome, blaming others, no reflection).
- Run a full mock behavioral round with a friend,
  landing 4-5 clean answers in 60 minutes.

If you can do all 4, the behavioral round of the
interview is in the bag.
