# Cross-Module Exercise: Build Your Story Bank and Run a Mock Loop

> **The single exercise that ties the whole course together.**
> Plan for 8-12 hours over 1-2 weeks.

This is the exercise that produces the artifact you'll
take into every real interview for the next 5 years. Do
it once, do it well.

---

## 1. The goal

By the end of this exercise you will have:

- A written **story bank** of 10-15 STAR stories
  (Module 03)
- A **60-second self-introduction** polished and timed
  (Module 04 Lesson 01)
- **5 polished reverse-interview questions** ready to
  deploy (Module 05 Lesson 04)
- A **full mock loop** (5-6 questions) recorded and
  self-critiqued
- A **2-week prep schedule** for any real interview

---

## 2. The exercise, step by step

### Step 1: Story mining (3-4 hours)

Block out 3-4 hours in a single session, or split into
2 sessions. Use the workshop in
`05_workshops/01_story_mining.md`.

- **Pass 1 (60-90 min):** Brain dump. Set a timer. Write
  for 60 minutes. Don't filter. Target 30-50 raw items.
- **Pass 2 (60 min):** Categorize. Tag each item with
  category, senior level, number, quality.
- **Pass 3 (90-120 min):** Write the top 10-15 stories in
  full, using the format from the workshop. Each story
  should be 90-120 seconds when spoken.

**Output:** A 10-15 story story bank, written out, with
each story passing the 5-question "so what" test from
`02_theory/06_so_what_test.md` and the 5-bar checklist
from `01_fast_track/design/03_avoiding_downleveling.md`.

### Step 2: Number-finding pass (1-2 hours)

For every story in your bank that doesn't have a specific
number, find one (or estimate one). Use the workshop in
`05_workshops/02_quantifying_impact.md` as your guide.

**Output:** Every story in your bank has at least one
specific number.

### Step 3: Tightening pass (1-2 hours)

For every story in your bank, time it. If it's over 2
minutes, apply the 4 cuts from
`05_workshops/03_tightening_delivery.md`. Re-time. Re-cut
until every story is 90-120 seconds.

**Output:** Every story in your bank speaks in 90-120
seconds.

### Step 4: 60-second introduction (1-2 hours)

Write your 60-second intro using the 4-part structure
from `04_practice/design/01_60_second_intro.md`. Time it.
Record yourself. Listen back. Rewrite. Repeat until it's
clean.

**Output:** A 60-second intro that you can deliver
without notes, in under 65 seconds, that doesn't sound
rehearsed.

### Step 5: Reverse-interview questions (30 min)

Pick 5 reverse-interview questions from
`05_workshops/04_reverse_interview.md`. Write them down.
Rehearse them so you can deploy them without notes.

**Output:** A list of 5 pre-loaded reverse-interview
questions.

### Step 6: Run a mock loop (2-3 hours)

Find a friend (ideally someone more senior than you).
Run a 45-minute mock interview. Use the questions from
Module 04 (any of the 4 mock interviews works). Record
it (with permission).

After the mock, listen back. Write down:

- 3 things that worked
- 3 things that didn't work
- 1 specific change you'll make to each story that
  didn't work

**Output:** A recording of a full mock, a self-critique,
and a list of specific changes to make.

### Step 7: Iterate (1-2 hours)

Take the changes from Step 6. Apply them to the affected
stories. Re-time. Re-record just the changed stories.
Compare.

**Output:** Updated stories that are demonstrably better
than the v1 versions.

### Step 8: Build your 2-week prep schedule (30 min)

Using the structure from
`01_fast_track/module_overview.md`, build a 2-week
prep schedule for any upcoming real interview. The
schedule should include:

- Daily story-telling practice (15-20 min)
- 2-3 mock interviews with friends
- Final week: re-read the story bank, re-rehearse the
  60-second intro, re-load the 5 reverse-interview
  questions

**Output:** A 2-week schedule you can actually follow.

## 3. Total time

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| 1. Story mining | 3-4 hours |
| 2. Number-finding | 1-2 hours |
| 3. Tightening | 1-2 hours |
| 4. 60-second intro | 1-2 hours |
| 5. Reverse-interview | 30 min |
| 6. Mock loop | 2-3 hours |
| 7. Iterate | 1-2 hours |
| 8. Build schedule | 30 min |
| **Total** | **10-15 hours** |

Spread over 1-2 weeks, this is roughly 1-2 hours per
day. Doable alongside a full-time job.

---

## 4. The artifact

By the end, you'll have a single folder (or doc, or
Notion page) containing:

- `stories/` — 10-15 stories, each 90-120 sec when
  spoken, with numbers
- `intro.md` — your 60-second intro
- `reverse_interview.md` — your 5 pre-loaded questions
- `mock_1_recording.m4a` — your first full mock
- `mock_1_critique.md` — your self-critique
- `prep_schedule.md` — your 2-week prep schedule
- `changelog.md` — what changed between v1 and v2 of
  each story

This folder is what you take into every real interview.
Skim the titles the morning of. Trust the bank.

---

## 5. The compounding

The first time you do this exercise, the bank is v1.
After your first real interview, you update it to v2.
After your third, v3. The bank compounds.

Senior engineers who've been at this for years have
banks with 30+ stories, all tagged, all measured, all
polished. The first time you build a bank, you're at
version 1. By version 5, you'll be able to walk into any
interview and pull the right story in 5 seconds.

Start now.

## Try it

If you have 2-3 hours this weekend, do Step 1 only. If

The exercise is the course. Everything else is setup.
