# 01 — Building a Personal Story Inventory

> **Lesson 1 of 6 — Story Bank** · ~15 min

The end-to-end process for mining 10-15 stories from your career.
Do this once, well, and you'll have interview material for the
next 5 years.

---

## 1. Why 10-15

A 6-question round covers 4-5 distinct question categories
(see `02_theory/02_question_taxonomy.md`). If you have 10-15
stories, you have:

- 2-3 stories per category (so you can pick the best fit)
- 3-5 "spare" stories for the inevitable question you didn't
  anticipate
- Enough variety to span your career (not all from the last 6
  months)

Most candidates stop at 4-5 stories. That's a coverage problem,
not a quality problem. The senior move is to have *more than
you need*.

---

## 2. The mining process

Total time: 4-6 hours over 1-2 weeks. Do it in 3 passes.

### Pass 1: Brain dump (60-90 min)

Set a 60-minute timer. Open a blank document. Set the title:
"CAREER STORIES." Don't stop writing for the full 60 minutes.
For every project, role, or moment that comes to mind, write:

- 1-line description
- Year(s)
- The specific decision I made
- The outcome (with a number if possible)

Don't filter. Don't edit. Don't worry about whether it's a
"good" story. The point is to get raw material.

You should end this pass with 30-50 items. Most will be small.
Some will be clear stories. A few will be hidden gems you
hadn't thought about in years.

### Pass 2: Categorize and tag (60-90 min)

Take the brain dump. For each item, tag it with:

- **Category** (leadership / conflict / failure / ambiguity /
  technical depth / ownership / helping)
- **Senior level** (E4 / E5 / E6 / E7 — gut feel for what level
  the story demonstrates)
- **Number** (yes / no — does it have a quantifiable outcome?)
- **Quality** (1-5 — your gut feel for the story's strength)

The output is a 2-axis matrix:

```
              E5     E6    E7
Leadership   [3]    [1]   [0]
Conflict     [2]    [1]   [0]
Failure      [2]    [0]   [0]
Ambiguity    [3]    [1]   [0]
Tech depth   [4]    [2]   [1]
Ownership    [2]    [0]   [0]
Helping      [2]    [0]   [0]
```

The matrix tells you where you have coverage and where you
have gaps. The gaps are your prep priorities for the rest of
this module.

### Pass 3: Write the top 10-15 (90-120 min)

Pick the 10-15 stories that have:
- The highest quality score
- The best category coverage (fills gaps)
- At least one number
- The clearest decision

For each, write a 90-120 second spoken version. Use the STAR /
CAR / PAR / SOAR template from `02_theory/01_star_method.md`
as the structure.

The format I recommend for each story in your bank:

```
TITLE: [One-line name, e.g. "Streaming Migration"]
CATEGORY: [Leadership / Conflict / etc]
QUESTION(S) IT ANSWERS: [2-3 specific questions]
RUBRIC ROW: [Ownership / Judgment / etc]
THE STORY (90-120 sec spoken):
[Write it out as you'd say it, in first person, in present
tense where possible]
THE NUMBER: [The 1-2 numbers that anchor the impact]
THE TAKEAWAY: [One sentence, the beat you end on]
FOLLOW-UP I'LL PROBE FIRST: [The follow-up I want them to ask]
```

The "follow-up I'll probe first" field is the senior move.
Every story has multiple angles, and you should be ready for
the interviewer to push on any of them. Pre-deciding which
follow-up you want to talk about lets you steer there.

---

## 3. Quality bar for each story

Every story in the final bank should pass these tests:

- [ ] The 5-question "so what" test from
      `02_theory/06_so_what_test.md` (4+/5)
- [ ] The 5-signal test from `02_theory/04_signal_vs_noise.md`
      (18+/25)
- [ ] The 5-bar checklist from
      `01_fast_track/design/03_avoiding_downleveling.md` (5/5)
- [ ] Speaks in 90-120 seconds when timed
- [ ] Has at least one specific number
- [ ] Doesn't blame anyone
- [ ] Ends on a beat, not a fade

If a story doesn't pass all 7, it's not ready. Either rewrite
it or remove it.

---

## 4. The story-bank doc

Build your bank in a single searchable document. Format doesn't
matter — Notion, Google Doc, spreadsheet, Markdown file. What
matters is:

- **Searchable** by category (so you can pull the right story
  fast)
- **Editable** in real time (so you can refine after every
  interview)
- **Portable** to your phone (so you can skim before an
  interview)
- **Versioned** (so you can see how a story has evolved over
  multiple interviews)

The senior move is to **reread your bank the morning of every
interview**. 5 minutes. Just skimming the titles and the
takeaways. The re-read primes the stories so they're at the
front of your mind when the question lands.

## 5. A worked example: my bank

For concreteness, here's a 10-story bank for a hypothetical
Senior Data Engineer (Sam, from the Module 01 mock):

| # | Title | Category | Rubric row | Number |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Streaming migration | Leadership | Influence, Ownership | 24h→sub-minute, $2.4M partner renewal |
| 2 | Schema negotiation with data science | Leadership | Collaboration, Influence | 4 teams aligned, 0 downstream breakage |
| 3 | Production outage in the recommender | Failure | Growth, Ownership | 47min→6min MTTD, 80% drop in repeat |
| 4 | On-call rotation redesign | Ownership | Initiative, Scope | 2-person pain → team-wide volunteering |
| 5 | Disagreement with senior peer on Kafka | Conflict | Collaboration, Judgment | Hybrid approach shipped, 0 incidents |
| 6 | 1-line mandate → 6-week scoping | Ambiguity | Judgment, Bias for Action | 3 of 4 users had same problem |
| 7 | Late-arriving events bug | Technical depth | Technical depth, Deliver | 2-year ticket cleared, 0 recurrences |
| 8 | Junior engineer onboarding | Helping | Collaboration, Growth | Independent shipping in 6 months |
| 9 | Q3 missed deadline | Failure | Growth, Self-awareness | Within 10% on next 4 projects |
| 10 | Cost-cutting on cloud spend | Ownership | Deliver Results | $180k/year saved, 2 servers reclaimed |

This covers 6 of the 7 question categories (Helping is the
weakest). Each story has at least one number. Each story
demonstrates 1-2 leadership principles.

When Sam walks into an interview, the bank is searchable: any
question that lands, Sam can pull a story in 5-10 seconds.

---

## Try it

Start your brain dump. 60 minutes, no filter, just write.
Don't worry about quality. Just get the raw material.

If 60 minutes feels too long, do 30 minutes and stop. You can
do the second pass tomorrow. The point is to start.
