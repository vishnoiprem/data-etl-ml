# Module 03 — Tactics

> **6 lessons · 3 videos · ~3 hours**

The practical tactics for executing behavioral interviews well: build
your personal story inventory, internalize 5 story categories with
worked templates, and learn how to deploy them under pressure. Tactics
is what you *do* in the room — Theory is what you know.

## How to use this module

1. **Read Lesson 01 first.** It walks through the story-mining
   process end-to-end. The other lessons assume you have a
   working inventory.
2. **Lessons 02-06 are templates, not scripts.** Each lesson
   gives you 3 example stories and a template you can use to
   build your own. Don't copy the example stories — they won't
   fit your career. Use the structure.
3. **Build your bank in a single doc.** Spreadsheet, Notion,
   whatever. The format matters less than having it written
   down somewhere you can search.
4. **Rehearse out loud.** The tactical content here is only
   useful if your delivery matches. See Module 04 for mock
   interviews and Module 05 for delivery practice.
