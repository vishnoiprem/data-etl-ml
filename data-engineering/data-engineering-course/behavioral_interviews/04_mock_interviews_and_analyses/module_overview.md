# Module 04 — Mock Interviews & Analyses

> **5 lessons · 5 videos · ~3 hours of reading + 4-6 hours of practice**

This is where the rubber meets the road. Lessons 02-05 are full mock
interviews at 4 different levels. Read each, take the interview,
then read the analysis.

The 60-second self-introduction in Lesson 01 is the single
most-rehearsed 60 seconds in the entire interview loop. Get it
right.

## How to use this module

1. **Lesson 01 first.** Get the 60-second intro locked before
   anything else.
2. **Read each mock interview like the previous ones in Module 01:**
   cover the answers, take the question, plan your own response,
   then reveal and compare.
3. **Do at least one mock out loud with a friend.** Recording
   yourself is good. Doing it with another human is 10x better.
4. **After each mock, write down 3 things you'll change.** Then
   re-do the mock 1 week later. Compare.
