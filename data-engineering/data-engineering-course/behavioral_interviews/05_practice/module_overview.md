# Module 05 — Practice

> **5 lessons · 3 videos · ~3 hours**

The hands-on practice workshops. Story mining. Quantifying impact
when you don't have numbers. Tightening delivery. The reverse
interview. Books and continued practice.

These are not "read me" lessons. They're "do me" lessons.
Plan to spend at least 60-90 minutes on each one.

## How to use this module

1. **Lesson 01 first.** This is the most important workshop in the
   course. It produces the artifact (your story bank) that the
   rest of the course assumes you have.
2. **Lesson 02 next.** Numbers are the single biggest senior
   signal. This workshop gives you the number even when you
   think you don't have one.
3. **Lesson 03 is the editing pass.** Do it after your bank is
   built, not before.
4. **Lesson 04 is reference material.** Skim once, then come
   back when you're prepping for a specific interview.
5. **Lesson 05 is the long-term continuation plan.** Read it
   last.

## Cross-reference: The Cheatsheet

After completing these workshops, build yourself a one-page
cheatsheet (use the `exercise.md` template in the track root) that
you review in the 30 minutes before every interview. It should
include:

- Your 5 best STAR stories (1 line each)
- 3 quantified impact numbers
- 3 reverse-interview questions you want to ask
- One sentence on what "the senior signal" means for your level

That's the entire behavioral battle in one page. Carry it.
