# 01 — Tips for Acing Technical Coding Interviews

> **Author:** Prem Vishnoi <prem.vishnoi@example.com>

A short list of high-leverage habits, no fluff.

1. **Read the problem twice.** Restate it in your own words before you touch the keyboard.
2. **Write 2-3 example inputs and outputs before you code.** Edge cases become obvious.
3. **State the brute force first, then optimize.** Interviewers want to see the journey.
4. **Talk while you type.** Silence is interpreted as confusion.
5. **Test your solution on the example you wrote in step 2.** Walk it through by hand.
6. **Use real variable names.** `left`, `right`, `mid`, not `l`, `r`, `m`.
7. **Know your complexity.** Always finish with a "this is O(n) time, O(1) extra space".
8. **Don't optimize prematurely.** A correct O(n²) beats a broken O(n log n).
9. **Ask clarifying questions.** "Is the array sorted? Can it be empty? Are there negatives?"
10. **Manage the clock.** If you've been stuck for 5 minutes, say so and ask for a hint.
