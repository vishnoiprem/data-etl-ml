# 12 — Two Pointers

> **Author:** Prem Vishnoi <prem.vishnoi@example.com>

Two indices march through the array, usually from opposite ends or both from the front.

## When to use

- Sorted array + find pair/triplet summing to target
- In-place removal / partition (slow + fast pointer)
- Comparing two strings for palindromes
