# Section 1 — Introduction to EC2

Welcome to the **AWS EC2 + Load Balancing Crash Course**. This is the
shortest section in the course — three lectures, about 14 minutes total —
and it sets the tone for everything that follows. No code is written in
this section; the first hands-on material begins in section 2.

## Lecture map

| L# | Title | Duration | File |

| L01 | Course Overview | 5:00 | [`lecture_scripts/L01_course_overview.md`](lecture_scripts/L01_course_overview.md) |

**L01 — Course Overview** opens the course: what EC2 is at a high level,
the 8 sections at a glance, the 6 working boto3 demos, and the Mermaid
arc showing how the sections build on each other.

**L02 — What You'll Learn** is the formal learning-objectives lecture.
Six concrete outcomes, mapped one-to-one onto sections 2 through 8: EC2
fundamentals, the creation wizard, lifecycle management, pricing, the
three load balancers (NLB, ALB, GWLB), and the 30,000-foot architectural
view.

**L03 — Who This Course Is For** describes the three target personas —
EC2/LB beginners, AWS certification candidates, and practitioners who
work with AWS infrastructure day to day — and the personas the course
is **not** designed for.

## How to read this section

1. Read L01 first. It is the only lecture in the section that you
   strictly need before moving to section 2.
2. Read L02 once you have decided the course is for you. The 6
   learning objectives are referenced from every later section.
3. Read L03 if you want to validate that your background matches the
   intended audience. The self-check at the bottom is a good way to
   gauge your starting point.
4. After L03, take the section quiz in
   [`../quizzes/section_1.md`](../quizzes/section_1.md). The pass bar
   is 5 out of 8. The quiz covers L01 and L02 in roughly equal
   proportion.
5. Then move to section 2 — **EC2 Fundamentals** (L04–L08) — which
   starts the hands-on material.
