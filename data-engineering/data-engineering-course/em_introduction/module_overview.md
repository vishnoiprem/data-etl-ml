# Module 01 — EM Introduction

> **6 lessons · 2 videos · ~3 hours of reading + 1-2 hours of self-assessment**

> **Author:** [Prem Vishnoi](https://medium.com/@premvishnoi) · <prem.vishnoi@example.com>

This is the on-ramp to engineering management as a career. In 6
lessons, you get the entire decision framework: what EM actually
is, the 3 flavors of the role, the trade-offs you'd be making, the
interview loop, the vocabulary, and the 4 career archetypes you
might pick instead.

If you only have one weekend, do Lessons 01-04. By Sunday night
you'll know whether management is a path you want to explore or
one you've just ruled out. Both are good outcomes.

## How to read this module

1. **Lessons 01-02 in one sitting (~45 min).** Set the mental
   model: what EM is, what it isn't, what the trade-off looks
   like. Don't decide anything yet.
2. **Lesson 03 with a pen (~30 min).** Score yourself honestly
   on the 4 "ready" signs and the 3 "not ready" signs. This is
   the lesson that decides whether the rest of the track is
   worth your time.
3. **Lesson 04 (~20 min).** Read this even if you've decided
   *against* management — it's the only way to understand what
   your future manager is being evaluated on.
4. **Lesson 05 as a reference (~20 min skim, then bookmark).**
   You won't memorize the glossary in one read. You'll come back
   to it whenever a term in a JD, a perf doc, or a skip-level
   meeting doesn't ring a bell.
5. **Lesson 06 last (~25 min).** This is the bonus lesson. The
   4 career archetypes matter even if you decide to stay IC —
   they reframe what "IC ladder" means past L6.
6. **End-of-track exercise:** write your "why EM" story in
   `exercise.md`. This is the deliverable. If you can tell it in
   90 seconds and it sounds like you, you're ready to pitch
   the move to your manager. If it doesn't, you have more
   evidence to gather.
