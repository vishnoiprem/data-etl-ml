# Module 01 — How to Get The Interview

> **9 lessons · ~3 hours · 1 artifact**

The "before-the-loop" playbook for senior+ data engineering
candidates. By the end of this module, you will have a working
job-search pipeline: a master resume, two targeted resumes, a
cover letter template, a tracking spreadsheet, and 5+ outreach
messages in flight.

---

## Module goals

1. **Understand the funnel.** Who reads your resume, in what order,
   for how long, and what they're scanning for. Most candidates
   optimize for the wrong reader.
2. **Write a resume that survives.** Beat the ATS, survive the
   6-second recruiter scan, and give the hiring manager a reason
   to take the next 30 minutes.
3. **Customize per role.** Produce targeted resumes for 2 different
   job descriptions, with concrete before/after rewrites.
4. **Get past the application black hole.** Use referrals, internal
   mobility programs, and cold outreach to hiring managers.

---

## Suggested schedule

- **Day 1 (90 min):** Lessons 01, 02, 03, 04. The theory.
- **Day 2 (90 min):** Lesson 05 (read + annotate the sample resume).
  Start drafting your own master resume.
- **Day 3 (90 min):** Lessons 06, 07, 08, 09. Read in one sitting.
- **Day 4 (2-3 hours):** The capstone in `exercise.md`. Produce 2
  targeted resumes + 2 cover letters + 1 tracking spreadsheet.
- **Day 5 (1-2 hours):** Send 5+ outreach messages. Apply to 5+ roles.
  Track in spreadsheet.

---

## Deliverables (the artifacts you should ship)

By the end of this module you will have produced:

- [ ] **Master resume** (1 page, untargeted)
- [ ] **2 targeted resumes** (each < 1 page, each for a specific JD)
- [ ] **2 cover letters** (each < 250 words, each for a specific role)
- [ ] **1 tracking spreadsheet** (10+ open applications)
- [ ] **5+ outreach messages sent** (referrals or cold outbound)
- [ ] **5+ applications in flight** (real, tracked, not spray-and-pray)

If you ship those 6 things, you have a job-search pipeline. The
rest is iteration.

---

## What this module assumes

You have 2+ years of data engineering work experience. You have
something to put on a resume. If you have <1 year, the same
mechanics apply, but you'll get more value from `data-engineering/`
foundations first.

You can read and write Markdown. The artifact in
`artifacts/sample_de_resume.md` is a `.md` file so it's easy to
copy, fork, and adapt. A `.pdf` version of the same is in
`artifacts/sample_de_resume.pdf` (not generated in this version).
