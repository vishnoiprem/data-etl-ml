# Module 02 — Compensation, Leveling & Negotiation

> **4 lessons · ~2 hours · 0 artifacts (it's all scripts and spreadsheets)**

The closing round of the interview loop is when the company decides
what you're worth. Most candidates under-prep this and leave
**5–15% on the table** — sometimes more. This module fixes that.

---

## Why this module

You did the work. You sent the targeted resume (Module 01), passed
the recruiter screen, cleared the technical loop, nailed the system
design, charmed the behavioral. The recruiter calls and says the
obnoxious line: *"So, what are your salary expectations?"*

This is where most senior+ data engineering searches die.

- **73% of recruiters** say candidates don't negotiate at all.
- Of the 27% who do, **most leave $20k–$80k on the table** over a
  4-year vest, because they negotiate the wrong number (the base,
  not the all-in TC).
- **Down-leveling** — being offered L5 when you were running an
  L6 interview loop — happens to ~15% of senior candidates, and
  almost no one knows to push back.

This module is 4 lessons that close the loop:

1. **Leveling** — what each level actually signals, and how to make
   sure you're offered the right one.
2. **Offer anatomy** — what every dollar in the offer means (base,
   RSU, bonus, perks), how it's taxed, vested, and valued.
3. **Negotiation scripts** — the 3 levers, the 5 reframes, and 5
   worked transcripts so you know exactly what to say.
4. **Comp benchmarking** — where the numbers actually come from,
   and how to build a 6-month / 12-month / 4-year TC comparison.

By the end you will:

- Know the **level** you should be interviewing at and how to defend
  it in a leveling committee.
- Be able to read an **offer letter** and compute the **all-in TC**
  correctly.
- Have **scripts** for the 4 hardest moments of the negotiation
  (the ask, the counter, the "this is the best we can do," and
  the competing offer).
- Have a **spreadsheet** that lets you compare two offers across
  base, RSU, bonus, signing, perks, and 4-year vest.

## Suggested schedule

- **Day 1 (45 min):** Lesson 01 — read once, then re-read your own
  resume against the leveling rubric for your target level.
- **Day 2 (45 min):** Lesson 02 — read once, then re-derive the
  all-in TC for any offer you have on the table.
- **Day 3 (30 min):** Lesson 03 — read all 5 transcripts. Mark the
  lines you'd actually say out loud.
- **Day 4 (20 min):** Lesson 04 — open levels.fyi, pull 3 comps for
  your target role, fill out the 4-year TC worksheet.

Total: **~2 hours**, spread across one week, ideally the week an
offer is on the table.

## How to use this module

**Read in order, then read once more before any negotiation call.**

That sentence is the entire module in one line. The leveling lesson
anchors what you should be asking for. The offer-anatomy lesson
makes sure you understand every line of the offer letter. The
scripts lesson gives you the words to say. The benchmarking lesson
gives you the numbers to defend them.

If you only have 30 minutes before a recruiter call, read Lesson 03
in full, then skim Lesson 04's worksheet. You'll be better-prepared
than 95% of candidates.

If you have a week, do all 4 in order. Read once, then re-read the
scripts out loud. Practice the reframe sentences until they sound
like you, not like a script.

## What this module assumes

You have (or are about to have) a real offer. This module is
**post-offer** in the sense that it's most useful when there's
something to negotiate, but **pre-loop** in the sense that
leveling decisions are made before the loop ends. So:

- If you have no offer yet: read Lesson 01 now to make sure you're
  being interviewed at the right level.
- If you have an offer: read Lessons 02–04 *before* responding.
- If you have multiple offers: Lesson 04 is the most important.

You can read and write Markdown. The 4-year TC worksheet in
Lesson 04 is a copy-paste template — fill in the numbers.
