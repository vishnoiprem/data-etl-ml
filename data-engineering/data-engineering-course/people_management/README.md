# People Management Questions — A Practical Guide for Engineering Manager Interviews

> **21 lessons · 8 videos · ~10 hours of focused prep**
>
> **Author:** [Prem Vishnoi](https://medium.com/@premvishnoi) · <pvishnoi@avilx.com>
>
> **Companion articles:** [medium.com/@premvishnoi](https://medium.com/@premvishnoi)

A practical, prose-only guide to the round of the engineering manager
(EM) interview loop that most M5/M6 candidates underestimate: the
**people management** round. This is the round where senior engineers
get downleveled — not because they can't design a system, but because
they can't tell a clean 2-minute story about a hard personnel decision
they made last year.

System design tests how you think about systems. Coding tests how you
think about code. **People management tests how you think about
people, careers, performance, and yourself as a leader.** That's the
round this track is built for.

This track fixes that.

---

## What's in this track

**Prose-only.** There is no code, no service to run, no test to pass.
Every lesson is a Markdown file you read, think about, and — most
importantly — *do the exercises in*. The exercises are the part that
moves the needle. Reading alone doesn't.

**Pattern-based.** Every lesson is built around the 22 canonical
People Management questions and the 131 canonical Behavioral questions
in `docs/reference/em_interview_canonical_questions.md` (the spine
of this track). Once you see the pattern, you stop being surprised
by the question.

**Calibrated for senior+ EM.** The audience is engineers interviewing
for **EM (M5/M6) at Meta**, **Manager (L5/L6) at Google**, **Engineering
Manager at Stripe / Airbnb / Datadog / the big banks and AI labs**, and
**Senior Manager / Director at smaller companies**. Senior ICs
considering the IC→manager transition will also get value, but the
framing is "be seen as a senior EM" — see Module 01.

## How to use this track

**Week 1 — Overview (3-4 hours).** Read Module 01 in one sitting. Build
your EM story bank using Lesson 02 as the template. By Sunday you
should have 12-15 raw stories tagged to the 5 categories.

**Week 2 — Managing Individuals + Performance (6-8 hours).** Modules
02 and 03. For each lesson, read the worked example, write your own
answer in 90-120 seconds, then compare to the model. The "Try it"
sections are non-negotiable.

**Week 3 — Team Execution (5-6 hours).** Module 04. This is the
operational muscle — 1:1s, cadence, on-call, hiring. Read in order.
The hiring lesson in particular deserves 2 passes.

**Week 4 — Cross-functional (4-5 hours).** Module 05. The "soft power"
work — influencing without authority, working with PM, working across
timezones. End with the capstone in `exercise.md`: build your own PM
question bank and rehearse the answers out loud.

By week 4, you should be able to answer any of the 22 canonical
People Management questions with a clean 90-120 second story that
has a specific decision, a specific person, a measurable outcome, and
a clear "so what" about your management philosophy.

## A note on the prose-only format

There is intentionally **no code, no `app.py`, no `pytest`** in this
track. People management is a *craft* — like writing, like public
speaking, like sales. You can't run a unit test on whether your story
lands. You can only practice, get feedback, iterate.

So: **read the lesson. Do the "Try it" section out loud. Get a
friend to listen. Repeat.** That's the entire workflow.

## The 5 categories of EM questions (preview)

Before you read a single lesson, internalize this frame. Every EM
question you'll get in an interview falls into one of 5 categories:

1. **Managing down** — coaching, career, performance, delegation
2. **Managing up** — disagreement with manager, skip-levels, exec comms
3. **Managing sideways** — PM/Design, peer managers, cross-team
4. **Managing self** — mistakes, growth, why EM, IC→manager transition
5. **Crisis / ambiguity** — incidents, hard calls, layoffs, reorgs

Modules 02-04 are organized around #1, #3, and #5 (the bulk of the
work). Module 05 is the "soft power" work in #2 and #3. Module 01
introduces all 5 with worked examples. The story-bank in Lesson 02
is built so you have 2-3 stories per category, ready to deploy.
