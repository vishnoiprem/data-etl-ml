# Module 03 — Performance Management

> **4 lessons · 2 videos · ~2.5 hours**

The hard part of the EM job: running perf reviews, handling
underperformance (PIPs and beyond), retaining top performers, and
advocating for your team in calibrations. This is where the
People Management round gets its reputation — most senior ICs
haven't done any of this work, and the interview is the first
time they're forced to think about it.

The lessons in this module are organized around the **calendar
of an EM's year**: perf reviews in Q1, calibration in Q2,
promo cycles in Q3, retention conversations throughout. The
mistake new EMs make is treating each of these as a discrete
event. The senior move is to treat them as a continuous loop:
the 1:1s you ran in Q4 determine the perf review you write in
Q1, which determines the calibration argument you make in Q2,
which determines the promo packet you write in Q3.

## How to use this module

1. **Read Lesson 07 first.** The 4-boxes framework is the
   foundation for everything else — PIPs are an extension of
   "doesn't meet," retention is an extension of "exceeds,"
   calibrations are how the 4 boxes get distributed across the
   org.
2. **Lesson 08 is the heaviest.** Plan 2 passes. The first
   pass is the framework; the second is the legal/HR
   checklist. If you take only one lesson from this module,
   this is the one.
3. **Lesson 09 includes 3-5 retention frameworks** — this is
   the "extra important" lesson per the spec. Block extra time
   for the frameworks section.
4. **For each lesson, identify the stories in your EM story
   bank that fit.** The "Try it" sections ask you to write a
   90-second answer for a canonical question; do it with your
   own stories.
5. **Pair each lesson with reflection.** Most of these
   situations are uncomfortable to anticipate. The work of
   this module is making the discomfort familiar before
   you're in the actual conversation.
