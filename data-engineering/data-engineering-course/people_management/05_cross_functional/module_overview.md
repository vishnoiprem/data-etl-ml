# Module 05 — Cross-functional Collaboration

> **5 lessons · 1 video · ~2.5 hours**

The "soft power" work of the EM job: PM/Design, skip-levels,
influencing without authority, stakeholder communication, and
cross-timezone collaboration. The lessons in this module are
the ones senior ICs find hardest — most of the work is about
people and politics, not systems, and the EM's job is to
navigate it without becoming a blocker or a bottleneck.

## How to use this module

1. **Read Lesson 17 first.** The PM/Design partnership is the
   most-frequent cross-functional relationship, and it
   informs the others.
2. **Lesson 19 is the framework for the rest.** The
   influence-vs-power model is the substrate for all the
   cross-functional work. Read it before Lesson 20 (stakeholder
   comms) and Lesson 21 (timezones).
3. **Lessons 18 and 20 are paired.** Managing up (Lesson 18) is
   the substrate for upward reporting (Lesson 20). Read both
   before changing your relationship with your skip-level.
4. **Lesson 21 is the senior move.** Cross-timezone
   collaboration is increasingly the norm, and the patterns
   are different from co-located work. Read with a current
   distributed team in mind.
5. **For each lesson, identify the stories in your EM story
   bank that fit.** The "Try it" sections ask you to write a
   90-second answer for a canonical question; do it with your
   own stories.
