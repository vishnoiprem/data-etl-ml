# Module 01 — Overview

> **2 lessons · 1 video · ~1.5 hours**

The mental model for the entire People Management round. You'll learn
the 5 categories of EM questions, what interviewers in this round
are actually evaluating, and how to build the EM story bank you'll
reuse for the next 19 lessons.

This is the foundation. Read these two lessons first, in order, before
touching Modules 02-05. The story bank from Lesson 02 is the raw
material every later lesson assumes you have.

---

| # | Lesson | What you'll get out of it |
|---|---|---|
| [01](design/01_intro_to_pm.md) | An Introduction to People Management | The 5 categories of EM questions, what the rubric is, what the bar is, and why this round is different from senior-IC behavioral. |
| [02](design/02_em_story_bank.md) | Creating Your EM Story Bank | The end-to-end process for mining 12-15 EM-flavored stories from your career, with 5 worked examples from the canonical 245 questions. |

---

## How to use this module

1. **Read Lesson 01 first.** It frames the round. The 5 categories
   become the spine of the rest of the track.
2. **Lesson 02 is the highest-leverage lesson in the track.** Block
   3-4 hours for it. Mine your career. Write the stories down. Tell
   them out loud.
3. **Return to your story bank weekly.** Every lesson in Modules
   02-05 will reference "use a story from your EM story bank that
   fits this question." The bank is a living document — refine it
   as you go.
4. **Don't move to Module 02 until you have at least 8 stories
   written down.** Less than that and the rest of the track reads
   as theory. With 8+ stories, it becomes pattern-matching on your
   own career.
